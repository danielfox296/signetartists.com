Booking

From the first email to the last invoice.

Here's what happens at each step, who does it, and how long it takes. The contract, the certificate of insurance and the W-9 arrive in one email.

The steps

Timings assume a date six months out. A booking made three weeks before the event goes through the same steps in the same order, only faster. For a wedding, what couples report about their own timing is set out in how far ahead to book wedding musicians in Colorado.

StepWhenWhat happens
1. You write24 to 48 hrsAvailability and an exact number, itemised
2. You hold the dateThe day we agreeContract, insurance certificate, W-9 and invoice, one email
3. Your contact reaches youBefore the eventYour personal contact for the night, with a name and cell number
4. You send the run of showAny timeWe fill in the band’s rows and send it back
5. We agree the cuesThe week beforeOne person owns each program moment
6. The venue confirmsThe week beforePower, stage, load-in route, decibel cap
7. Load-in and sound checkThe dayThe space handed back to you, ready
8. The nightThe nightYour contact is reachable, load-in to load-out
9. The invoiceAfter the eventOne page, everything on its own line

Deposit, balance timing and payment methods are on the question list. Load-in and sound-check times by band size, and the six venue answers in step 6, are on what we need from your venue.

Three things we need from you

Six of those nine steps need nothing from you at all. Three do, and they're the three that stall a booking when they arrive late.

The date and the venue, to get a number
A city works if the venue isn't chosen yet. We can price a night without a signed venue and re-price it when you have one, because travel is its own line on the quote.
The venue name, to raise the certificate of insurance
The certificate names your venue as additional insured, so it can't be issued before the venue is chosen.
The run of show, whenever it exists
Send us the draft, even if it's nowhere near final. We'll fill in the band's rows against whatever you have and send it back, whether or not you've booked us.

If something changes

Guest counts move, programs get rewritten, a venue falls through. Tell us and we'll re-quote. Added performance time costs what the earlier hours did, a smaller build re-prices down, and a changed date is something we'll talk through with you.

If a musician can't make the date, you get the substitute's name and the reason as soon as we have them.

Send us the date and the venue.

That's enough for us to come back with an exact number.
