For Planners

The paperwork, before you ask for it.

Every vendor you shortlist carries your name into the room. Rather than describe how we handle that, here is the material itself: what the venue will ask for, what the night looks like hour by hour, and the answer to every question we have been asked so far.

What we need from your venue

Stage footprint, dedicated circuits, channel counts, load-in times and volume, at every size from duo to thirteen-piece. Written to be forwarded to the venue. Includes the six things worth confirming at contract.

Run of show templates

A gala, a wedding and a holiday party, timed to the minute, with the band’s rows filled in and a cue owner against every moment. Take them and change the times.

The full question list

Personnel and substitutions, deposits and payment terms, set and break lengths, microphones for speeches, decibel caps, who chooses the songs. Two dozen answers, none of them about price.

How a booking works

Nine steps from the first email to the last invoice, with what we need from you at each one and how long it takes. Three of the rows need anything from you at all.

The mountain corridor

Colorado’s statewide decibel caps, the 2025 Supreme Court ruling that can void a town sound permit, and what Aspen, Telluride, Breckenridge, Steamboat and Vail each enforce. Cited and dated.

The song book

The catalog and six curated sets, one for each shape a night takes. What gets played during arrival, through dinner, at the turn, on the floor and at last call.

Every rate is published on the pricing page, alongside a quote tool that itemises a night line by line while you change it.

Four things you can rely on

  • A named contact, given to you weeks before the event and reachable throughout it. A player, on the stand all night, whose number is in your phone weeks before load-in. Who that is, and how substitutions work
  • Certificate of insurance naming the venue, W-9 and one itemised invoice, in the same email as the contract. What else is included
  • A reply inside 24 to 48 hours, business hours, from first enquiry to final invoice.
  • The number you forward is the number your client signs. The rate card

Unbundling is normal

If you want your own ceremony musician, or a marquee DJ you already trust, and us only for the dance floor, that is a booking we want. Assemble the night your way and bring us in for the part where we fit. Where it makes sense to book us across more of it the reason is arithmetic rather than persuasion, and the arithmetic is on the pricing page where you can check it.

How long have you been around?

Signet opened this year. The musicians on its stages have spent ten and twenty years playing rooms like the ones you produce, and we are glad to put résumés in front of you. For a planner meeting us for the first time, everything on this page is built to make that first time easy, and to make it checkable rather than something you have to take on trust.

If you have a band you trust, keep them. We would like to be the call when their calendar fills.
