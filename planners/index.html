For Planners

A band you can put in front of your best client.

Every vendor you shortlist carries your name into the room. This page is how we treat that.

A named contact, given to you in advance

One person, named before the event and reachable throughout it, usually one of the players and most often the bandleader. Their number arrives with the contract, weeks before load-in, and it comes with every booking.

Paperwork before you ask for it

Certificate of insurance naming the venue, W-9 and the contract, sent together in one email. Your event file is complete months before anyone needs to open it.

A reply inside 24 to 48 hours

Business hours, every time, from first enquiry to final invoice, including the questions that are annoying to answer.

The number quoted is the number contracted

Rates are published. Travel, peak dates and added hours each get their own line, so the quote you forward is the total your client signs.

Unbundling is normal

If you want your own ceremony musician, or a marquee DJ you already trust, and us only for the dance floor, that is a booking we want. Assemble the night your way and bring us in for the part where we fit.

Where it helps to book us across the night, the reason is arithmetic. Once we are in the building the setup is paid for, so an hour of cocktail duo added to a full band booking is $450, and an hour of DJ after the band is $350. Booking three or more parts of the night together takes 10% off performance fees.

How long have you been around?

Signet opened this year. The musicians on its stages have spent ten and twenty years playing rooms like the ones you produce, and we are glad to put résumés in front of you. For a planner meeting us for the first time, everything here is built to make that first time easy: rates you can forward as they stand, paperwork that arrives with the contract, a named contact whose cell you have weeks out, and a straight answer to any question on this page.

If you have a band you trust, keep them. We would like to be the call when their calendar fills.
