Run of show

Three run of show templates, already timed.

Most run-of-show documents carry the band as one row that says band plays, and the detail gets filled in during the week of the event, by email, at speed. Here's that detail already worked out for the three kinds of night we see most. Take them, change the times, delete what doesn't apply.

How to read these

The cue column is the one worth settling before the day. Every moment in an evening belongs to somebody, and what usually goes wrong is two people each assuming the other was going to nod at the band, or nobody knowing who decides the speeches are running long.

Agree the cue column at sound check with the person holding the master document. Our bandleader takes their cues from that one person.

The row to watch on this night is 8:00pm. Remarks at a holiday party start late, run long, and land on a crowd that has been drinking since doors opened. Put a hard finish on them in the document, give it to whoever is speaking in advance, and put the band back up on time regardless.
