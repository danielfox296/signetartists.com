Pricing

What live music costs, and what moves the number.

This page is the market: what a band goes for in Colorado, how a quote is put together, and which parts of a night move the figure. Every number on it belongs to the market and carries the source it was read from, so you can set a budget before you write to anybody.

We do not publish our prices. A Saturday in December and a Tuesday in September are not the same job, and neither are four hours in a Denver ballroom and one hour past the passes. Tell us the date and what you are trying to solve, and the number that comes back is for that night and no other.

What moves a number

Up: a wedding rather than a private party. A Saturday in June, September or October. A holiday weekend, which this market prices 20 to 30 percent above a standard date. A venue with a hard load-in, a long carry or a late curfew. A run of show with ceremony, cocktails, dinner and dancing all needing cover. A drive over the passes, and past them a night's lodging.

Down: a weeknight. A short call. A single block rather than the whole evening. A smaller build for the same music. A venue the load-in is easy at.

Send the date, the venue and the guest count, and the number that comes back accounts for all of it. It does not move afterwards.

How a quote is put together

Most acts in this market bill two things. A call-out covers the evening committed regardless of length: the drive, load-in, setup, sound check, strike, load-out and the production that rides along with all of it. Then a rate for performance time. That is why one hour of cocktails is not a quarter of a four-hour reception, and why a quote that is only a headcount times an hourly figure usually has something missing from it.

Sound is the line worth checking hardest. With some acts it sits inside the figure. With others it arrives later, or the venue is expected to have a system that was specified for speeches. Ours is included at every size, which is a statement about scope rather than about price.

Travel and lodging are separate lines on any honest quote. So is a holiday weekend, which this market prices above a standard date. Folding those into a headline number makes a quote look cheaper than the evening is, and it makes two quotes impossible to compare.

Booking more than one part of the night

Once an act is in the building, the evening is already committed: the drive is made, the system is up, the sound check is done. So covering the ceremony or the cocktail hour alongside the main booking is a cheaper way to fill the night than hiring twice, and it is one contract, one load-in and one invoice.

How much of the night to cover is your call, and a smaller group early is a real answer as often as a bigger one later. If you would rather bring your own ceremony musician and use us for the dance floor, that is a normal booking and it should feel like one.

Tell us the shape of the evening when you send the date. The number that comes back is for that shape.

Common questions

What does a band cost for a corporate event in Denver?
In this market a cover or jazz band for a corporate event runs $2,500 to $8,000, and the four to six piece build the Denver market treats as its middle runs $4,000 to $6,000. Do you publish your prices?
No. What is published here is what live music costs in this market, each figure with the source it was read from, so a budget can be set before anyone writes to us. The number for your night comes back when you send the date, and it accounts for the size, the hours, the venue and the drive.
How is the price calculated?
Most acts in this market bill two things: a call-out that covers the evening they commit to whatever its length, and an hourly rate for performance time. That is why a one-hour cocktail set is not a quarter of a four-hour reception. Sound sits inside the figure with some acts and arrives as its own line with others, which is the first thing worth checking when two quotes look far apart.
Do you provide a certificate of insurance?
Yes, naming your venue as additional insured, sent in the same email as the contract. The limits are the pair Colorado venues write into their vendor requirements. A W-9 and one itemised invoice come with it.
Can one booking cover cocktails and dancing?
Yes. Once we are in the building the setup is already paid for, so a smaller group covering ceremony, cocktails or dinner alongside the main booking is a cheaper way to cover the night than hiring twice. One contract, one load-in, one invoice.
How far in advance should we book?
Corporate holiday parties are typically decided August through October for November and December dates, and New Year's Eve books earliest of all. Weddings and galas run longer: ten to twelve months, and twelve to eighteen for a mountain venue or a fall Saturday.
