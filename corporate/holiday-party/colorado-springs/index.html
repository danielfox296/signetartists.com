Colorado Springs holiday parties

In Colorado Springs, the holiday party goes through procurement.

The corporate directory here runs on defense contractors, firms clustered around the Air Force Academy and the bases, and national nonprofits headquartered in town. Those buyers bring vendor onboarding, purchase orders and net-30 approval to a band booking, so decisions land early and the vendor file is usually the slowest step between a shortlist and a signature. Every rate we would quote you is already published.

The venues are decided early too. The Broadmoor class of December calendar stacks company parties onto the same Fridays and Saturdays as the rest of the Front Range, and the ballrooms at Garden of the Gods Resort and Club and Cheyenne Mountain Resort fill the same way. We quote against a purchase order, invoice net-30 on approval, and send the certificate of insurance and W-9 in the same email as the contract.

The venues

From the Broadmoor's ballrooms to a table at the Mining Exchange.

The Springs season runs in tiers, and each tier books a different size of night.

The resort tier
The Broadmoor, Garden of the Gods Resort and Club, Cheyenne Mountain Resort. Ballroom calendars that hold the market's biggest company parties and stack them onto the same few peak December Saturdays. Quartet work once the floor opens, 88 to 95 dBA on the floor, with dinner and remarks held at conversation volume in front of it.
The downtown tier
The Mining Exchange and the Antlers. A function floor or a private dinner for the firm that wants the night walkable from the office. Duo and trio territory: 65 to 72 dBA through dinner, with a lift for the toasts.
The restaurant buyout
The whole company at tables, the music under the conversation for the entire night. A solo or duo, and the simplest version of a small company's December: one evening, one line on the invoice, no floor to fill.

The same three resorts anchor the retreat season, and corporate retreats get their own page.

The clock

Decided early, booked like a vendor.

The published window holds here as it does everywhere on the Front Range: the season is decided August through October, and New Year's Eve goes first. A Springs buyer adds a second clock to that one. Vendor onboarding, a PO number and a signature chain take however long your finance process takes, so the practical deadline sits weeks ahead of the calendar one. Start the vendor file when you shortlist the venue rather than after the date is chosen.

Logistics from our side are one line and one drive. Front Range travel is a flat published rate covering the run down from Denver, with no mileage arithmetic and no lodging line, and it buys the same night a Denver client gets: load-in of 45 to 90 minutes depending on size, sound sized to the venue, dock and unload settled with the venue before the day. For a ballroom night with remarks and an award in it, the run of show page shows how the music is built around a program.

The season's dates and their premiums sit on the holiday season page, and the Denver page carries the same dates alongside the downtown dock detail. Behind both is one booking calendar, ours, so a date checked once is checked for both cities.

Questions

Asked about Colorado Springs bookings.

Does a Colorado Springs booking cost more than a Denver one?
One flat travel line more, published on the rate card with everything else. Colorado Springs prices off the same column as Denver; the resort column is for the mountain corridor. Peak weekends and New Year's Eve carry the same published premiums they carry everywhere on the card.
Can you clear a defense contractor's vendor process?
Yes. We can be set up as a vendor, quote against a purchase order and invoice net-30 on approval, with a W-9 and a certificate of insurance naming your venue sent in the same email as the contract. Start onboarding when you shortlist dates; it is usually the slowest step in a corporate booking.
What size fits a Broadmoor-class ballroom party?
A quartet, once the floor has to fill. Dinner and remarks sit at 65 to 72 dBA, which a duo or trio holds comfortably; dancing runs 88 to 95 dBA, which is quartet work. One act can ladder across both halves of the night on a single contract.
Which dates does the season cover for Colorado Springs?
The same 21 published party dates as the whole Front Range: Thursdays through Saturdays across November and December, laid out with their peak premiums on our holiday season page. Whether one is free is a booking-calendar question, and it is our availability rather than any venue's, so check your venue's calendar in parallel and send us the date that clears both.
