The season itself

Four things December does to a party.

There are speeches, and they are the point. Somebody thanks the team, somebody announces the number, somebody hands out an award. A dedicated announcement microphone is live on its own channel from the end of sound check at every size we bring, so nobody borrows the singer's mic and nothing cuts out halfway through.

The venue is a restaurant or a hotel function space, not somewhere built for a band. That means a real load-in question, a real power question, and a ceiling on volume. All three get settled before the day rather than at 5pm on it.

Half the crowd does not want to dance and the other half is waiting. The night has to work at conversation volume for two hours and then turn. That is why the duo-plus-DJ pairing wins the season: standards under two hours of conversation, then the DJ turns the same sound system to the floor, and neither half is an afterthought.

Procurement is slower than you think. Vendor setup, a PO, net-30 on approval, a COI naming the venue. All of it is available and none of it is fast. Start in October and it is invisible. Start in December and it is the reason the booking falls over.

The venues

The December calendar, the way the hotels run it.

A Denver company party in December usually means a downtown hotel: the Brown Palace, the Four Seasons, the Oxford, and the class of venue around them that runs private events all season. Every company that waits until November is asking those calendars for the same two or three Saturdays, which is why the season is decided months before it starts.

Hotel function space comes with hotel logistics: a dock time, an elevator window, and a definite hour the ballroom becomes yours. Our load-in runs 45 to 90 minutes depending on size, needs one or two dedicated 20-amp circuits, and is coordinated with banquet staff before the day, so the venue's questions are answered in writing rather than at 5pm on the night.

The Denver page carries the downtown season in detail, and sixteen of the venues this market actually books, the Brown Palace to Mission Ballroom, are read one by one in the Denver venue guide. Colorado Springs runs a calendar of its own around the Broadmoor class of property, an hour south and priced with Front Range travel. Both sit under the corporate page, and what the whole Colorado market charges for a December band is set out in the cost guide. When do companies book holiday party entertainment?
August through October is when the season is decided for November and December dates. Established acts book out by Thanksgiving, and New Year's Eve books first of all. Past October you are choosing from cancellations rather than the calendar.
What does a holiday party band cost?
Every rate is published before you write: duo, trio, quartet and DJ each price as a range on the rate card, with sound included at every size. Holiday and peak weekends carry +25% and New Year's Eve +50%, published rather than discovered at contract. Travel beyond the Denver metro is its own line.
Can one booking cover dinner and dancing?
Yes, and it is the most common booking of the season: a duo through drinks and dinner, then a DJ carrying the back half, on one contract. Once we are in the building the call-out is already paid, so the added hours bill at the hourly rate only.
Can our procurement process clear a booking in time?
Yes, if it starts in October rather than December. We can be set up as a vendor, quote against a purchase order and take net-30 on approval, with a certificate of insurance naming your venue, a W-9 and one itemised invoice in the same email as the contract.
