Corporate retreats

Aspen corporate retreats, and what a far-resort date adds to a quote.

Aspen is the trip people talk about for years afterwards, and the evenings are most of the reason. A dinner in a private space that most guests will never get into again, a dance floor that fills late, and a night that feels like a reward rather than an agenda item.

Aspen sits about four hours from Denver when the roads cooperate, and further in winter, when Independence Pass closes and the drive runs the long way through Glenwood Springs. That makes an Aspen date a two-day trip for a band, and the quote shows what that adds, line by line.

The retreat itself books like any other: a welcome night, dinner at conversation volume, one evening with a dance floor. What’s different here is the travel and lodging around it, and we’d rather show those as their own lines than fold them into the fee. How we work every resort town starts on the corporate retreats page.

Where the evening activities are still being chosen, eight of them are laid out with the size each one needs in corporate retreat evening ideas in Vail, and they work for Aspen too, with the travel and lodging lines added.

details and costs

The lines on an Aspen quote.

There are three, and the second and third are what make an Aspen date different from a Denver one.

The fee for the music
The fee covers the band, sound support that’s the right size for the venue, the load-in, the sound check, the strike and a personal contact for the night. A duo through cocktails and dinner or a quartet when the dance floor is the point, whichever the evening needs.
Travel
Travel is its own line on the quote. Aspen is roughly four hours from Denver each way in good conditions, over passes that don’t always give you good conditions, and that’s most of a working day at both ends of the evening.
Lodging, at cost
A band that has played a late set isn’t driving four hours of mountain highway home that night. Lodging bills at cost, on its own line. That’s the difference between a far resort and the I-70 towns, which mostly don’t need it.
Why it’s itemised
A retreat budget goes through finance, and a quote that separates fee, travel and lodging is easier to forward, question and approve.

A holiday weekend prices above a standard date across this market. How a quote is put together is on the pricing page, and the Colorado cost guide sets the mountain towns against the wider market.

The venues

Aspen venues.

The Little Nell at the base of Aspen Mountain and the Hotel Jerome in the town's core anchor the dinner calendar, The St. Regis Aspen carries the ballroom end of it, and Aspen Meadows is the campus a working offsite actually runs on, sessions by day and dinner on the property at night. The Limelight holds the more casual end of the market, welcome nights and closing parties.

Venues at this level hold vendors to their standards: a certificate of insurance, load-in on the venue's schedule, and the cap and curfew honoured as written. The certificate comes with the contract.

Where the retreat wants one act across the whole evening, Tejas Singh can start as a duo and finish as a quartet on the same contract. Where it wants dinner music and no more, a jazz duo of upright bass and guitar holds the evening at a volume the table talks across.

Dinner at this class of venue books small.

The evenings here happen on dining floors and terraces built at human scale, and what the calendar asks for at dinner is a duo or a trio holding 65 to 72 dBA at the nearest table, the level a table of executives talks across without effort. A quartet is the booking when a dance floor has to fill.

The town's rules push in the same direction. Outdoor amplified sound at a private event in Aspen was capped at 80 dBA at the perimeter, between 9am and 8pm, when we checked, with neighbours within 250 feet owed seven days' notice. An 8pm outdoor cutoff moves the loud chapter of the evening indoors, which is where a small format does its best work. The figures, cited and dated, are on the mountain corridor page.

At 7,908 feet the altitude is gentler than Vail's and well under Breckenridge's, and we still plan a longer sound check for it. Footprints and power are on the technical page: a duo takes an 8 by 6 foot corner and one dedicated 20-amp circuit.

Private celebrations in Aspen.

Milestone birthdays, anniversaries and family gatherings book Aspen on the same three lines: the fee, the travel and the lodging at cost, on one contract. The occasions themselves are gathered on the private parties page.

If the drive is the deciding factor, the I-70 towns are a shorter day and mostly do without the lodging line, and Vail has its own page.

What does live music cost in Aspen?
There are three lines, itemised. The first is the fee for the music, with sound support that's the right size for the venue inside it. The second is travel, because Aspen is roughly four hours from Denver each way over passes. The third is a night's lodging at cost, because a band that played a late set isn't driving that road home. Why is lodging on an Aspen quote?
Because an Aspen date is a two-day trip for the band. The drive runs about four hours in good conditions and longer in winter, and a band that finishes late isn't driving mountain highways home that night. Lodging bills at cost, on its own line.
How long is the drive from Denver, really?
About four hours when the roads cooperate. Independence Pass closes seasonally, so winter dates run the long way through Glenwood Springs, and weather can stretch any of it. We build that margin into the load-in day.
