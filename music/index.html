The roster

Six bookings, and the price of every one.

Two kinds of thing are on this page, and the difference is worth thirty seconds because it changes what you are buying.

Named acts. Tejas Singh and Dirty Flamenco. Each is published under the name of the person who fronts it, so you can go and look them up rather than take an adjective for it. Any change of personnel is named to you in advance.

Formats. Jazz Duo, Funk Trio, DJ, Blues Rock. These are sold by specification: the instrumentation, the repertoire and the volume, at a published price. We field them from more than one working group, which is exactly why they hold a Saturday in October when a single named band would already be gone. What we will not do is put a name on one and quietly send someone else.

Signet opened this year. The acts and the players on it did not. Nothing on this page is larger than a quartet, which is the range a private event actually uses; five and six piece is quoted on request rather than sold off the card. Three of these have a full page with configurations, a sample of the set and a footage slot. Everything carries the same published floor and the same contract. For the songs themselves, see the song book.

Every act, every size

What the price on the card already covers.

The number next to an act is not a musician fee with production quoted later. It is the booking. Two acts of the same size cost the same because the thing being delivered is the same.

  • A named contact for the night, given to you before the event
  • Sound sized to the venue
  • Load-in, setup, strike and load-out
  • Certificate of insurance naming your venue
  • W-9 and one itemised invoice
  • Timeline coordination before the event
  • Continuous audio during breaks

Stage lighting and a dedicated engineer are optional add-ons, quoted as their own line. Travel outside the metro, holiday weekends and New Year's Eve are quoted the same way, and all three are published on the pricing page. Nothing else appears on an invoice that was not on the quote.
