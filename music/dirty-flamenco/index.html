Flamenco

Dirty Flamenco

Fronted by Gary Meyers. Traditional and covers.

The only flamenco act on this roster, and close to the only one in Colorado sold as a private event product rather than a concert booking. Real palos, played by a group with a standing book. Book it when the room needs one thing guests photograph.

Footage slot

Flamenco is the one booking on this roster that nobody else in Colorado sells as a private event product. It exists here as a concert act, or as a guitarist playing Spanish-flavored background. Dirty Flamenco is neither. It is a working group with a standing book, sold at private event scale.

That matters for a specific kind of night. Most live music at a private event is designed to be felt and not watched. This is the opposite booking. It is the act you hire when the program has a moment in it that needs to be the moment: a cultural centerpiece, a corporate dinner that has to be memorable rather than pleasant, a reception that wants something guests will describe to people who were not there.

The book is palos, the traditional forms, not a set of pop songs with nylon strings on them. That is the difference between a flamenco booking and a Spanish guitar booking, and it is audible inside eight bars.
