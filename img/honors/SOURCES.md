# Award and press marks

The marks shown beside artists' awards, press and stages (an act's `honors` in
content/acts). Each was taken from its owner's own site on 2026-11-10 and set
in one colour (#e2e2e2) on a transparent ground: raster marks by script from
their alpha or brightness, SVG marks by setting every fill. No shape was
redrawn.
