Wedding cocktail hour music in Denver.

Where the hour happens

Hotel mezzanines, gallery floors, garden terraces.

The cocktail hour rarely gets the main floor, because the main floor is being set for dinner. It gets the lobby, the mezzanine, the gallery, the terrace. A duo is built for exactly those: an 8 by 6 foot footprint, one power circuit, and a volume that suits marble as well as open air.

The grand hotels
Downtown weddings keep the hour a floor from dinner, in the lobby lounge or on the mezzanine. The Brown Palace runs it under the atrium lobby, and at the Four Seasons and The Oxford it lands in spaces where a banquet team is already working a tight service window. A duo that is set and sound-checked before guests arrive fits the way these buildings run.
The galleries
Space Gallery on Santa Fe Drive is the pattern: concrete floors, white walls, art on every one of them. Hard surfaces carry sound further than carpet and drapery do, so the duo starts the hour lower than it would in a ballroom and lets the walls do the projecting.
The gardens
Denver Botanic Gardens puts the hour outdoors, and outdoors has rules of its own: a covered, level and dry performance area, power run to it, and a move-indoors call written before the day. Denver evenings drop fast after sunset, and instruments and electronics both stop behaving in the cold.
The mansions and clubs
Grant-Humphreys Mansion and the University Club of Denver spread the crowd across parlors and landings rather than one open floor. The duo sets up within sight of the bar, where the crowd pools, and the far parlors stay usable for conversation.
The industrial floors
Mile High Station holds ceremony, cocktails and dinner under one roof, which makes it the venue flip in its purest form: the hour runs at one end of the building while the floor turns at the other, and the duo is what keeps the turn from reading as a delay.

Nearly every Denver cocktail hour lands in one of these five shapes, which is why the venue name is the first thing we ask for. Send it with the date and the answer is a number for that hour in that building, off the published card.

Load-in, parking and power downtown

A duo is one van and one trip. Load-in runs 60 minutes, strike runs 45, and both go through the service entrance where the venue has one, with dock windows and freight elevators settled with the banquet office before the day. Downtown, the parking question gets answered at contract rather than at 4pm on the day: a hotel dock has a window, a gallery has a curb, and either way the van is gone before guests arrive.

Where ceremony and reception share a building, we set up and sound-check before guests are seated, so no cases move through a landing full of people holding drinks. The duo plays the hour from its own corner while the main floor turns from ceremony rows to dinner tables.

Power for a duo is one dedicated 20-amp circuit, and dedicated is the word that matters: sharing with catering warmers is the most common cause of trouble at load-in. In a mansion or a gallery we ask the circuit question at contract. Denver questions

The general question set, what plays, how long, duo or DJ, is on the cocktail hour page. The same duo plays private parties across the city on the same card.

Where do Denver wedding cocktail hours usually happen?
In the pause between ceremony and reception: hotel lobbies and mezzanines downtown, gallery floors in the Santa Fe arts district, garden terraces at the Botanic Gardens, and the parlors of Capitol Hill mansions. A duo fits all of them; the footprint is eight feet by six.
What does load-in look like at a downtown Denver venue?
Small and early. A duo loads in within 60 minutes through the service entrance, plays on an 8 by 6 foot footprint, and runs on one dedicated 20-amp circuit. We settle dock windows and freight elevators with the venue before the day, so no cases cross the lobby.
Is there a travel charge for a Denver wedding?
No. Denver is the home market, so a metro date carries no travel line. Travel appears on the quote only outside the metro: Boulder and Colorado Springs add a flat figure, and mountain venues add their own line, all published on the rate card.
Can the duo play an outdoor cocktail hour at a Denver garden venue?
Yes, on a covered, level and dry performance area with power run to it. Denver evenings drop fast after sunset, so the weather call and the move-indoors time are written before the day. Garden terraces with a pavilion make this the easiest outdoor booking on the calendar.
