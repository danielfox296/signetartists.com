What the figure is made of.

With most acts in this market, the figure has two parts. A call-out covers the evening committed, whatever its length: the drive, load-in, setup, sound check, strike and production. A rate for performance time covers the music. The split is why a one-hour cocktail set is never a quarter of a four-hour reception.

Overtime is the next thing to look for on a quote. This market charges {{market:denver_overtime}} for time beyond the contracted set, which is worth knowing before the night. Added time with us costs what the earlier hours did.

Then there are the lines that sit outside the fee: travel beyond the metro, a night's lodging when the drive home crosses the passes after a late strike, and a holiday weekend, which this market prices above a standard date. Each of those belongs on its own line, because folding them in makes a quote look cheaper than the evening is.
