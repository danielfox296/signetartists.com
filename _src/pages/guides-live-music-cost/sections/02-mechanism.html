What the Price covers

With most acts in this market, the figure has two parts. A call-out covers the whole event commitment, whatever its length: the drive, load-in, setup, sound check, strike and production. In addition to that, a rate for performance time covers the music. That split is why a 1-hour cocktail set costs more than 1/4th of a 4 hour reception.

Overtime is the next thing to look for on a quote. This market charges {{market:denver_overtime}} for time beyond the contracted set, which is worth knowing before the night. Added time with us costs what the earlier hours did.

Beyond that, you may have travel beyond the Denver metro area, lodging for the talent if it's outside our metro service area, and a bit more for holiday weekends.
