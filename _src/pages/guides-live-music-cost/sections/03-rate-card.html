The market

What live music goes for, figure by figure.

Published numbers from cost guides, a wedding-budget dataset and one booking marketplace, each read on the date under the table. They describe the market. They are not our prices and we do not publish ours.

A figure only reads right next to what it covers, which is why the spread is this wide. The marketplace average and a full band with horns are two different purchases. What sits inside the number varies as much as the number does: sound in particular is inside some quotes and a later conversation with others, and a certificate of insurance naming the venue is standard with some acts and an unwelcome surprise with others.

Resort dates sit above metro dates everywhere in this market for the same two reasons: the drive, and the night in a bed at the far end of it. Altitude, town noise rules, curfews and the rest of the corridor's working detail are on the mountain corridor page, and corporate retreats is where the resort booking is set out in full.
