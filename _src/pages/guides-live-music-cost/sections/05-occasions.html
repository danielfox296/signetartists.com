What each kind of night comes to.

Four bookings cover most of the enquiries we see. Each is priced below from the published card, and each occasion's own page carries the fuller picture.

The corporate holiday party
A quartet covers dinner at conversation volume, then fills the floor: {{rate_4h:Quartet}} over four hours. The season's other winning shape pairs a duo through cocktails and dinner, {{rate_range:duo}}, with the DJ carrying the late half on the same contract, and the added hours bill at the hourly rate with no second call-out. The season is decided August through October, and the season's dates and premiums are laid out on the holiday party page.
The client dinner
The conversation is the event, so the music sits under it: 65 to 72 dBA at the nearest table, a level guests talk across. An hour of jazz duo is {{rate_hour:duo}}, and the whole front of the night, arrival through dessert, is {{rate_range:duo}}. The format and the paperwork are on the client dinners page.
The private dinner party
A solo player holds a dinner for twelve or a garden party for sixty at {{rate_range:solo}}, and a duo adds an upright bass and a fuller sound at {{rate_range:duo}} for the evening. The formats, and what fits a home against what fits a club, are on the private parties page.
The wedding cocktail hour
One hour between ceremony and reception. An hour of jazz duo is {{rate_hour:duo}}, and a solo runs {{rate_range:solo}}. What should play, and how the hour is timed against the ceremony, is on the cocktail hour page.
