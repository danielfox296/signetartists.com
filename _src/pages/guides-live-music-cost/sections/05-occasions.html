What each kind of night comes to.

Four bookings cover most of the enquiries we see. Each one is a different shape, and the market prices them differently.

The corporate holiday party
A cover or jazz band for a corporate event runs {{market:us_corporate_band}} nationally, and December is the month this market charges above a standard date for: {{market:denver_holiday_surcharge}} on a holiday weekend in Denver. The season is decided August through October, and the shapes a holiday party actually takes are on the holiday party page.
The client dinner
The conversation is the event, so the music sits under it: 65 to 72 dBA at the nearest table, a level guests talk across. A jazz trio or acoustic duo for a dinner runs {{market:us_dinner_band}} in this market, the spread being how long the music plays and how much production comes with it. The format and the paperwork are on the client dinners page.
The private party
A cover or acoustic band for a private party runs {{market:us_private_party_band}}, and a smaller build for the same music sits well under that: an acoustic duo over a four-hour reception runs {{market:us_duo}} and a trio {{market:us_trio}}. What fits a home against what fits a club is on the private parties page.
The wedding cocktail hour
One hour between ceremony and reception, and the smallest real booking on this list. A classical duo or trio for a ceremony or cocktail hour runs {{market:denver_strings}} in Denver. What should play, and how the hour is timed against the ceremony, is on the cocktail hour page.

The corporate shapes, holiday parties, client dinners and retreats in the resort corridor, sit together on the corporate page.
