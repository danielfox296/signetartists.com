Market context

Why the figures spread this far.

Live music in Colorado is priced in tiers, and each tier buys a different thing. Reading a number without knowing which tier it came from is how two quotes end up looking like the same purchase.

The marketplace tier
One online booking marketplace shows that the average cost for a Denver party band is {{market:denver_marketplace_avg}}. But the listings show prices that range from {{market:denver_marketplace_range}}. You're seeing a price that buys the act, but what else is included (sound, insurance, travel, production) varies listing by listing. Two quotes for the same raw material, can cover two very different evenings.

The production tier
