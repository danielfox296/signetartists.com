Market context

Where these figures sit in the Colorado market.

Live music here prices in three tiers. Each buys a different thing, and a figure only reads right next to what it covers.

The marketplace tier
Commodity averages run roughly $1,600–$2,100 for a band on a standard date. The figure buys the act. What else sits inside it, sound, insurance, travel, production, varies listing by listing, so two quotes at the same number can cover two different evenings.
The boutique tier
Full production starts at $8,000 and up: show direction, lighting, staffing and a program designed around the act. For a gala carrying a production budget, it is the right purchase.
