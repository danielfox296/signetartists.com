Market context

Why the figures spread this far.

Live music here prices in tiers, and each one buys a different thing. Reading a number without knowing which tier it came from is how two quotes end up looking like the same purchase.

What else sits inside it, sound, insurance, travel, production, varies listing by listing, so two quotes at the same number can cover two different evenings.

The production tier
A seven to nine piece show band runs {{market:us_showband}}, and full production adds show direction, lighting, staffing and a program designed around the act. For a gala carrying a production budget, it's the right purchase.
