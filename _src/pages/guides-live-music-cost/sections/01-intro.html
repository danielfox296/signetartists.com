What live music costs in Colorado.

Colorado couples budget {{market:co_band_budget}} for a live band, and the Denver market runs {{market:denver_band_range}}, a trio at the bottom of that and a full band with horns at the top. Those are the market's published figures. This page is what they cover, why they spread that far, and what changes a price inside the range.

We don't publish our own prices, because the date, the size, the hours and the drive all change them. What's here is the market you're shopping in, sourced line by line, so you can set a budget before you write to anybody.
