What live music costs in Colorado.

Colorado couples budget {{market:co_band_budget}} for a live band, and the Denver market runs {{market:denver_band_range}}, a trio at the bottom of that and a full band with horns at the top. Those are published figures from the market, not ours. This page is what they cover, why they spread that far, and what moves a number inside the range.

We do not publish our own prices, because the date, the size, the hours and the drive all move them and a single figure would be wrong for most of the nights it was read on. What is published here is the market you are shopping in, sourced line by line, so a budget can be set before anybody writes an email. Send us a date and you get our number for that night.
