The evenings

Where the after-party comes from.

Conferences and incentive programs
The closing night of a customer conference, the hospitality evening a sponsor hosts, the last night of an incentive trip. The corporate page covers the contract and the vendor onboarding, and the retreats page covers the mountain version.
Galas and award nights
The floor after the last award. The galas and award nights page sets out every cue in the program that comes before it.
Holiday parties
The back half of the office party, once the speeches and the raffle are done. The holiday party page dates the season and covers the January party.
Weddings and private parties
The late set after the first dance and the toasts, or the birthday that keeps going. Weddings and private parties carry the shapes and the venues.
