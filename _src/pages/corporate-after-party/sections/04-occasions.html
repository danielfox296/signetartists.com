Which nights end this way

The occasions that book an after-party.

Conferences and incentive programs
The closing night of a customer conference, the hospitality evening a sponsor hosts, the last night of an incentive trip, booked like the rest of a company event, in Denver or on a mountain retreat.
Galas and award nights
The dance floor after the last award.
Holiday parties
The back half of the office party, once the speeches and the raffle are done.
Weddings and private parties
The late set after the first dance and the toasts at a wedding, or the birthday that keeps going at a private party.
