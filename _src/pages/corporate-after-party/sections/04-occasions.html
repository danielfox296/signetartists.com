Which nights end this way

The occasions that book an after-party.

Conferences and incentive programs
The closing night of a customer conference, the hospitality evening a sponsor hosts, the last night of an incentive trip. The corporate page covers the contract and the vendor onboarding, and the retreats page covers the mountain version.
Galas and award nights
The dance floor after the last award. The galas and award nights page sets out every cue in the program that comes before it.
Holiday parties
The back half of the office party, once the speeches and the raffle are done. The holiday party page lists the dates of the season and covers the January party.
Weddings and private parties
The late set after the first dance and the toasts, or the birthday that keeps going. Weddings and private parties each have their own page, with the configurations and the venues.
