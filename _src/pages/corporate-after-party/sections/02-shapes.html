The shapes

Four ways the hour after the program can go.

Each one runs through the same sound system as the dinner music, on the same contract, so the floor opens the moment the program closes.

The DJ, with a live player over the set
An open-format DJ reading a floor of people from six cities, with a saxophone or a bass player on the floor over the set. It is the most common single booking of the corporate season, and the live player is the difference between a playlist and a crowd watching something happen.
Live band karaoke
A real band plays the 80s and 90s hits on its own and hands the mic to anyone who wants a song, with the words on a screen and a host on the floor. Nobody has to sing for it to work. For a conference crowd that spent the day in a ballroom, it is the after-party they describe to the colleagues who skipped it.
The soul and R&B band
Stevie Wonder, Aretha Franklin, Al Green and the Motown book, sung out front with a horn on the choruses. The ballroom floor act for a gala’s second half or a holiday party once the awards are done.
The yacht rock band
Doobie Brothers, Steely Dan, Hall and Oates and Toto in three-part harmony. For the crowd that wants to sing every chorus rather than dance every song, and for a summer after-party on a terrace.

A band into the DJ through the same system is the shape for a night that will outlast the band. The DJ page sets out how that handoff works.
