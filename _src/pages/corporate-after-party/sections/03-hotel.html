What the hotel needs to know

An after-party in a hotel ballroom or a hospitality suite is a set of hotel questions before it is a music question: a dock time, an elevator window, the circuits, the decibel cap and the curfew. All of them are settled with the events office before the day. Our load-in runs 45 to 90 minutes depending on the size of the act and needs one or two dedicated 20-amp circuits, and the system goes up before the doors open so the floor can turn on the emcee’s last word.

A hotel’s house system is specified for speeches and will not carry a dance floor across a ballroom, which is why sound sized to the venue comes with every booking here. The cap and the curfew are honoured as written; give us both numbers at contract and the set is built under them rather than turned down on the night.

Footprints, power and load-in for every size are on the technical page, written to be forwarded to a hotel as it stands. The paperwork, a certificate of insurance naming the property, the W-9 and one itemised invoice, arrives in the same email as the contract.
