What the hotel needs to know

An after-party in a hotel ballroom or a hospitality suite comes with a few hotel questions: a dock time, an elevator window, the circuits, the decibel cap and the curfew. We settle all of them with the events office before the day. Our load-in usually runs 45 to 90 minutes depending on the size of the act and needs one or two dedicated 20-amp circuits, and the system goes up before the doors open so the dance floor can open on the emcee’s last word.

A hotel’s house system is specified for speeches and won’t carry a dance floor across a ballroom, which is why sound support that’s the right size for the venue comes with every booking. The cap and the curfew are honoured as written; give us both numbers when we sign the contract and we’ll build the set under them rather than turning it down on the night.

Footprints, power and load-in for every size are on the technical page, written so you can forward it to the hotel as it stands. A certificate of insurance, the W-9 and one itemised invoice come with the contract.
