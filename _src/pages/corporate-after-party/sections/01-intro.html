After-parties

The after-party is the part of the night people talk about.

The conference after-party, the hour after a gala’s last award, the back half of a holiday party once the speeches are done. It’s the part that comes up on the flight home. A DJ with a live musician over the set, a band that lets the crowd sing, or a soul band on a ballroom floor, on the sound system that’s already up.

Denver hosts corporate conferences of several thousand people at a time, and the hotels beside the convention center run the after-parties for them and for the city’s largest galas. This page is written for the destination management company, the conference hospitality lead and the gala committee who book that hour: what it can be, what the hotel needs to know, and how it sits on one contract with the rest of the evening.
