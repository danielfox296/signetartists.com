After-parties

The after-party is the part of the night people talk about.

The emcee says good night, the DJ brings the first song in under the applause, and the floor is full before the house lights come up. The hour after the program, at a conference, a gala or a holiday party, is the part people talk about on the flight home, and it can be a DJ with a live musician over the set, a band that lets the crowd sing, or a soul band on the ballroom floor, all on the sound system that’s already up.

Denver hosts conferences of several thousand people at a time, and the hotels beside the convention center run their after-parties and the city’s largest galas. The after-party goes on one contract with the rest of the evening.
