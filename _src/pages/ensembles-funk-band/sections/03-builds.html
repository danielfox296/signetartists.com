Configurations

Two configurations, the trio and the quartet.

The trio
Guitar, bass and drums. This is the whole idea at its smallest, and it’s the size for a venue where the floor is close to the tables: a club dining space, a restaurant buyout, a backyard with a patio. It sets up in 12 by 8 feet on one dedicated 20-amp circuit and loads in inside an hour.
The quartet
The trio plus a horn, or keys, for a ballroom or a crowd past two hundred, where three musicians start to feel thin across a wide floor. A horn takes the melodies the records gave it; keys fill the middle under a guitar solo. It takes 14 by 10 feet, two dedicated circuits and a 90-minute load-in.

Either configuration plays dinner at 65 to 72 dBA at the nearest table before the night turns. On a patio it plays on a covered, level and dry performance area with power run to it, and we agree a move-indoors plan with you ahead of time.

Footprints and power by size are on the technical page. When the night should keep going after the band, the DJ takes the same sound system on the same contract.
