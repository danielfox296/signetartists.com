Sizes

A trio, or a quartet with a horn or keys.

The trio
Guitar, bass and drums. It’s the size for a floor that sits close to the tables: a club dining space, a restaurant buyout, a backyard with a patio.
The quartet
The trio plus a horn or keys, for a ballroom or a crowd past two hundred, where three musicians start to sound thin across a wide floor. A horn plays the lines the records gave it, and keys fill in under the guitar solos.

A jazz duo or a solo guitarist can play the drinks hour before the band on the same contract, and the DJ can keep the party going afterward through the same sound system.
