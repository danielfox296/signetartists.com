Booking it

The whole evening, or the set after dinner.

The band books for the full night or for the floor after dinner, and it pairs with a jazz duo or a solo guitarist through drinks on the same contract.

Sound support that’s the right size for the venue is included, and with an instrumental band that’s most of what decides whether the floor fills: the bass has to be felt at the back tables without the guitar being loud at the front ones. If the night runs long, another half hour is easy to add on the night.

Send the date, the venue and the headcount, and we’ll come back with a recommendation on configuration. What live music costs in this market.
