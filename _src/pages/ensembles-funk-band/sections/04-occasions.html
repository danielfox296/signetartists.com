Milestone birthdays and private parties
The fortieth or fiftieth where the furniture ends up against the wall. Private parties covers the sizes and the venues, and the trio is the size most of those parties book, because it fills a floor without a stage.
The hour after a company dinner
Once the remarks are done and the floor is open. The after-party page sets out how that hour is booked on the sound system that’s already up, and the corporate page covers the contract and the paperwork.
Summer picnics and patios
A lawn or a rooftop in July, where an instrumental band can play all afternoon without anyone needing to face a stage. Company picnic entertainment ideas in Denver puts it beside the other formats a summer party can take.
The last night of a retreat
The one the group retells on the flight home, when the whole company is in one place. Corporate retreats covers the mountain venues, the travel line and the altitude.
