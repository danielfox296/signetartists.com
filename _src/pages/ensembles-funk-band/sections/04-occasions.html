Occasions

Who books a funk band.

Milestone birthdays and private parties
A fortieth or a fiftieth at a club, a restaurant or the house. The trio fills a floor without a stage, which is why it’s the size most of these parties book.
Company after-parties
The hour after a company dinner or a conference program, played on the sound system that’s already set up for the evening. It books like the rest of a company event, on one contract with the paperwork your office needs.
Summer picnics and patios
A lawn or a rooftop in July. Instrumental funk works from anywhere on the lawn, so people can dance near the band or keep eating at the far tables.
The last night of a retreat
The one night the whole company is in one place with the agenda finished, at a mountain venue or back in Denver.
