Funk band

A funk band for parties, after-parties and patios.

Instrumental funk and soul covers for a party where the dance floor is close to the tables. It books as a trio of guitar, bass and drums, or as a quartet with a horn or keys when the floor wants more, and nobody has to wait for a chorus to start moving.

Think a fiftieth in a private club where the tables get pushed back after dessert, the hour after a company dinner when the program is over, a summer picnic on a lawn, or the last night of a retreat when the whole group is finally in one place. It plays dinner at conversation volume first, then turns up.
