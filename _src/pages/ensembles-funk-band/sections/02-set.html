The set

A sample of the set.

James Brown, The Meters, Stevie Wonder, Herbie Hancock, Sly and the Family Stone, Prince, Earth, Wind and Fire, and the funk side of the Motown catalogue, played without a singer. What your guests recognise is the groove, and a groove doesn’t need a chorus to be over before the next one starts.

A set is built around the flow of your evening. Through dinner the band plays the slower, warmer end of the material at a level the table talks across, and once the floor is open the tempos come up and the songs run into each other, because there’s no verse to wait for. When you want the same songwriters sung out front, the soul and R&B band is the sung version of this booking. The wider covers list is searchable on the repertoire page, and the first learned song is included in every booking.
