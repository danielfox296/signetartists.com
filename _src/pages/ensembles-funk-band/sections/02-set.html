The set

James Brown to Prince, played as instrumentals.

James Brown, the Meters, Stevie Wonder, Herbie Hancock, Sly and the Family Stone, Prince, Earth, Wind & Fire and the funk side of Motown. Your guests know these records by the bass line and the horn parts, and that’s what the band plays.

While people eat, the band plays the slower, warmer end of the book, and the tempos come up once the floor opens. When you want the same songwriters sung, the soul and R&B band is this band with a singer out front. The rest of the covers list is on the repertoire page.
