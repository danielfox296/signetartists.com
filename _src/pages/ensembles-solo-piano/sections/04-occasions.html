Occasions

Common occasions for a pianist.

Client dinners and member events
Twenty at one table or eighty at ten, where the conversation is the event and the music sits under it all night. The client dinners page covers the volume, the paperwork and the private dining spaces that book this.
Galas and award nights
The doors, while guests find their tables, and dinner at conversation volume before the program. The galas page walks through the evening, and gala entertainment ideas has the small dinner where the piano is the whole evening.
The low-key company dinner
The holiday party that’s a dinner rather than a floor, or the January reset. The holiday party page carries the season, and January office party ideas puts the piano dinner beside the other formats.
Brunch, memorials and senior living
A Sunday jazz brunch at a club or a hotel, the prelude at a memorial service where the chapel has a piano, a standing afternoon at a senior living community. The brunch, memorial and senior living pages each have the booking in their own terms.
Dinner parties at home
A house with a piano in it, and a table of twelve. Private parties covers the sizes and what a home needs, which for a pianist is a piano or a corner and one outlet.
