Occasions

Who books a pianist.

Client dinners and member events
Twenty at one table or eighty at ten, in a private dining space or a club, where the conversation is the event.
Galas and award nights
The doors and the dinner, before the program. Gala entertainment ideas has the smaller gala where the piano is the whole evening.
The low-key company dinner
A holiday party that’s a dinner rather than a dance floor, or the January office party after the December rush.
Sunday brunch
A jazz brunch at a club, a hotel or a restaurant, with standards under the whole service.
Memorial services
The prelude as people arrive, and the songs the family chooses, when the chapel or the reception has a piano.
Senior living
A standing afternoon at a community, with the residents’ own songs from the Great American Songbook.
Dinner parties at home
A house with a piano in it, and a table of twelve.
