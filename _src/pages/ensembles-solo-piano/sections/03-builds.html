The booking

One musician, the piano, and a voice when you want one.

The piano
When the venue has a piano, the pianist plays it, and a hotel lobby or a club dining space usually has one. When it doesn’t, a stage piano comes with the booking, and from the tables it sounds like a piano in the corner.
With a voice
On its own the piano is instrumental, which is the version most client dinners and galas book. When the evening wants singing, a voice joins for a set or for the night, on the same line of the contract.

For a bigger crowd, a jazz duo plays the same hours. When the night wants a dance floor after dinner, a band or the DJ takes over through the same sound system on the same contract. The rest of the covers list is on the repertoire page.
