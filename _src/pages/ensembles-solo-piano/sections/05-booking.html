Booking it

An hour of cocktails, or arrival through dessert.

The pianist books for a single hour or for the whole front of the evening, and pairs with a jazz duo for a bigger crowd or a band for the floor afterward on the same contract.

Sound support that’s the right size for the venue is included, which for a piano means a stage piano and a small system when the venue has neither, and nothing at all when it has a grand in the lobby. A dedicated announcement microphone is ready for the toast or the remarks. If the dinner runs long, another half hour is easy to add on the night.

Send the date, the venue and the headcount, and we’ll come back with a recommendation. What live music costs in this market.
