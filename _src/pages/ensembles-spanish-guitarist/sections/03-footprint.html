The footprint

A corner, a circuit, and often no amplifier at all.

One Spanish guitarist takes 6 by 4 feet, one dedicated 20-amp circuit when the system is used, a 45-minute load-in and a 30-minute strike. Nylon strings carry a prelude and a processional in most indoor spaces with nothing plugged in.

Outdoors, and in any space where the officiant needs to be heard at the back, a small system on the same stand carries the guitar and an announcement microphone together. That is the whole rig, and it is why this booking goes where a band cannot: a courtyard, the end of a long table, the landing of a staircase, a gallery evening set between the works, a house where nobody wants the furniture moved.

Footprints, inputs and circuits for every size are published on the technical page, written so you can hand your venue the link.
