Spanish guitarist

A Spanish guitarist for weddings, dinners and events.

Nylon strings, a rumba under the first drink, a Malagueña as the guests sit down, one hand keeping time on the body of the guitar. A Spanish guitarist is a small booking that guests talk about afterwards, and it does its work without a stage, a microphone in anyone's hand or a program to run around it.

It plays a wedding ceremony and the hour after it, a dinner party for twenty, a milestone birthday, a corporate dinner that wants one thing worth remembering, and the proposal or the toast that only needs an hour. When the night wants full flamenco, with bass, cajón and a set piece the crowd stops to watch, that's Dirty Flamenco, the trio this booking is the solo version of.
