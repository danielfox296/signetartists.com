Nylon strings, solo

A Spanish guitarist for weddings, dinners and events.

A rumba under the first drink, a Malagueña as your guests sit down, and one hand keeping time on the body of the guitar. A Spanish guitarist is one musician on nylon strings, playing from a corner of a courtyard, the end of a long table or the landing of a staircase.

It books for a wedding ceremony and the hour after it, a dinner party for twenty, a milestone birthday, a corporate dinner, and a proposal or a toast that only needs an hour. When the night wants full flamenco, with bass, cajón and a set piece the crowd stops to watch, that’s Dirty Flamenco, the trio this booking is the solo version of.
