The set

The Spanish pieces, and the familiar songs.

A Malagueña, rumbas, a farruca, tangos, Entre dos Aguas: the pieces that sound like Spain even to guests who couldn’t name them. Around those sit familiar songs played on nylon strings in the same style, which is how Can’t Help Falling in Love ends up in a processional and a Beatles song ends up under dessert.

It’s instrumental, and the guitarist watches the guests, so a prelude stretches while people find their seats and a processional ends when the wedding party reaches the front. The set is built with you before the date, and one learned song of your choosing comes with every booking.

For one musician on steel strings working blues, roots, jazz and the singer-songwriter catalog, book the solo guitarist. The wider covers list is on the repertoire page.
