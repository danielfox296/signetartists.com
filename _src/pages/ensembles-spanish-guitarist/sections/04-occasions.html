Occasions

Common occasions for a Spanish guitarist.

Spanish guitar fits the moments that want one instrument and no fuss around it.

The wedding ceremony
Prelude, processional, an interlude if the ceremony has a still moment in it, and the recessional, on nylon strings. Spanish guitar is what a lot of couples are picturing when they say they want something warmer than strings. How the four parts are ordered and how long each runs is on the ceremony page.
The cocktail hour
An hour of rumbas and familiar songs on a terrace, at a level two guests who have just met can talk across. The cocktail hour page covers the hour itself.
The dinner party, and the milestone birthday
Twenty around a long table, a courtyard, a back garden with the lights strung: Spanish guitar under the whole meal is the booking this instrument fits best. Private parties and dinners covers the night, and the Denver page covers the city's venues and parking.
The corporate dinner
Dinner at conversation volume with one instrument that makes the evening feel hosted, for a client dinner or a member event that wants one thing guests mention afterwards. The client dinners page covers the corporate details.
The single hour
Think a proposal on a terrace, the toast at a sixtieth, or a surprise at the end of an anniversary dinner. It's one musician for one hour, with no stage, on the same contract as a full night.

What live music costs across Colorado, size by size, is in the cost guide and on the pricing page.
