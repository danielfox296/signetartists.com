Occasions

Who books a Spanish guitarist.

The wedding ceremony
Prelude, processional, an interlude if the ceremony has a still moment in it, and the recessional, on nylon strings. It’s what a lot of couples are picturing when they want something warmer than a string quartet.
The cocktail hour
An hour of rumbas and familiar songs on a terrace, at a level two guests who have just met can talk across.
The dinner party, and the milestone birthday
Twenty around a long table, a courtyard or a backyard with the lights strung, with Spanish guitar under the whole meal, in Denver or up in the mountains.
The corporate dinner
A client dinner or a member event that wants one instrument to make the evening feel hosted.
The single hour
A proposal on a terrace, the toast at a sixtieth, or a surprise at the end of an anniversary dinner: one musician for one hour, on the same contract as a full night.
Memorial services
A service or a graveside on nylon strings, with the pieces the family chooses.
