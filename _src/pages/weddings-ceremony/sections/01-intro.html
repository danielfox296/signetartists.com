The ceremony

Acoustic guitar for a wedding ceremony.

The processional is the only music at a wedding that everybody hears. Nobody is talking, nobody is eating, and every guest is turned toward the aisle waiting for one person to appear in it. A guitarist watches that aisle instead of a clock, keeps the piece going while the walk takes as long as it takes, and lands the last chord where it belongs.

That's the case for hiring an acoustic guitarist for your ceremony. Spanish guitar is the same booking with nylon strings and a different repertoire, and Spanish guitar wedding ceremony music is what a lot of couples are picturing when they say they want something warmer than strings. Both are on this page, and both come with an announcement microphone for the officiant.
