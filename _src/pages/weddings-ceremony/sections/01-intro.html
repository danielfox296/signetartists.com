The ceremony

Acoustic guitar for a wedding ceremony.

The processional is the only music at a wedding that everybody hears. Nobody is talking, nobody is eating, and every guest is turned toward the aisle waiting for one person to appear in it. A guitarist watches that aisle instead of a clock, holds the piece while the walk takes as long as it takes, and lands the last chord where it belongs.

That is the case for hiring an acoustic guitarist for a wedding rather than pressing play. It is a live player's whole advantage and it lasts about ninety seconds. Spanish guitar is the same booking with nylon strings and a different repertoire, and Spanish guitar wedding ceremony music is what a lot of couples are picturing when they say they want something warmer than strings. Both are on this page, and both carry an announcement microphone for the officiant.
