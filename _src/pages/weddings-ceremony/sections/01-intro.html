The ceremony

Acoustic guitar for a wedding ceremony.

The processional is the one piece of music at a wedding every guest listens to: the talking has stopped, every head is turned toward the aisle, and they’re all waiting for one person to appear in it. A guitarist watches that aisle instead of a clock, keeps the piece going while the walk takes as long as it takes, and lands the last chord on the arrival.

An acoustic guitarist plays it on steel strings, and a Spanish guitarist plays it on nylon, which is what a lot of couples are picturing when they want something warmer than a string quartet.
