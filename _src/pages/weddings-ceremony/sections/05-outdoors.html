Outdoor ceremonies in Colorado

Most ceremonies here happen outside, and outdoors takes three things at any size: a covered, level and dry performance area, power run to it, and a move-indoors call written before the day. Open air gives nothing back, so a guitar that fills a chapel reaches about the fourth row on a lawn. That's what the small system is for: it carries the same conversational level to the back rows.

Two more things only matter outdoors. Wind moves a microphone stand and puts noise straight into the officiant's microphone, so the ceremony spot is chosen with the wind in mind as much as the view. And Colorado evenings drop fast after sunset: instruments go out of tune in falling temperatures and electronics stop behaving, which is why a late-afternoon mountain ceremony gets the weather call written into the contract.
