Outdoors in Colorado, which is most of them

Most ceremonies here happen outside, and outdoors takes three things at any size: a covered, level and dry performance area, power run to it, and a move-indoors call written before the day rather than decided in the moment. Open air gives nothing back, so a guitar that fills a chapel reaches about the fourth row on a lawn. That is what the small system is for, and it is set to carry the same conversational level to the back rows rather than to make anything loud.

Two more things that only bite outdoors. Wind moves a microphone stand and puts noise straight into a vocal channel, so the ceremony site wants a position out of the prevailing line rather than the prettiest spot on the lawn. And Colorado evenings drop fast after sunset: instruments go out of tune in falling temperatures and electronics stop behaving, which is why a late-afternoon mountain ceremony gets the weather call written into the contract.
