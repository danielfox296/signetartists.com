The venues

The venues, and what each one asks of the music.

The village hotels hold the classic Vail wedding: a ceremony on a lawn or a deck with the mountain behind it, the cocktail hour on the terrace, and the reception in the ballroom once the cold comes down. The Sonnenalp and the Four Seasons Resort Vail anchor that day in Vail Village, The Arrabelle at Vail Square and The Hythe carry it in Lionshead, and the Grand Hyatt Vail runs the same day at the west end of town along the creek. Their ballrooms take a quartet or a full band, their terraces take a duo, and a ceremony guitarist reaches any of their outdoor sites with one small case.

Donovan Pavilion is the town’s own venue on Gore Creek, and the one to hold when a couple wants a single floor with their own vendors and their own timeline. The ceremony and the reception happen in one building, so a duo through the hour and a band for the floor share one load-in, and the creek outside is the backdrop for the ceremony music.

A ceremony up the mountain, reached by gondola, is a different plan: the guitarist and a small system ride up with the guests, the music is battery powered or runs on the venue’s own circuit, and the reception happens back in the village afterward. Tell us the site and we plan the ride.
