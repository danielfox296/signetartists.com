Vail weddings

Live music for a Vail wedding.

A Vail wedding happens at 8,150 feet, and the day is shaped by it. The ceremony is outdoors while the light is on the mountain, the cocktail hour is on a terrace that cools the moment the sun drops behind the ridge, and the floor fills a ballroom in a village that measures its sound at the property line. So the music is a guitarist for the vows, a duo for the hour after them, and a band or a DJ for the dancing.

It’s one of our weddings bookings: one part of the day or all of it, on one contract with a personal contact through the day and one travel line for the drive up I-70.
