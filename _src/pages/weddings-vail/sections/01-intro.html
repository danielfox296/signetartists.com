Vail weddings

Live music for a Vail wedding.

A Vail wedding happens at 8,150 feet, and the day is shaped by it. The ceremony is outdoors while the light is on the mountain, the cocktail hour is on a terrace that cools the moment the sun drops behind the ridge, and the floor fills a ballroom in a village that measures its sound at the property line. A guitarist for the vows, a duo for the hour after them, a band or a DJ for the dancing, each one planned for the altitude and the cap.

This is the Vail version of our weddings page. Book one part of the day or all of it on one contract, with sound included at every size, a personal contact through the day, and one travel line for the drive up I-70.
