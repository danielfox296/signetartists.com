Ideas

Birthday party entertainment ideas for adults.

Live band karaoke for the 40th
The band plays the hits and hands the microphone to the friends who have been threatening to sing all night. Live band karaoke fits a club or a big main floor.
The one song, learned for the night
A singer and guitarist learns the song the guest of honor has loved for thirty years and plays it once, at the moment you choose.
A flamenco set piece under a long dinner
A table of twelve and a guitar in the corner with real technique in it, with Dirty Flamenco stepping forward for one set piece.
A sing-along the whole family can join
A 70th or an 80th usually has four generations in it, and the Beatles are the songbook all four know. Your Bird Can Sing gets the whole family singing.
