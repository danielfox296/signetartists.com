Ideas

Birthday party entertainment ideas for adults.

The ideas that fit a night built around one person, each with the page that covers it.

A band for the floor after dinner
The soul and R&B band for a crowd that grew up on Stevie Wonder and Aretha, the funk band for one that wants a groove, and the yacht rock band for the fiftieth of anyone who owned those records.
Live band karaoke for the 40th
The band plays the hits and hands the microphone to the friends who have been threatening to sing all night. The live band karaoke page covers the size it needs and how the sign-up works.
The one song, learned for the night
A singer with a guitar who learns the song the guest of honour has loved for thirty years and plays it once, at the moment you choose. The proposals and surprise songs page covers how one song gets placed, and Tejas Singh and a singer and guitarist both do it.
A Spanish guitarist under a long dinner
A table of twelve, a long dinner and a guitar in the corner with real technique in it. Dirty Flamenco plays it as a trio with a set piece in it, and a single Spanish guitarist covers the table alone.
A sing-along the whole family can join
A 70th or an 80th usually has four generations in it, and the Beatles catalogue is the songbook all four already know. Your Bird Can Sing plays it as a duo and hands out booklets with the words in them.
A jazz duo that goes silent for the toasts
Upright bass and guitar at a volume the table talks across, silent for the speeches and back the moment the glass goes down. The jazz duo and trio page covers it.
The DJ for the late end
When the band is done and the 30th is not, the DJ takes the same sound system on the same contract.
