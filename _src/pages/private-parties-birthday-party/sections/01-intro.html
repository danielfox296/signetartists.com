Birthday party

Live music for a milestone birthday party.

A milestone birthday is a whole night built around one person, and the size follows the number on the cake: a trio that fills a floor at a 30th, a jazz duo under the dinner and the toasts at a 50th.

It’s one of our private parties bookings, at home, at a club or in a private dining space, and the anniversary party is the couple’s version of the same night.
