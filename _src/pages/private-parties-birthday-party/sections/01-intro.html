Birthday party

Live music for a milestone birthday party.

Most birthdays get a restaurant. The round ones get a whole night built around one person: the friends who would fly in for a wedding fly in for this, somebody stands up with a glass, and at some point the song the guest of honour has loved since college gets played by a band that learned it that week. The size that suits the night changes with the number on the cake, from a trio that fills a floor at a 30th to a jazz duo under the dinner and the toasts at a 50th, on one contract with one personal contact in charge of the music.

A milestone birthday is part of our private parties bookings. It comes with sound support that’s the right size for a house, a club or a private dining space, a certificate of insurance and a W-9 with the quote, and an announcement microphone for the toasts. The anniversary party page covers the couple’s version of the same night.
