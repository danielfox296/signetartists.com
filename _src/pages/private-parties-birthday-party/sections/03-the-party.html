The party

The surprise, the toast, and the set built for one person.

The surprise entrance
The guest of honor thinks it’s dinner with three friends, the private dining space is full of everyone they’ve known since school, and the band is already playing, so the first thing they hear as the door opens is their song. It runs on the same cue as a surprise proposal song.
The toast
Somebody stands up with a glass, and the music stops before they reach the microphone. When the toasts are a program, three speakers and a slideshow, we plan them with you on the run of show.
The set built for the person
Tell us the name, the number and a few of the songs, and the set is built from the years the guest of honor cares about: the college years for a 40th, the wedding years for a 60th. The whole song list is on the repertoire page.
The 50th that dances anyway
Some 50ths sit down and some don’t, and a band that plays dinner quietly and then turns up covers both. The yacht rock band and the soul and R&B band both do that on one contract.
