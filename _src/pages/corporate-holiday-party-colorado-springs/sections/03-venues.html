The venues

From the Broadmoor's ballrooms to a table at the Mining Exchange.

The Springs season runs in tiers, and each tier books a different size of night.

The resort tier
The Broadmoor, Garden of the Gods Resort and Club, Cheyenne Mountain Resort. Ballroom calendars that hold the market's biggest company parties and stack them onto the same few peak December Saturdays. Quartet work once the floor opens, 88 to 95 dBA on the floor, with dinner and remarks held at conversation volume in front of it.
The downtown tier
The Mining Exchange and the Antlers. A function floor or a private dinner for the firm that wants the night walkable from the office. Duo and trio territory: 65 to 72 dBA through dinner, with a lift for the toasts.
The restaurant buyout
The whole company at tables, the music under the conversation for the entire night. A solo or duo, and the simplest version of a small company's December: one evening, one line on the invoice, no floor to fill.

The same three resorts anchor the retreat season, and corporate retreats get their own page.
