Colorado Springs holiday parties

Corporate holiday parties in Colorado Springs, November and December.

A resort ballroom with the whole company in it, dinner at a volume people can talk across, and a dance floor that fills once the thank-yous are done. That’s the Colorado Springs company party, from a duo through dinner to a quartet on the floor, on one contract.

The venues are decided early. The Broadmoor’s December calendar stacks company parties onto the same Fridays and Saturdays as the rest of the Front Range, and the ballrooms at Garden of the Gods Resort and Club and Cheyenne Mountain Resort fill the same way.
