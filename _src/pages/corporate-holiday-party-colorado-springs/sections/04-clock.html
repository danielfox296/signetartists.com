The clock

Decided early, booked like a vendor.

The published window holds here as it does everywhere on the Front Range: the season is decided August through October, and New Year's Eve goes first. A Springs buyer adds a second clock to that one. Vendor onboarding, a PO number and a signature chain take however long your finance process takes, so the practical deadline sits weeks ahead of the calendar one. Start the vendor file when you shortlist the venue rather than after the date is chosen.

Logistics from our side are one line and one drive. Front Range travel is one flat line covering the run down from Denver, with no mileage arithmetic and no lodging line, and it buys the same night a Denver client gets: load-in of 45 to 90 minutes depending on size, sound sized to the venue, dock and unload settled with the venue before the day. For a ballroom night with remarks and an award in it, the run of show page shows how the music is built around a program.

The season's dates and their premiums sit on the holiday season page, and the Denver page carries the same dates alongside the downtown dock detail. Behind both is one booking calendar, ours, so a date checked once is checked for both cities.
