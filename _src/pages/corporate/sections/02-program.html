Working around a program

A corporate evening runs on a program: remarks from leadership, an award, an auction, a video, a window where people need to hear each other talk. Dinner and networking sit at conversation volume, and the room gets loud later, when loud is the point.

The music stops while your CEO is still three steps from the microphone. Transitions are rehearsed against the run of show the week before, and whoever holds it has our bandleader’s cell.

The same discipline covers awards dinners, auctions, remarks and a video: music up to the moment, silence through it, music again when the applause starts. If the program wants a louder chapter, live-band karaoke adds $1,500. Your people sing, the band plays behind them.
