We Work around your event's program

Every great corporate evening runs on great programming. You've got remarks from leadership, maybe an an award or auction, a window of time where people need to hear each other talk. Dinner and networking are served with live music at conversation volume, and the night can get loud later, when loud is exactly the point.

The music stops while your CEO is still three steps from the microphone. Transitions are planned with the run of show.

We work awards dinners, auctions, remarks and a video the same way: music up to the moment, silence through it, music again when the applause starts. If the program wants a louder chapter, live-band karaoke is its own line on the quote. Your people sing, the band plays behind them.

Galas and fundraisers book this way too, at the same sizes. A reception set through arrival, dinner music around the program, and the auction, the paddle raise and the thank-yous treated as cues the band works around. What a gala needs from the music is precision against its program, and solo through quartet covers it.
