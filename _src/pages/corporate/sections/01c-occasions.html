The occasions

Three corporate occasions, each with its own page.

The shapes above put a starting figure on the budget. Each occasion also carries a full page of its own: the dates, the venues that market books, and the questions that come with it.

Office holiday parties
The season page, with every Thursday, Friday and Saturday of November and December dated and the peak premiums tagged. The season is decided August through October, so start there early.
Client dinners and member events
Dinners where the conversation is the event: client dinners, member and association evenings, private clubs, firms and showrooms. The jazz duo leads that page, priced by the hour, with the dinner volume figures printed next to the price.
Corporate retreats
Retreats and incentive events in the mountain corridor, Vail through Aspen. A resort date carries the drive and, past the passes, a night’s lodging, both itemised. Altitude, power and load-in are settled before anyone drives.

What each size costs, and where those figures sit in the wider Colorado market, is set out in the cost guide. The card itself is on the pricing page.
