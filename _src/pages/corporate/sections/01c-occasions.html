Common types of corporate occasions

Five formats for your corporate event

Office holiday parties
Booking from late November into January, the holiday party is something special for your employees and associates.
Galas and award nights
An evening built around your programming (likely including remarks, an auction, videos and walks to the stage). We work together on cues so the music flows seamlessly with the night.
Client dinners and member events
Dinners where the conversation is the event. Many times we recommend a jazz duo playing low enough to add a sense of class to the ambience without demanding attention away from developing relationships.
Corporate retreats
Retreats and incentive trips in the mountain corridor, Vail through Aspen, and the Colorado foothills. We create the evening to meet the expectations of a group that flew in for it.
After-parties
You know your people better than we do - and some people like to keep going after the first party ends. The hour after a conference dinner, a gala program or the holiday party’s speeches, with a DJ alone (or a DJ plus a live musician over top), a band that lets the crowd sing, or live band karaoke.
