Playing the mountain corridor

Mountain venues run on their own rules. Decibel limits and curfews are honoured as written, and where there’s a cap we want the number written into the contract: a band that knows the limit in advance builds a different night under it, choosing arrangements and instrumentation that stay inside it instead of just turning down.

We coordinate load-in with the venue before the day, down to dock times, elevator windows, dedicated circuits and where the cases sit during dinner. All of it is written down by band size, so you can hand the venue a link rather than relay questions between us.

Travel is its own line on the quote, at the rates on the pricing page. The caps, the curfews, the altitude and what Colorado’s statewide noise law does to a town sound permit are all set out on the mountain corridor page, with each one cited and dated.
