Playing the mountain corridor

Mountain venues run on their own rules. Decibel limits and curfews are honoured as written, and load-in is coordinated with the venue in advance: dock times, elevator windows, power, where the cases sit during dinner.

Travel is its own line on the quote. Vail, Beaver Creek, Breckenridge and Winter Park add $600. Aspen, Telluride, Steamboat and Crested Butte add $600 plus lodging at cost. And the named contact whose number arrives with the contract is reachable from load-in to load-out.
