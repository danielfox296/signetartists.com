Playing the mountain corridor

Mountain venues run on their own rules, and where there’s a sound cap we want the number in the contract: a band that knows the limit in advance chooses arrangements and instrumentation that stay inside it, which sounds better than a band turning down. Every town’s cap, cited and dated, is on the mountain page.

We settle setup with the venue before the day, from dock times to where the cases sit during dinner, and all of it is written down by band size.

Travel is its own line on the quote, and so is a night’s lodging past the passes. The retreats page covers the resort towns one by one.
