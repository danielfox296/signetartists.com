The acts

A reception act, a dinner act and a dance floor act, on one contract.

They can be the same musicians all night or three different sounds. The contract, the load-in and the sound system don’t change.

As the doors open
The jazz duo on upright bass and guitar, a solo pianist, a Spanish guitarist, or Last Consulate for an evening that wants to sound like this decade.
Through dinner
The jazz trio with drums played with brushes, or the Americana trio when the gala is a ranch, a barn or a mountain evening.
After the last award
The soul and R&B band on a ballroom floor, the yacht rock band for a crowd that wants to sing, live band karaoke when the committee wants the after-party to be the thing people describe, or the DJ on its own or with a live musician over the set.

Ideas for the whole evening, music and otherwise, are in gala entertainment ideas. The hour after the program has its own page.
