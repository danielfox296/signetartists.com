Galas and award nights

Music that works around your program.

The emcee steps toward the podium and the music has already stopped, and the last honoree reaches the stage on the final bar of their walk-up. A gala runs on a program, and the music’s job is to know where every cue is: a reception act as the doors open, dinner music the tables talk across, silence for the emcee, and a dance floor after the last award.

Nonprofit galas and fundraisers, association award nights, chamber annual dinners and company award ceremonies book it, and the cues below are written so you can forward them to whoever signs.
