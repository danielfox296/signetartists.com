Galas and award nights

Music that works around your program.

A gala or an award night runs on a program: remarks, an auction, a paddle raise, a video, a walk to the stage. The music’s job is to know exactly where those are and to be doing the right thing on either side of each one. A reception act as the doors open, dinner music the tables talk across, silence the second the emcee steps up, and a dance floor after the last award if the night wants one.

This is the page for nonprofit galas and fundraisers, association award nights, chamber annual dinners and company award ceremonies. Below is what the music does at one, cue by cue, so a committee can forward it to the board member who signs.
