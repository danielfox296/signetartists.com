Where these evenings happen

Denver’s galas concentrate in a recognisable set of calendars: the ballrooms of the downtown hotels and the ones beside the convention center, the Gaylord out by the airport, the museums and gardens that rent their halls for a night, the performing arts complex, and the mountain resorts for the summer galas in Vail and Aspen. Each comes with its own dock time, elevator window, power and sound cap, and each gets settled with the venue’s events office before the day.

Footprints, power and load-in for every size are published on the technical page, written to be forwarded to a venue as it stands. The paperwork a nonprofit’s finance office asks for, the W-9, the certificate of insurance naming the venue and one itemised invoice, arrives in the same email as the contract, and the corporate version of that path is on the client dinners page.

When the award night belongs to a company rather than a cause, the corporate page covers the vendor onboarding and purchase order side. When it is the last night of a retreat, the retreats page prices the mountain evening.
