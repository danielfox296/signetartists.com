Where these evenings happen

Denver’s galas usually happen in a recognisable set of places: the ballrooms of the downtown hotels and the ones beside the convention center, the Gaylord out by the airport, the museums and gardens that rent their halls for a night, the performing arts complex, and the mountain resorts for the summer galas in Vail and Aspen. Each comes with its own dock time, elevator window, power and sound cap, and we settle each with the venue’s events office before the day.

Footprints, power and load-in for every size are published on the technical page, written so you can forward it to a venue as it stands. The W-9, the certificate of insurance and one itemised invoice come with the contract, and the corporate finance side is on the client dinners page.

When the award night belongs to a company rather than a cause, the corporate page covers the vendor onboarding and purchase order side. When it’s the last night of a retreat, the retreats page covers the mountain evening.
