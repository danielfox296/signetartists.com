Today, we think of a signet as the mark an artist leaves behind.

Every great artist has one: a sound, a feeling, a presence that is unmistakably their own. And every great event deserves entertainment that leaves a mark on the people experiencing it.

Signet Artists brings distinctive artists to distinctive moments.

The roster is chosen by Daniel Fox, a Denver guitarist who still plays the dates himself, which is why it is chosen the way a player would choose it: for the sound, and for how each act carries a night.

The artists are on the roster, with what each one plays and where they have played it.
