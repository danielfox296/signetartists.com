The book

Three books, and the balance is yours.

A December dinner runs for hours, longer than the carols last, so the trio draws on three books and moves between them through the evening.

The Guaraldi record
Christmas Time Is Here, Skating, Linus and Lucy, O Tannenbaum the way Guaraldi’s trio played it. It’s the one Christmas record almost everybody in a ballroom grew up on, and it was written for a piano trio at conversation volume, which is why it sits so well under dinner.
The standards that are Christmas songs
White Christmas, The Christmas Song, Let It Snow, Have Yourself a Merry Little Christmas, Winter Wonderland, I’ll Be Home for Christmas. They were written by the same songwriters as the rest of the standards book, and the trio plays them the same way.
The standards that aren’t
Autumn Leaves, All of Me, The Girl from Ipanema and the rest of the book the trio plays in any month, threaded through the season’s songs so the evening feels like December without being a medley of it. The whole list is on the repertoire page.

Tell us how much season you want in it. Some parties want carols wall to wall, some want one Christmas set placed after dinner, and most want the balance the trio reads off the crowd.
