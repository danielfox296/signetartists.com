Christmas jazz trio

A Christmas jazz trio for holiday parties and December dinners.

The Charlie Brown record, the standards that are Christmas songs, and the standards that aren’t, played on guitar, upright bass and drums with brushes at a volume the table talks across. It’s the sound of a December evening that feels like the season without playing the same twelve carols on a loop.

Think the dinner half of a company holiday party, a client dinner in the second week of December, a member evening at a club, or a party at home with a tree in the corner. It books for a cocktail hour, for drinks and dinner, or for the whole evening with a quartet or the DJ carrying the floor afterward, and the dates of the season are on the holiday party page.
