The Charlie Brown record and the standards

A Christmas jazz trio for holiday parties and December dinners.

Christmas Time Is Here on guitar, upright bass and brushed drums, while the coats come off and the first glasses of wine go around. The Christmas jazz trio plays the Charlie Brown record, the standards that are Christmas songs and the standards that aren’t, at a volume the table talks across, so a long dinner sounds like December from the first course to dessert.

It books for the dinner half of a company holiday party, a client dinner in the second week of December, a member evening at a club, and a party at home with a tree in the corner.
