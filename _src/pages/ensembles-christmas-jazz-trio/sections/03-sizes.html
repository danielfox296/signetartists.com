Configurations

One guitar at a client dinner, a quartet on the floor.

Solo
One jazz guitarist playing the Christmas book at a dinner for twenty or a party at home. It’s the smallest booking here, and it sets up in 6 by 4 feet on one circuit.
Duo
Upright bass and guitar, for a cocktail hour or a longer dinner where the conversation is the point. It sets up in an 8 by 6 foot corner and never drifts louder than the table.
Trio
The duo plus drums played with brushes, for a company party in a ballroom. It sets up in 12 by 8 feet on one dedicated circuit and takes about an hour to load in. Changes Jazz Trio plays this size under its own three names.
Quartet, and the floor
The trio plus a singer or a horn for the hour after dinner, when the swing side of the book opens a floor. When the night should run past that, the DJ takes the same sound system on the same contract.

Footprints and power by size are on the technical page. The same trio in any other month is the jazz duo and trio, and the corporate evening slot by slot is on the corporate jazz band page.
