Sizes

One guitar at a client dinner, a quartet on the floor.

Solo
One jazz guitarist playing the Christmas book at a dinner for twenty or a party at home. It’s the smallest booking here.
Duo
Upright bass and guitar, for a cocktail hour or a longer dinner where the conversation is the point. It fits in a corner and stays under the table talk.
Trio
The duo plus drums played with brushes, for a company party in a ballroom. Changes Jazz Trio plays this size under its own three names.
Quartet, and the floor
The trio plus a singer or a horn, for a party that wants the swing side of the book on a dance floor. When the night should run past that, the DJ takes the same sound system on the same contract.

The same trio in any other month is the jazz duo and trio, and a company evening part by part is on the corporate jazz band page.
