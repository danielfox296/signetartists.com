Before you send

What we need to consider an act.

The form asks for all of it.

Live video
A clip of you playing to a crowd. A phone recording from the back of the venue is fine, and it’s the first thing we open.
A song list
Enough songs for a three-hour event, most of them songs the crowd already knows. Paste it in the form or link it.
Your own PA
Sound sized for the venues you play, from a dinner party to a courtyard, and the hands to run it. Tell us in the form what you carry.
Liability insurance, or the will to get it
Venues ask for a certificate before the date. Insurance Canopy writes performer policies by the year, and MusicPro covers the gear.
How you dress
Clothes that fit a wedding or a corporate dinner. Business casual at least, and the client tells us when they want more.
The set format
Sets of 45 to 60 minutes with 15 to 20 minutes between them, and music playing through the break.
A quick answer on a date
When we write to ask about a date, a reply the same day keeps the booking moving.
