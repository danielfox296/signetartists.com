Three things we need from you

Six of those nine steps need nothing from you at all. Three do, and they're the three that stall a booking when they arrive late.

The date and the venue, to get a number
A city works if the venue isn't chosen yet. We can price a night without a signed venue and re-price it when you have one, because travel is its own line on the quote.
The venue name, to raise the certificate of insurance
The certificate names your venue as additional insured, so it can't be issued before the venue is chosen.
The run of show, whenever it exists
Send us the draft, even if it's nowhere near final. We'll fill in the band's rows against whatever you have and send it back, whether or not you've booked us.

If something changes

Guest counts move, programs get rewritten, a venue falls through. Tell us and we'll re-quote. Added performance time costs what the earlier hours did, a smaller build re-prices down, and a changed date is a conversation.

If a musician can't make the date, you get the substitute's name and the reason as soon as we have them.
