Three things, and the rest is ours

Six of those nine steps need nothing from you at all. Three do, and they are the three that stall a booking when they arrive late.

The date and the venue, to get a number
A city works if the venue is not chosen. We can price a night without a signed venue and re-price it when you have one, because travel is its own line rather than something folded into the headline figure.
The venue name, to raise the certificate of insurance
The certificate names your venue as additional insured, so it cannot be issued before the venue exists. This is the single most common reason a complete event file sits at 90% for a month.
The run of show, whenever it exists
Send the draft, not the final. We fill in the band's rows against whatever you have and send it back, and we do that whether or not you have booked us.

If something changes

Guest counts move, programs get rewritten, a venue falls through. Tell us and we re-quote. Added performance time bills at the same hourly figure in 30-minute increments with no overtime rate, a smaller band re-prices down the published ladder, and a changed date is a conversation rather than a form.

If a player cannot make the date, you get the substitute's name and the reason as soon as we have them. Discovering a substitution at load-in is the version of this that goes wrong, and advance notice is the part we can actually keep.
