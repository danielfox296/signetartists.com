The sound policy

A number on every venue, and a cool evening.

Boulder has its own rules on evening sound, and every venue in it has a policy on top, so the first question we ask about a Boulder date is the number. Whatever your venue, HOA or rental agreement puts on evening sound, and whatever cut-off it sets, both go into the contract. A ceremony guitarist and a cocktail hour duo sit inside any of those numbers on their own, and the dance floor goes indoors, which is why a trio is the size most Boulder venues allow for it.

The evening cools fast once the sun is behind the Flatirons, so an outdoor cocktail hour is timed to the light, and the ceremony gets a covered, level and dry spot with power run to it in case a summer afternoon turns.
