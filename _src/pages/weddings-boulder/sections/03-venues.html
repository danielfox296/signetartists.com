The venues

Chautauqua, the downtown hotels, and the backyards between them.

Chautauqua holds the Boulder wedding people picture: the ceremony on the lawn with the Flatirons behind it, the cocktail hour on the porch, the dinner in the Dining Hall or the floor in the Community House. It also sits inside a residential historic district, so its sound policy is the first document to ask for, and the sizes that book there are a guitarist for the ceremony, a duo for the hour and a duo or a trio inside for the rest.

The Hotel Boulderado and the St Julien carry the downtown wedding, with the ceremony and the reception a corridor apart and sleeping guests on the floors above, which is why their event floors have a number on evening sound. Both ballrooms take a quartet or the DJ. Flagstaff House holds the dinner that is booked for its view, up a mountain road with the city below it, and a duo on its terrace is the size that suits the terrace. Rembrandt Yard is the downtown gallery a block off the mall, one floor that takes a ceremony, a dinner and a trio in turn.

A backyard wedding on Mapleton Hill or in a neighborhood off Broadway is a Boulder wedding too, and the plan for it is the plan for a house: a solo or a duo on the patio, a trio indoors, and the number the neighbors and the city put on evening sound written into the contract before the invitations go out. What a house has to provide is on the Boulder private parties page.
