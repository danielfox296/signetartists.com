Boulder weddings

Live music for a Boulder wedding.

A Boulder wedding happens close to other people. The ceremony is on a lawn with houses beyond the trees, the cocktail hour is on a porch with the Flatirons going pink behind it, and the floor fills a dining hall or a hotel ballroom that has a sound policy on the wall. So the music is a guitarist for the vows, a duo for the hour after them at a volume the neighbours never hear, and a trio or the DJ for the dancing, indoors, under whatever number the venue sets.

This is the Boulder version of our weddings page. Book one part of the day or all of it on one contract, with sound support that’s the right size for the space, a personal contact through the day, and one short travel line for the drive up from Denver.
