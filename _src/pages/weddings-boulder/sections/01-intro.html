Boulder weddings

Live music for a Boulder wedding.

A Boulder wedding happens close to other people: the ceremony on a lawn with houses beyond the trees, the cocktail hour on a porch with the Flatirons going pink behind it, and the floor in a dining hall or a hotel ballroom with a sound policy on the wall. So the music is a guitarist for the vows, a duo for the hour after them, and a trio or the DJ for the dancing, indoors, under whatever number the venue sets.

It’s one of our weddings bookings: one part of the day or all of it, on one contract with a personal contact through the day and one short travel line for the drive up from Denver.
