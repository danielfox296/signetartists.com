The venues

The calendar these evenings sit on.

The Little Nell at the base of Aspen Mountain and the Hotel Jerome in the town's core anchor the dinner calendar, The St. Regis Aspen carries the ballroom end of it, and Aspen Meadows is the campus a working offsite actually runs on, sessions by day and dinner on the property at night. The Limelight holds the more casual end of the market, welcome nights and closing parties.

Venues at this level hold vendors to their standards, and the paperwork is the same every time: a certificate of insurance naming the venue, load-in on the venue's schedule, and the cap and curfew honoured as written. All of it arrives with the contract rather than on request.

Where the retreat wants one act across the whole arc, Tejas Singh's duo grows to a quartet on the same contract. Where it wants dinner music and no more, a jazz duo of upright bass and guitar holds the evening at a volume the table talks across.
