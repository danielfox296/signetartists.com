details and costs

The lines on an Aspen quote.

Three of them. The second and third are what make an Aspen date different from a Denver one.

The fee for the music
The band, sound support that’s the right size for the venue, the load-in, the sound check, the strike and a personal contact for the night. A duo through cocktails and dinner or a quartet when the dance floor is the point, whichever the evening needs.
Travel
Its own line on the quote. Aspen is roughly four hours from Denver each way in good conditions, over passes that don’t always give you good conditions, and that’s most of a working day at both ends of the evening.
Lodging, at cost
A band that has played a late set isn’t driving four hours of mountain highway home that night. Lodging bills at cost, on its own line. That’s the difference between a far resort and the I-70 towns, which mostly don’t need it.
Why it’s itemised
A retreat budget goes through finance, and a quote that separates fee, travel and lodging is easier to forward, question and approve.

A holiday weekend prices above a standard date across this market. How a quote is put together is on the pricing page, and the Colorado cost guide sets the mountain towns against the wider market.
