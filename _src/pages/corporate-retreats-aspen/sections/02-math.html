Travel
A flat $600 for the far resorts, on its own line. The performance fee stays the published resort figure, so the drive never hides inside it.
Lodging, at cost
A band that has played until eleven is not driving four hours of mountain highway home. Lodging bills at what it costs, on its own line, with the receipt behind it. It is the difference between a far resort and the I-70 towns, which carry the $600 alone.
Why this is published
A retreat budget goes through finance. A quote that itemises fee, travel and lodging survives being forwarded, questioned and approved, and nothing about the booking appears on the invoice for the first time.

Every size and both columns are on the rate card, and the Colorado cost guide sets those figures against the wider market. Holiday and peak weekends carry +25%, published there too.
