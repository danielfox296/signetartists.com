The far-resort math

Every line of an Aspen quote.

Three of them, and the second and third are why an Aspen date is not a Denver date with a longer drive attached.

The fee for the music
The band, the sound sized to the venue, the load-in, the sound check, the strike and the named contact for the night. A duo through cocktails and dinner or a quartet when the floor is the point, whichever the evening actually needs.
Travel
Its own line, never inside the fee. Aspen is roughly four hours from Denver each way in good conditions, over passes that do not always give you good conditions, and that is most of a working day at both ends of the evening.
Lodging, at cost
A band that has played until eleven is not driving four hours of mountain highway home. Lodging bills at what it cost, on its own line, with the receipt behind it. That is the difference between a far resort and the I-70 towns, which mostly do not need it.
Why it is itemised
A retreat budget goes through finance. A quote that separates fee, travel and lodging survives being forwarded, questioned and approved, and nothing about the booking appears on the invoice for the first time.

A holiday weekend prices above a standard date across this market. How a quote is put together is on the pricing page, and the Colorado cost guide sets the corridor against the wider market.
