02The night

01It's a quarter to eight on a Sunday and the door's been open since seven.

02Six people at the bar. Two of them came for this. The other four are figuring that out.

03The sign-up sheet is on the end of the bar, half full, mostly names the bartender already knows.

04A drummer is adjusting a hi-hat that was fine. A trumpet player nobody's met is standing by the wall with the case still shut.

05The house band counts off the first tune and the case opens.

06By the third song the guy who came in for a flight has moved to a stool with a sightline.

07Somebody's filming. Somebody always is.

08The tune goes somewhere it wasn't supposed to and the bartender looks up.

09Same time next week.
