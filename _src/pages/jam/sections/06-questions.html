Questions

Questions owners ask

Is there a fee?
Yes. The early fee is invested in marketing and promotion for the night, and the figure is part of the call. The rest of the venue's side is a corner of the floor, your normal staff for the night, a closet, a post a week and a round for the band. At week twelve we both decide whether it continues, and on what terms.
Do we need a stage or a sound system?
No stage. The band sets up in a corner of the floor and brings its own sound, sized for a taproom. The backline stays in your closet between nights, so nothing comes through your front door every week.
How loud is it?
It is a band with a drummer, so it is a band's volume. The host runs the volume from the floor. If your space has a ceiling on it, or neighbors, tell us on the call and the night gets set up around that.
Who gets to sit in?
Any musician who signs the sheet. The host runs the order and puts each one on tunes they can play.
What if only a few people sign up the first week?
The house band plays a full night whether the sheet has three names on it or twenty. The twelve weeks are there so that neither of us decides anything on week one.
Why Sunday or Monday?
They are usually a taproom's slowest nights, and they are the nights working musicians have free, because the paid gigs are on Friday and Saturday. If a different night is your slow one, say so.
What do we have to post?
One post a week from your accounts. We send the clip, cut and ready, with the venue tagged. Nothing else is on you.
