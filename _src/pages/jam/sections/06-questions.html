Questions

Questions owners ask

Is there a fee?
Yes. In the early weeks the fee goes into marketing and promotion for the night, and we'll go over the figure on the call. Beyond the fee, the venue covers a corner of the floor, your normal staff for the night, a closet for the gear, one post a week and a round for the band. At week twelve we both decide whether it continues, and on what terms.
Do we need a stage?
No. The band brings the backline and sets up in a corner of the floor. The gear stays in your closet between nights, so there's no weekly load-in through your front door.
How loud is it?
It's a band with a drummer, so it's as loud as a band in a bar. The host keeps the volume in check from the floor. If your space has a limit, or close neighbors, tell us on the call and we'll plan around it.
Who gets to sit in?
Any musician who signs the sheet. The host sets the order and puts each one on tunes they already know.
What if only a few people sign up the first week?
The house band plays a full night whether the sheet has three names on it or twenty. That's also why the trial is twelve weeks. Neither of us has to judge it on the first night.
Why Sunday or Monday?
They're usually the slowest nights in a taproom, and they're the nights working musicians have off, since the paid gigs are Fridays and Saturdays. If a different night is your slow one, tell us.
What do we have to post?
One post a week from your accounts. We send you the clip already cut and tagged, so all you do is post it.
