On the night

How the night runs

  1. 01

    The house band opens with a set.

    The band plays a full set before anyone from the sheet comes up.

  2. 02

    Musicians sign the sheet at the bar.

    The host calls them up in order, a few tunes each, on songs they already know.

  3. 03

    Once a month, a featured act plays the second set.

    An act with its own following, and their crowd comes with them.

  4. 04

    The band closes the night.

    Then the band packs the gear back into your closet until next week.

  5. 05

    You get the clip.

    We cut it and send it to you ready to post, with your venue tagged.
