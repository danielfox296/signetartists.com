On the night

How the night runs

  1. 01

    The house band opens with a set.

    A full set from the house band before anyone from the sheet sits in.

  2. 02

    Musicians sign the sheet at the bar.

    The host calls them up in turn, a few tunes each, on songs they can play.

  3. 03

    Once a month, a featured act takes the second set.

    An act with a following of its own, and their people come with them.

  4. 04

    The band closes the night.

    Then the band packs the gear into your closet for next week.

  5. 05

    You get the clip.

    Cut by us and sent to you ready to post, with the venue tagged.
