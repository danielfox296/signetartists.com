The deal

What it costs the venue, and what Signet brings

The venue covers a fee, a corner of the floor, your normal staff for the night, a closet for the gear, one post a week and a drink for the band. In the early weeks, the fee goes into marketing and promotion for the night. Signet brings the band, the host, the sign-up sheet, the backline, the monthly featured act and the weekly clips.
