Changes is a guitar trio with a modern edge. Doug Emery on guitar, Sri Viswanath on fretless bass and Brian Jaffe on drums, playing the standards the way a working trio plays them, with the arrangements left open and a little risk in the solos. It holds a dinner at conversation volume, and it holds a listening crowd when the night wants that instead.

The three players are the reason it books under a name. Doug studied guitar performance at Berklee, played more than three thousand dates across New England, from jazz duos to wedding bands, and now teaches music full time in Denver. Sri spent decades as a working bassist in New York, on stages from Smalls to the Plaza Hotel, and played on Radha Thomas's 2019 tribute to Chet Baker. Brian came through the Lamont School of Music at the University of Denver and has played more than a hundred theater productions in the Denver pit orchestras since 2011.

It books as a trio, or as a quartet with a saxophone out front when the night wants a horn on the melody. The trio is the cocktail hour and dinner size. The quartet is the one for a featured set at a gala, or a client dinner where the music is part of the program.

Their own site carries the three bios and two studio recordings, and the recordings are the quickest way to hear what the trio sounds like before you write to us.

This is a named act, booked under the names of the three players in it, and if anyone in the lineup changes, we tell you in advance.
