They play the standards the way a working trio plays them, with the arrangements left open and a little risk in the solos. Under a dinner they sit at the level of the conversation, and when the crowd turns to listen, they give it something to listen to.

Doug Emery studied guitar performance at Berklee, played more than three thousand dates across New England, from jazz duos to wedding bands, and now teaches music full time in Denver. Sri Viswanath spent decades as a working bassist in New York, on stages from Smalls to the Plaza Hotel, and played on Radha Thomas’s 2019 tribute to Chet Baker. Brian Jaffe came through the Lamont School of Music at the University of Denver and has played more than a hundred theater productions in the Denver pit orchestras since 2011.
