Changes Jazz Trio

Doug Emery, guitar. Sri Viswanath, fretless bass. Brian Jaffe, drums. Standards, trio to quartet.

A standard you know, taken somewhere new: the guitar stretches the melody, the fretless bass slides under it, and the drums stay quiet enough to talk across. Changes is a Denver guitar trio playing the standards with a modern edge, and a saxophone joins when the evening wants a featured set.
