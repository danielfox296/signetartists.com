Occasions

Common occasions for Changes Jazz Trio.

Four nights the trio plays often. Each wants a different size of the same three players, and each has a page of its own.

Client dinners
The trio sits under the conversation the way the jazz duo does, with the drums played soft and the fretless bass underneath, and the quartet with saxophone turns the same booking into a featured set when the dinner has a program in it. The corporate page covers the corporate details.
Wedding cocktail hours
The trio plays the hour after the ceremony at a volume guests can talk across, and a modern take on the standards suits a couple who want jazz in the hour without the lounge feel. The cocktail hour page covers the hour itself.
Galas and award nights
A listening act for the stretch between the seating and the program. The quartet with saxophone is the size for a gala that wants the music to be one of the things guests remember, and the galas page sets out how the cues around it work.
Dinner parties and milestone birthdays
A dinner party for thirty, or a sixtieth birthday that wants real jazz in the house. The trio fits a home or a patio on a single circuit, and the private parties page covers the configurations by size.

How the music sits around speeches and a program is planned with the run of show. What live music costs across Colorado, size by size, is set out in the cost guide.
