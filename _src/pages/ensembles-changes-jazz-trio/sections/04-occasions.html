Occasions

Who books Changes.

Client dinners
The trio sits under the conversation with the drums played soft, and the quartet with saxophone turns the same booking into a featured set when the dinner has a program in it. It books like the rest of a company event, on one contract with the paperwork your office needs.
Wedding cocktail hours
The trio plays the hour after the ceremony at a volume guests can talk across, and a modern take on the standards suits a couple who want jazz in the hour without the lounge feel.
Galas and award nights
A listening act for the stretch between the seating and the program. The quartet with saxophone is the size for a gala that wants the music to be one of the things guests remember, and it stops on the emcee’s cue like every act on the roster.
Dinner parties and milestone birthdays
A dinner party for thirty, or a sixtieth birthday that wants real jazz in the house. The trio fits a home or a patio.
