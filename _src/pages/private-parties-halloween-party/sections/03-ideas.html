Ideas

Halloween party entertainment ideas for adults.

The ideas we can actually put in a house on a Saturday in October, each with the page that covers it.

Karaoke in costume, with a real band behind it
The idea that turns a Halloween party into the one people tell stories about, because the person dressed as Prince is going to sing Prince, and the band is ready the moment somebody gets brave. The host keeps the sign-up list on paper, the crowd gets through six to ten songs an hour, and the band plays the same book as a party band whenever nobody is up. It needs the floor space of a big main floor or a garage. The live band karaoke page covers the songbook and the microphone.
A DJ set built around one decade
Pick the decade your guests grew up in and the DJ builds the night around it, with the songs that have to happen placed where you want them. It’s the smallest setup a party can book, a booth in 6 by 4 feet, and it runs as late as the house allows.
A costume contest run from the microphone
Every booking can come with an announcement microphone, so the host or whoever is judging can run the contest from the band’s sound system rather than shouting over it, and the band or the DJ plays the winner on.
A grown-up Halloween dinner before the party
Some houses do a seated dinner in costume first and open the doors to the bigger crowd after. Upright bass and guitar play the dinner at a volume the table talks across, and the same night can turn into the band or the DJ later. The private parties page covers the dinner party on its own.
The office Halloween party
The Friday-before version at work books the same three configurations, and the corporate page covers how a band works around a program and what a venue needs to know.
