Ideas

Halloween party entertainment ideas for adults.

A costume contest run from the microphone
Every booking can come with an announcement microphone, so whoever is judging runs the contest through the band’s sound system, and the band or the DJ plays the winner on.
A grown-up Halloween dinner before the party
A seated dinner in costume first, with upright bass and guitar under it, and the doors opened to the bigger crowd after, when the same night turns into the band or the DJ. The private parties page covers the dinner party on its own.
The office Halloween party
The Friday-before version at work books the same three, and the corporate page covers how a band works around a program.
