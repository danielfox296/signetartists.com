Three ways to book it

A DJ, a party band, or the band that hands over the microphone.

A Halloween party is a floor first and a dinner second, if it’s a dinner at all.

The DJ
The usual first question, and the easiest one for a house. A DJ plays the songs a costume crowd wants, built around the decade your guests grew up in, with the songs that have to happen placed where you want them, and it runs as late as the house allows.
The party band
A trio or a quartet playing the hits to a floor with no stage in it. Tony Medina plays it up to a four piece, and Tejas Singh plays a quartet built from the songs everyone in the house knows. Either can play the early part of the night quietly before the floor turns.
Live band karaoke
The band plays the 80s and 90s hits on its own, sung out front, and hands the microphone to anyone in costume who wants a song. The person dressed as Prince is going to sing Prince, and the band is ready the moment they get brave. Live band karaoke wants a big main floor, a finished basement or a garage with the cars out.
