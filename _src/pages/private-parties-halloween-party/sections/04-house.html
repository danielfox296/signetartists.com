At home

What the house needs to provide on Halloween.

A Halloween party runs late and loud, and the loud part of any home party belongs indoors once the neighbors have gone to bed, which is why the floor for this one is usually a finished basement, a garage with the cars out, or the main floor with the doors shut. A DJ takes 6 by 4 feet on one dedicated circuit, a trio 12 by 8 on one, and a quartet or the karaoke four-piece 14 by 10 on two, and none of them want to share a circuit with the fog machine and the string lights, so tell us where the decorations are plugged in before the night. If your street or HOA has quiet hours in writing, give them to us at contract and the night gets built under them.

Load-in is a parking question on a night when every house on the block has guests, so tell us about the driveway or the alley and which door is ours. Sets run 45 to 60 minutes with music through the breaks, so the party never goes quiet.
