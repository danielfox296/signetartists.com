For whomever is running the schedule

Wedding dates get decided ten to twelve months out, and twelve to eighteen for a mountain venue or a fall Saturday. We see that music tends to be shopped for 9 to 14 months before the date.

Send a date and you get a yes or a straight no on an act's availability with more details and other recommendations should you desire them.

Most of a wedding's music questions are really timing questions, and those details are on the planners pages: Things like stage footprints, and power requirements, load-in and strike time. How each moment hands off to the next can be found on the run of show page, and altitude, curfews and mountain-venue detail on the mountain corridor page.

The rest of the vendor list is on the Preferred Vendor List: the photographers, planners, videographers, caterers, florists and venues we’d hand to our own clients, each with a link to their site.
