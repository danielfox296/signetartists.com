The formats

What a wedding actually books.

Four sizes cover nearly every Colorado wedding, and the guest count decides more of it than the budget does. Each one is spec'd on its own page: instrumentation, footprint, power and a sample set.

The solo guitarist
One instrument, the smallest booking here, and the standard ceremony build. It plays a processional without amplification in most indoor spaces and sets up in 6 by 4 feet. Details on the solo guitarist page.
The jazz duo, and the trio
Upright bass and guitar playing standards, the lead booking for the cocktail hour and the one that carries straight through dinner. The trio adds drums played with brushes for a bigger floor or a longer guest list at the same conversation volume. Both are on the jazz duo and trio page.
The acoustic duo
The same footprint with the set turned toward singer-songwriter covers, for a wedding that wants the songs one notch more familiar than standards. Where the hour wants a voice, Tejas Singh fronts it. The format is on the acoustic duo page.
Spanish guitar
Nylon strings and real flamenco, played by Dirty Flamenco. It works as ceremony music, as an hour of palos instead of standards, or as one set piece placed where the reception leaves space for it.

Every size arrives with sound, load-in and strike, a certificate of insurance naming the venue, and a named contact reachable through the day. The whole song list is on the repertoire page.
