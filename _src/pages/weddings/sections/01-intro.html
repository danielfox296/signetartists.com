Weddings

Live music for a Colorado wedding.

A guitar starts the processional, and everyone turns toward the bride. An hour later those same guests enjoy a jazz duo on over cocktails. By the end of the night, they’re shaking it on the dance floor .

3 different jobs for the music - one booking covers all of them.

Learn more about: the ceremony, where an acoustic or Spanish guitarist plays the prelude, the processional and the recessional, and the cocktail hour, where a jazz or acoustic duo plays at a volume guests can talk across. For the reception, a DJ hands out late night bangers.
