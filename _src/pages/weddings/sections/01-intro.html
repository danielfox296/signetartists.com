Weddings

Live music for a Colorado wedding.

A wedding has three pieces of music in it and they are three different jobs. Guitar carries the walk down the aisle, where the whole guest list is listening and nobody is talking. The hour after it has to do the reverse, sitting under a hundred conversations that all start at once. Then a floor has to fill and stay full until the last song. One booking covers all three.

The two live moments have their own pages here: the ceremony, where an acoustic or Spanish guitarist plays the prelude, the processional and the recessional, and the cocktail hour, where a jazz or acoustic duo plays at a volume guests can talk across. For the reception, a DJ holds the late night on the same contract and through the same sound system. In the city, the Denver cocktail hour page covers the venues and the downtown logistics.
