Weddings

Live music for a Colorado wedding.

The guests go quiet, a guitar starts the processional, and every head turns toward the aisle. An hour later the same guests are talking over a jazz duo on the terrace, and by the end of the night they’re on the dance floor. Those are three different jobs for the music, and one booking covers all three.

The two live moments have their own pages: the ceremony, where an acoustic or Spanish guitarist plays the prelude, the processional and the recessional, and the cocktail hour, where a jazz or acoustic duo plays at a volume guests can talk across. For the reception, a DJ holds the late night on the same contract and through the same sound system, and in the city the Denver cocktail hour page covers the venues.
