For the planner running the day

Most cocktail hours sit inside a day a planner is timing, and the working documents live on the planners pages: stage footprints and power by size, load-in and strike durations, what arrives with the contract. How the hour hands off to dinner, and how transitions land against a timeline, is on the run of show page. Wedding dates book ten to twelve months out, and twelve to eighteen for a mountain venue or a fall Saturday, so the cocktail hour is decided when the band is.

The paperwork is the fast part. A certificate of insurance naming the venue, a W-9 and one itemised invoice come in the same email as the contract, and a named contact with a mobile number in writing is reachable from load-in to load-out.
