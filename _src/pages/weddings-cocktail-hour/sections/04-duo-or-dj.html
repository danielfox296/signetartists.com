Duo or DJ

Two players for the hour, a DJ for the late night.

A duo reads the crowd while the crowd is still forming, shades its volume live and takes a request. A DJ earns the booking after dinner instead, holding a floor for hours at a stretch. For one hour before dinner they are not close.

The jazz duo
Upright bass and guitar playing standards, the set sampled at the top of this page. It books for the hour on its own, or the same two players carry cocktails through dinner. This is the lead booking for the hour.
The acoustic duo
The same footprint with the set turned toward singer-songwriter covers, for a wedding that wants the songs one notch more familiar than standards. Details on the acoustic duo page.
The solo guitarist
One instrument, the smallest booking on the roster, sized for a short guest list or a second space the crowd passes through. Details on the solo guitarist page.
Tejas Singh, where the hour wants a voice
Voice and guitar with bass behind it, singer-songwriter covers fronted by the name on the act. Configurations and the set are on the Tejas Singh page.
The DJ, after dinner
For the hour itself, a duo is the better instrument. The DJ is the late-night answer, on the same contract and through the same sound system when the night runs past the last set.
