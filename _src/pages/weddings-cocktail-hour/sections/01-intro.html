Wedding cocktail hour

Live music for cocktail hour at a Colorado wedding.

Cocktail hour is the turn of the wedding. The ceremony ends, the couple leaves for photographs, and the guests stand with a first drink deciding what kind of night this is going to be. A jazz duo holds that hour at conversation volume, close enough that guests can hear the bass, and the hour feels like the start of the party.

The duo is upright bass and guitar playing standards, and the same two musicians can carry cocktails through dinner. An acoustic duo or a solo guitarist covers the hour in a different flavor. The hour before this one is the ceremony, Denver weddings have their own page, and the whole day routes from the weddings page.
