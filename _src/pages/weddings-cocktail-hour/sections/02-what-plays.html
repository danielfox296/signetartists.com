The set

Standards at conversation volume.

What should play is the easier half of the question: jazz standards and singer-songwriter covers, songs guests recognize without having to name. The harder half is volume.

The volume. The duo plays at about the level of the conversation around it, so two people who have just met talk across it without leaning in. That’s the real work of the hour, because half the guest list is meeting the other half.

The songs. Standards feel like a careful choice and don't ask for anyone's attention. A duo of upright bass and guitar leaves space in the middle of the sound where conversation sits. The instrumentation, the trio option and the longer sample set are on the jazz duo and trio page.
