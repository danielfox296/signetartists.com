The calendar

The office parties book the December Saturdays first.

The constraint on a holiday party at home is the calendar. In 2026 the December Saturdays before Christmas are the 5th, the 12th and the 19th, every office party in the city wants the same three nights, and those bookings get decided between August and October. Peak weekends carry their own line on the quote. Send us the date as soon as you have it and we’ll check the booking calendar and come back with a configuration and a price for that night.

Thanksgiving week
Thanksgiving falls on Thursday, November 26 in 2026, and the week books two ways: the night before, when the house fills with people who flew in, and the Friendsgiving on the Saturday before or after it. Both are dinners, so both book a solo or a duo, upright bass and guitar or one guitarist playing the standards at a volume the table talks across, with the season kept out of the set until you ask for it. The same size plays Christmas Eve and Christmas Day dinners.
New Year’s Eve at home
New Year’s Eve is a Thursday in 2026 and it gets booked earlier than any other date of the season, because every venue and every house in the city wants the same night. At home it’s a trio or a quartet with the DJ through the same sound system, the countdown gets the announcement microphone, and because it runs late it stays indoors. The Christmas jazz trio plays it with the set turning from the season to the swing book as the night goes on.
The January party
Some houses skip December on purpose and throw the party in the second or third week of January, when nobody else is asking for the same Saturday. It books the same sizes, and the January party post makes the case for the office version of the same move.
