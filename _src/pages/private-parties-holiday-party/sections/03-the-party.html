The party

Three holiday parties a house throws.

The open house
Neighbors, family and the people from work drifting in and out over an afternoon and an evening, so there are twelve people in the house at one point and sixty at another. A solo or a duo plays the season with the standards threaded through, so the people who stay all afternoon hear something new every hour.
The seated dinner
One long table, or a few, and the meal is the point of the night. Upright bass and guitar play under it, the toast gets an announcement microphone, and a dinner for someone, a fortieth in December, gets its set built the way a milestone birthday does.
The party that ends in dancing
The cocktail party where the coffee table ends up in the hall and somebody’s aunt is the first one dancing. A trio or a quartet fills a main floor with no stage in it, plays dinner quietly first, and hands the late end to the DJ. It’s the one to book earliest.
