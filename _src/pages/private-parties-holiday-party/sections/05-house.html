At home

What a December house needs to provide.

The tree usually stands in the corner a band would take, so the first thing we ask is which corner is ours. A solo or a DJ takes 6 by 4 feet, a duo 8 by 6, a trio 12 by 8, and each of them wants one dedicated circuit that the oven and the coffee makers aren’t on, because a December kitchen is the busiest electrical load in the house. Beyond that, the ask is a door we can carry through and somewhere to park for the load-in, and on a street lined with your guests’ cars that means telling us about the driveway or the alley before the night.

Dinner plays at 65 to 72 dBA at the nearest table, which is the level of the conversation around it, and sets run 45 to 60 minutes with music through the breaks so the house never goes quiet. The loud part of a winter party is indoors by definition, which makes December the easy month for the neighbors; if your building or HOA has quiet hours in writing, give them to us at contract and we build the night under them.
