Ideas

Holiday party entertainment ideas for adults, at home.

A Spanish guitarist for a long dinner
The request that turns up for December dinners at home more than any other specific sound: a guitar with real technique in it under a long table. Dirty Flamenco plays it as one guitarist or as a trio, with a dancer when the evening wants a set piece.
A surprise song for someone in the house
The one song a guest has been waiting for, played for them in front of everyone, whether it’s a proposal at the party or a song for the grandparents.
A Christmas set at the moment you choose
Carols wall to wall, one Christmas set placed after dinner, or the balance the trio reads off the crowd.
