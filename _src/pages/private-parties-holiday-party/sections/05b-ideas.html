Ideas

Holiday party entertainment ideas for adults, at home.

Hosts search for ideas before they search for a band, so here are the ones we can actually deliver in a house, each with the page that covers it.

A Christmas jazz trio for the dinner
The Charlie Brown record and the standards of the season on guitar, upright bass and drums with brushes, at a volume the table talks across. It’s the idea that makes a house sound like December without a playlist. The Christmas jazz trio page has the book.
Live band karaoke for a big main floor
The band plays the 80s and 90s hits on its own and hands the microphone to anyone who wants a song, with the words on a screen and a host keeping the sign-up list, so nobody has to sing for the night to work. It wants a big main floor or a finished basement. The live band karaoke page covers how a guest gets to the microphone.
A singer with a guitar taking requests
One voice and a guitar in the corner, building the songs people ask for from the ground up. Tejas Singh does it with a looper, and the same booking grows to a quartet if the night turns into a floor.
A Spanish guitarist for a long dinner
The request that turns up for December dinners at home more than any other specific sound: a guitar with real technique in it playing under a long table. Dirty Flamenco plays it as one guitarist or as a trio, and a dancer joins when the evening wants a set piece.
A surprise song for someone in the house
The one song a guest has been waiting for, played for them in front of everyone, whether it’s a proposal at the party or a song for the grandparents. The proposals and surprise songs page covers how it’s planned with the music rather than around it.
A DJ for the last hour
The cheapest way to keep a party going after the band, through the same sound system on the same contract, and the most common single booking of the corporate season for a reason.
