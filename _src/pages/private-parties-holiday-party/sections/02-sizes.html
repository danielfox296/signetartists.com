Sizes

The size that fits the house.

A holiday party at home is usually smaller than the host imagines, because the music has to fit where the party already is. These are the four sizes a house books in December, and any of them can be the whole night or one part of it.

One guitar, or a singer with a guitar
The size for an open house or a dinner for twelve. A jazz guitarist plays the Christmas book with the standards threaded through it, or a singer with a guitar plays the songs your guests ask for. Where the night will want a floor later, Tejas Singh plays the dinner on his own and the same booking grows to a quartet for the dancing.
The Christmas jazz duo or trio
Upright bass and guitar, with drums played with brushes when the party is bigger than a dinner, playing the Guaraldi record, the standards that are Christmas songs and the standards that aren’t. The balance of season to standards is yours, and the whole December book is on the Christmas jazz trio page.
The party band
A trio or a quartet playing the songs everyone in the house knows, for the part of the night when the furniture moves. Tony Medina plays it up to a four piece, and live band karaoke is the version where the band hands the microphone to whoever wants a song, on a big main floor or in a finished basement.
The DJ
A DJ on their own, or after the band through the same sound system on the same contract, and the easiest late end a house can book. Band or DJ has its own post.
