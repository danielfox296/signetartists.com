What it costs

What a Breckenridge date adds.

The corridor drive, the calendar, and a longer working day at 9,600 feet.

Travel is its own line on the quote, the I-70 corridor from Denver over the pass. What the altitude adds is not a line at all: it is time. The generous end of the load-in window, a longer sound check, paced sets and a retune between them. That planning is inside the booking rather than billed on top of it, and there is no altitude item on a Breckenridge quote.

The calendar is the other variable. A holiday weekend prices above a standard date across this market, and December in Breckenridge is the busiest the town gets. New Year's Eve is its own case here as it is everywhere.

Milestone birthdays, anniversaries and family gatherings book the same venues the same way, on one contract with a certificate of insurance naming the venue. Those occasions live on the private parties page, and the Colorado market is set out in the cost guide.
