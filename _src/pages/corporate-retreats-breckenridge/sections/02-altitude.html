9,600 feet

What the altitude changes.

Breath. Singers and horns tire faster in thin, dry air, so sets are paced with real breaks between them, and music keeps playing through the breaks. Pacing is the difference between a band that finishes strong and one that fades early.

Tuning. The temperature swing between sound check and the last set runs wide up here, and instruments drift with it. Retunes between sets are part of the plan, and the sound check runs longer than the same band would take in Denver.

Carrying gear is slower work at this altitude too, so setup takes the generous end of its window. The altitude of every mountain town is cited on the mountain corridor page.
