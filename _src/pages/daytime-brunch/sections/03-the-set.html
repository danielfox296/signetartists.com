The set

Standards, bossa nova, and the songs that suit a slow morning.

The duo plays the standards and the bossa novas: Autumn Leaves, The Girl from Ipanema, All of Me, Blue Bossa, Someday My Prince Will Come, and the rest of a set built for a dining space where the coffee is the loudest thing in it. A guitarist alone plays the same set instrumentally, a Spanish guitarist plays it on nylon strings for a patio, and a singer with a guitar takes the folk and singer-songwriter end of the song list.

The jazz duo and trio page covers the format in full, and the solo guitarist, Spanish guitarist and singer and guitarist pages cover the one-musician versions.
