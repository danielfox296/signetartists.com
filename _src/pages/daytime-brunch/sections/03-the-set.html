The set

Standards, bossa nova, and the songs that suit a slow morning.

The duo plays the standards and the bossa novas: Autumn Leaves, The Girl from Ipanema, All of Me, Blue Bossa, Someday My Prince Will Come, and the rest of a set built for a dining space where the coffee is the loudest thing in it. A guitarist alone plays the same set instrumentally, and a Spanish guitarist plays it on nylon strings for a patio. A singer with a guitar takes the folk and singer-songwriter end of the published song list, sung at a volume a table of eight talks across.

Every size plays at 65 to 72 dBA at the nearest table. A duo sets up in 8 by 6 feet with one outlet and a solo in 6 by 4, and load-in is 45 minutes to an hour before doors, so the setup is finished before the first seating. On a patio the three outdoor conditions apply: a covered, level and dry spot, power run to it or a battery-powered system, and a call time for moving inside.

The jazz duo and trio page covers the format in full, and the solo guitarist, Spanish guitarist and singer and guitarist pages cover the one-musician versions. What live music costs in this market is on the pricing page.
