Who books it

A restaurant’s Sunday, or a family’s.

Restaurants, hotels and clubs
A standing Sunday, weekly or monthly, with the same configuration every time: upright bass and guitar, a guitarist alone, or a pianist where the dining space has a piano. The musicians set up before the first seating, play sets of 45 to 60 minutes with music through the breaks, and pack up after the last. The footprint is an 8 by 6 foot corner, the volume is 65 to 72 dBA at the nearest table, and the run is one contract with one personal contact.
The morning after a wedding
A post-wedding brunch at the hotel, a rented house or a family’s home, with the same duo that played the cocktail hour the day before or a guitarist on their own, playing the songs from the weekend at a volume that suits a slow morning. The weddings page covers the day before it, and the brunch goes on the same contract.
A bridal brunch, a shower or a birthday
A private dining space, a garden, a table of twenty at home. A guitarist or a Spanish guitarist plays under the meal, and a singer with a guitar plays the songs the guest of honour asked for. The showers page covers the baby shower and the bridal shower on their own, and the private parties page covers the evening version of the same booking.
A client or member brunch
A hotel’s member event, a company’s family day, a club’s Mother’s Day seating: a pianist or a duo playing the standards while the tables turn. The client dinners page makes the same volume argument for the evening.
