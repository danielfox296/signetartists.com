Who books it

A restaurant’s Sunday, or a family’s.

Restaurants, hotels and clubs
A standing Sunday, weekly or monthly, with the same musicians every time: upright bass and guitar, a guitarist alone, or a pianist where the dining space has a piano, on one contract for the whole run.
The morning after a wedding
A post-wedding brunch at the hotel, a rented house or a family’s home, with the same duo that played the cocktail hour the day before or a guitarist alone, playing the songs from the weekend. The wedding and the brunch go on the same contract.
A bridal brunch, a shower or a birthday
A private dining space, a garden, a table of twenty at home. A guitarist or a Spanish guitarist plays under the meal, and a singer with a guitar plays the songs the guest of honor asked for. Showers have their own page, and the evening version is on private parties.
A client or member brunch
A hotel’s member event, a company’s family day, a club’s Mother’s Day seating: a pianist or a duo playing the standards while the tables turn, the daytime version of a client dinner.
