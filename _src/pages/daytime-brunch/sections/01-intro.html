Sunday brunch

Live music for a Sunday brunch.

The coffee keeps coming, a table of eight is on its second round of mimosas, and in the corner upright bass and guitar are playing The Girl from Ipanema. Restaurants, hotels and clubs book it as a standing Sunday, and families book it for the morning after a wedding, a bridal brunch, a shower or a birthday. It’s the same music either way, from a duo, a solo guitarist or a pianist.

It’s one of our daytime bookings, and a pianist plays it where the dining space has a piano.
