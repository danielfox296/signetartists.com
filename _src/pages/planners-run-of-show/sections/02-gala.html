Look twice at two of those rows. 7:00pm is the only reliable way to move four hundred people from a bar into a seated room, and a band doing it takes about a third of the time an announcement does. 9:15pm is the changeover: a seven-piece cannot get from a dinner setup to a dance setup silently, and the DJ covering that gap is the difference between a transition and a lull.
