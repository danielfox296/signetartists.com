The failure mode specific to this night is 8:00pm. Remarks at a holiday party start late, run long, and land on a room that has been drinking since half past six. Put a hard finish on them in the document, give it to whoever is speaking in advance, and put the band back up on time regardless. The room forgives a short speech. It does not come back from a long one.

If the program wants a louder chapter, live-band karaoke adds $1,500 and works best here, in the second set, once the room is warm enough that people will actually get up.
