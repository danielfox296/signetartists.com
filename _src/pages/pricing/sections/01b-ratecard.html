The rate card

Starting prices by configuration. The left column is the Denver metro. The right is the mountain and resort corridor: Vail, Beaver Creek, Aspen, Breckenridge, Telluride, Steamboat and the Broadmoor. Resort dates carry travel and lodging, which is why they are priced as their own column rather than as a surcharge you find out about later.

Solo through quartet is what the roster books. A five or six piece is quoted on request and priced on the same card, so you can see the number before you ask for it. Weddings run above private-party rates at the same size, because the day is longer, the run of show is tighter and the load-in is rarely simple. Every act's own ladder is priced on its page on the roster.

What moves a price inside the range

Up: a wedding rather than a private party. A Saturday in June, September or October. A holiday weekend. A venue with a hard load-in, a long carry or a late curfew. A run of show with ceremony, cocktails, dinner and dancing all needing cover.

Down: a weeknight. A short call. A single block rather than the whole evening. A date inside a month we have not filled. Booking three or more parts of the night together, which takes 10% off performance fees.

Ranges are honest rather than decorative. If you tell us the date, the venue and the guest count, the number we send back is inside the range on this page and it does not move afterwards.
