What changes a booking price

Price Increase: a wedding rather than a private party. A Saturday in June, September or October. A holiday weekend, which this market prices {{market:denver_holiday_surcharge}} above a standard date. A venue with a hard load-in, a long carry or a late curfew. A run of show with ceremony, cocktails, dinner and dancing all needing cover. A drive over the passes, and past them a night's lodging.

Price Decrease: Booked on an early weeknight. A short call. A single set rather than the whole evening. A smaller build for the same music. A venue where the load-in is easy.

Send us the date, the venue and the guest count, and the price that comes back accounts for all of it.
