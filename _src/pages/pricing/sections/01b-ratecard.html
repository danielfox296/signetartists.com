What the market charges

These are published figures from cost guides, a wedding-budget dataset and one booking marketplace, each read on the date below. They describe the Colorado and national markets, not us. They are here because a budget has to start somewhere, and the honest place to start is what live music actually goes for.

The spread is the point. A marketplace listing and a full band with horns are two different purchases, and a figure only reads right next to what it covers. What sits inside a quote, sound in particular, varies enough between acts that two quotes at the same number can be two different evenings.

What moves a number

Up: a wedding rather than a private party. A Saturday in June, September or October. A holiday weekend, which this market prices above a standard date. A venue with a hard load-in, a long carry or a late curfew. A run of show with ceremony, cocktails, dinner and dancing all needing cover. A drive over the passes, and past them a night's lodging.

Down: a weeknight. A short call. A single block rather than the whole evening. A smaller build for the same music. A venue the load-in is easy at.

Send the date, the venue and the guest count, and the number that comes back accounts for all of it. It does not move afterwards.
