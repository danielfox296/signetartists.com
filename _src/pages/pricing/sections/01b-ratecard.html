What the market charges

Denver first, then the national picture around it. These are published figures from cost guides and a wedding-budget dataset, each read on the date below. They describe the market rather than us, and they are here because a budget has to start somewhere.

Read each one next to what it covers. Sound is the line that most often makes two quotes at the same number cover two different evenings: it is inside some acts' figures and a later conversation with others. Insurance, travel and a named contact for the night vary the same way.

Listings well under this range exist, on the booking marketplaces, and they are a real option for some nights. What they buy is the act. The cost guide sets that tier out against the rest of the market so you can see which one your evening actually needs.

What moves a number

Up: a wedding rather than a private party. A Saturday in June, September or October. A holiday weekend, which this market prices {{market:denver_holiday_surcharge}} above a standard date. A venue with a hard load-in, a long carry or a late curfew. A run of show with ceremony, cocktails, dinner and dancing all needing cover. A drive over the passes, and past them a night's lodging.

Down: a weeknight. A short call. A single block rather than the whole evening. A smaller build for the same music. A venue the load-in is easy at.

Send the date, the venue and the guest count, and the number that comes back accounts for all of it. It does not move afterwards.
