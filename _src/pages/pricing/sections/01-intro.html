Pricing

What live music costs, and what moves the number.

This page is the market: what a band goes for in Colorado, how a quote is put together, and which parts of a night move the figure. Every number on it belongs to the market and carries the source it was read from, so you can set a budget before you write to anybody.

We do not publish our prices. A Saturday in December and a Tuesday in September are not the same job, and neither are four hours in a Denver ballroom and one hour past the passes. Send us the date and what you are trying to solve, and you get our number for that night.
