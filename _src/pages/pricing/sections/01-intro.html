Pricing

Published, not quoted on request.

Most bands in this market quote on request. Here is the whole rate card, so you can build a number for your event without waiting on an email.

How it works

Two parts. A call-out covers the evening we commit regardless of length — travel, load-in, setup, sound check, strike, load-out and production. Then an hourly rate for performance time.

A duo playing one hour still uses a whole evening, so a four-hour block is the wrong unit. This way a one-hour cocktail set and a four-hour reception are both priced honestly.

There is no overtime rate. Additional time bills at the same hourly figure in 30-minute increments. Hour five costs what hour four cost, so there is nothing to negotiate at 11pm in front of your client.
