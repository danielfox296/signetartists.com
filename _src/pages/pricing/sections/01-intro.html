Pricing

Every rate, on this page.

A solo act starts at $900. A quartet through a Denver evening is $4,000 to $5,500, and the same quartet in a resort town is $5,500 to $7,500. Every figure in between is published below, so you can put a real number in a budget before you write to us.

Almost nobody in this market publishes a rate card. The usual answer is a form, a discovery call, and a quote three days later that you cannot compare to anything. We would rather lose the enquiries that were never going to close and keep the ones that can.
