How signet puts together a live entertainment quote

Most acts in this market bill two things. A call-out covers the evening committed regardless of length: the drive, load-in, setup, sound check, strike, load-out and the production that rides along with all of it (in business parlance, they're the fixed costs). Then a rate for performance time (these map to variable cost). That is why the price for one hour of cocktail entertainment is not strictly a quarter of the price of a four-hour reception.

Sound is the line worth checking hardest. With some acts it sits inside the figure. With others it arrives later, or the venue is expected to have a system that was specified for speeches. Ours is included at every size, which is a statement about scope rather than about price.

Travel and lodging are separate line items for us. Similarly, high-demand times like holiday weekends add a little extra to the bill.

The Colorado market for these bookings is set out price-by-price in the cost guide, and the corporate version,(the types of entertainment Denver companies actually book) is in what live music costs for a corporate event.
