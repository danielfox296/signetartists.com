Booking more than one part of the night

Once we are in the building the call-out is already paid. So adding a smaller group for the ceremony, cocktails or dinner bills at the hourly rate only, with no second call-out.

An hour of cocktail duo added to a full band booking is $450. An hour of DJ after the band is $350. Booking three or more parts of the night together takes 10% off performance fees. How much of the night to book is your call.

If you would rather bring your own ceremony musician and use us for the dance floor, that is a normal booking.
