Booking more than one part of the night

Once we are in the building the call-out is already paid. So adding a smaller group for the ceremony, cocktails or dinner bills at the hourly rate only, with no second call-out.

An hour of cocktail duo added to a full band booking is $450. An hour of DJ after the band is $350. Two separate vendors cannot reach those numbers, because both would charge you a full setup.

Booking three or more parts of the night together takes 10% off. It is offered, never required — if you want your own ceremony musician and us for dancing, that is a normal booking.
