Firstwater is a sound bath led by one facilitator, and if you’ve been to one you already know how to arrive: lie down, get warm, and rest. The facilitator composes the music for each session, plays live instruments over it, and mixes the whole thing from the floor, lying where your people will lie. The facilitator treats the venue like a studio and tunes the sound system to it, so if you have a space, Firstwater can make it sound like a different one.

Your people come in to low light and lie down, and the mats and blankets are already there. The session opens with ceremony, then there are seventy-five minutes of sound strong enough to feel in your chest and soft enough to rest inside, with real silence partway through, and it closes the same way. Nobody is asked to share, to speak, or to be anywhere but on the floor. Some people follow the guidance closely, some drift, and some sleep, and each of those is a fine way to spend the hour. Afterward there’s tea near the door, and people stay awhile or slip out as they please.
