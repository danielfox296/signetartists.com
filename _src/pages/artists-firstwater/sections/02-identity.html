Firstwater is a sound bath led by one facilitator, and if you’ve been to one you already know how to arrive: lie down, get warm, and rest. The facilitator composes the music for each session, plays live instruments over it, and mixes the whole thing from the floor, lying where your people will lie. The facilitator treats the venue like a studio and tunes the sound system to it, so if you have a space, Firstwater can make it sound like a different one.

Your people come in to low light and lie down, and the mats and blankets are already there. The session opens with ceremony, then there are seventy-five minutes of sound strong enough to feel in your chest and soft enough to rest inside, with real silence partway through, and it closes the same way. Nobody is asked to share, to speak, or to be anywhere but on the floor. Some people follow the guidance closely, some drift, and some sleep, and each of those is a fine way to spend the hour. Afterward there’s tea near the door, and people stay awhile or slip out as they please.

Companies book it as a corporate sound bath three ways: a team session at your office or in a partner space in Denver, one or more sessions built into an offsite or a retreat, and a transition day, with a session for the people leaving a company and for the team that stays. As a group sound healing session it suits a wellness week or a team building day, and it’s for hospitals, hospices, libraries, schools and conference organizers as much as for companies and event planners. Couples book it too, as a private sound bath for a wedding weekend or a bachelorette, at the house they rented or a partner space in Denver. Firstwater brings the sound system, the mats, the blankets and the waivers, and what it needs from you is enough floor for your people to lie down, a standard outlet, and about ninety minutes ahead of doors.

Firstwater’s own site is where to read about the public sound baths it holds around Denver and the Front Range, the plain answers to what happens at one, and a journal written in the facilitator’s own words.

This is a named act. What you book is Firstwater, and the one facilitator behind the name is the one who arrives with the sound system and runs the session.
