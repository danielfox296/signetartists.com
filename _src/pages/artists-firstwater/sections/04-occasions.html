Occasions

Four ways a sound bath gets booked.

These are the four bookings Firstwater’s own site sets out. Two of them have a Signet page for the evening around the session, and the other two have pages of their own on that site.

The team session
One venue and one session, at your office or in a partner space in Denver, for a wellness week, a quarter close, or the month a team earned something quiet. It’s the simplest of the three, and the corporate page covers what else a company books around an evening.
Offsites and retreats
Firstwater builds one or more sessions into the agenda with you, and uses them to close an evening or open a morning, which is where a sound bath does its best work. A team that has spent the day in meetings gets seventy-five minutes with nothing to do. The retreats page covers the evenings a retreat books, and a session is the close of one of them or the morning after.
Transition days
When a company is doing a hard thing well, this is a session for the people leaving and for the team that stays, and it’s usually the employer who books it. The page for it on Firstwater’s own site is called Laid Off, and it’s written for the people who’ll be in it.
Wedding weekends and bachelorettes
A private sound bath for the wedding party, the bachelorette, or the couple, at the house you rented, your venue, or a partner space in Denver. It goes into the weekend as the welcome evening, the morning of, or the day after for whoever stayed, and the weddings page covers the music for the rest of it.

Where a sound bath sits in an offsite week, beside the evenings the music covers, is in company offsite ideas in Colorado for remote teams. During the day, for a yoga studio, a senior center, a hospice, a school or an office at lunchtime, the daytime sound bath page covers where it books and what a venue needs.
