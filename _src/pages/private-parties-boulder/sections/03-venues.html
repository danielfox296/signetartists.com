The venues

Chautauqua to the St Julien, and the houses in between.

Boulder's private events concentrate in a short list of the market's places. Chautauqua's Community House and Dining Hall carry the milestone dinners and anniversaries. The Hotel Boulderado and the St Julien hold the downtown celebrations. Flagstaff House holds the dinner that is booked for its view.

Each carries its own sound reality. Chautauqua sits inside a residential historic district. The Boulderado and the St Julien put their event floors a corridor away from sleeping guests. Flagstaff House sits up a mountain road with the city below it. The conclusion is the same at all of them: ask for the venue's sound policy early, choose a size that sits comfortably under it, and put the number in the contract.
