Priced for Boulder

The sizes Boulder books.

A quartet exists for a floor that has to fill. Most Boulder evenings are conversation first, in venues and neighborhoods that sit close together, and a solo, duo or trio covers that with the volume ceiling to spare. When a date genuinely wants four pieces we will say so, and we will tell you which side of that line your night sits on.

A solo through a patio dinner is the smallest of these and the one Boulder books most, because a patio at conversation volume is a real constraint before it is a preference. A trio is the biggest night most Boulder venues allow, and it fills a floor without needing a stage to do it.

Boulder is a Front Range drive from Denver, so the travel line is short and the date does most of the moving. What live music costs in this market is on the pricing page, and the cost guide sets out the Colorado figures.
