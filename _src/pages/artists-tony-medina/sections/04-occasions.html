Occasions

Common occasions for Tony Medina.

Blues and rock are songs almost everybody knows. Each of these occasions wants a different size of the same act, and each one has a full page of its own.

Milestones and private parties
A fiftieth or a sixtieth birthday is a great fit for him. The guests grew up on these records and will sing along from the first few bars, and the act starts the evening at a size that lets people talk and ends it at a size that fills a floor.
Office holiday parties
Classic rock covers are a reliable floor-filler for a mixed-age company party, because nobody has to like the same decade to know the songs. The four piece is the size that opens a floor once the program is done, and Denver carries the downtown season in detail.
Retreat evenings in the mountains
An evening after a day of sessions usually wants something loose and easy. Solo or duo on a deck or by a fire fits it, and the acoustic end of his book carries an evening without turning it into a show.
Welcome drinks and rehearsal dinners
The night before, and the hour after the ceremony. Solo voice and guitar sits under the conversation at the volume that hour needs, and it’s a great pick when a couple wants something with more grit in it than a jazz set.
