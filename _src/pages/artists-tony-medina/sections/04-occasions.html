Where it lands

Four nights this music was built for.

Blues and rock are the two genres almost nobody has to be sold on. The nights below each want a different size of the same act, and each one carries a full page of its own.

Milestones and private parties
A fiftieth or a sixtieth birthday is the single best fit on this roster for him. The guests grew up on these records, they will sing at the first four bars, and the act starts the evening at a size that lets people talk and ends it at a size that fills a floor.
Office holiday parties
Classic rock covers are the safest floor-filler a mixed-age company party has, because nobody has to like the same decade to know the songs. The four piece is the size that opens a floor once the program is done, and Denver carries the downtown calendar in detail.
Retreat evenings in the mountains
An evening after a day of sessions wants something loose rather than formal. Solo or duo on a deck or by a fire is the register for it, and the acoustic end of his book carries an evening without turning it into a show.
Welcome drinks and rehearsal dinners
The night before, and the hour after the ceremony. Solo voice and guitar sits under conversation at the volume that hour needs, and it is the pick when a couple wants something with more grain in it than a jazz set.

How a night is paced around speeches and a program is run of show work, written before the day. Where this sits in the wider Colorado market is set out in the cost guide.
