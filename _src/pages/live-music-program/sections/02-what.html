The program

What you get.

The same musician every week
One act, picked for your bar and your crowd, on the same nights for the whole term. Regulars come back for the person as much as the music.
A sub who fits
When the regular musician can’t play, another act from the roster plays the night, in the same style and at the same volume. You hear about it from us before the night, and the night goes ahead.
A change if the first fit is wrong
If the act isn’t right for the crowd after the first weeks, we swap it. Say so and a different act plays the following week.
Extra nights when you want them
A private party, a holiday or a game night gets added to the same program, with the same act if you want them.
Sound sized for a bar
The act brings a small PA and runs it, or plugs into your house system. Sets sit at the level of a conversation at the next table, and the musician turns down the moment the floor asks.
One invoice a month
One itemized invoice from one company, with the paperwork your venue asks for, every month of the term.
