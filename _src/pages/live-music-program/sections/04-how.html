Getting started

How it starts.

A conversation
Tell us the venue, the nights you have in mind and what the bar sounds like now. We come back with the act we’d put there and a price per night for your venue.
One evening at your bar
Before the first night, one of us spends an evening at the bar to hear the crowd and the volume the floor can take. The set gets built to that.
The first night, then every week
The act loads in, plays, and comes back on the same night the next week. We check in with you every month and adjust the set, the volume or the act.
