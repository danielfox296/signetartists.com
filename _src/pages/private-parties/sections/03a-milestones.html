Milestone birthdays

The birthday that gets a band.

For most birthdays, a restaurant with family and friends is a perfect night. But, sometimes the celebration is so important that the whole night is built to cheer on the guest of honor. If you're throwing the kind of party that's worth a band, check out some of our recomendations.

A 30th Birthday
These are the one that run latest and hits hardest. A trio fills a floor without a stage, and a DJ can get your guests dancing through the end of the night.
A 40th Birthday
These usually look more like two parties in one evening. Guests arrive and eat, then there's some letting loose. A trio or quartet can work well for these situations.
A 50th Birthday
These are more often a seated dinner with a toast. Live players hang out at a lower volume at the nearest table, and the song list is built from the years the guest of honor cares about.
A 60th Birthday and Beyond
These are earlier, warmer, with more family in the space and more of it seated. A solo player or a jazz duo holds this evening from arrival through dessert, and jazz standards are the repertoire that reaches everyone in the family at once.

The intention and flow of most parties similar. A toast, and a surprise entrance, a special video. Tell us the location, the plan and roughly who is coming, and we can build a setlist for the honoree specifically The birthday party page covers the whole night, the surprise and the ideas.
