The occasions

Nine parties with a page of their own.

Each one covers the size a house books for it, the dates that matter, what the house or the yard has to provide, and the ideas we can actually deliver.

Engagement party
The first night the two families are in one place, with the couple’s song learned for it and the same duo available for the wedding’s cocktail hour.
Baby and bridal showers
An afternoon at a long table with one guitar under it, and shower entertainment ideas that aren’t games.
Graduation party
Late May and June, two crowds in one yard, and the singer who reaches the grandparents’ table and the graduate’s friends at once.
Backyard party
The patio size and the lawn size, the power and the cover a yard has to provide, and where the loud part goes once the sun is down.
Milestone birthday party
The 30th, 40th, 50th and 60th, with the size that suits each number, the surprise entrance planned with the music, and the toast on a microphone.
Anniversary party
A dinner with one song in it that matters more than the rest, the surprise the children throw, and the vow renewal that books ceremony music.
Retirement party
Forty years of colleagues and three speeches, thrown by the company or the family, with the standards as the music that reaches every age at the table.
Halloween party
A DJ, a party band or live band karaoke in costume, and Halloween 2026 on a Saturday.
Holiday party at home
The Christmas jazz duo or trio, the party band and the DJ, the December Saturdays the office parties take first, and Thanksgiving week and New Year’s Eve on the same page.
