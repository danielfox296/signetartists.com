Common party configurations

three common configurations of a private party.

Most requests we get all into one of the following categories.

The dinner party

A table of twelve, or three tables of eight. The music sits below conversational volume for the whole night: jazz standards on upright bass and guitar, at a level you can talk across. Book an hour on its own or keep things going from the time your guests arrie through dessert.

Common formats include jazz duo. Or a solo guitarist can hold down the same night where the guest list is a bit smaller.

The cocktail party that ends in dancing

When the furniture is going to end up against the wall, trio size is the answer: guitar, bass and drums fill a floor without a stage. A trio through drinks that becomes the band that drive danceable energy.

In situations where dancing is a "maybe" rather than the plan, an acoustic duo can cover the night well, too.

The milestone with a moment in it

Think - a fortieth birthday, a notable anniversary, or an engagement dinner. There is a toast (and sometimes a surprise), and the band plays around both: a dedicated announcement microphone can be ready to go from sound check on.
