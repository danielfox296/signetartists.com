Where

Denver, Boulder & Colorado Springs.

In Denver, there's no additional cost for travel inside the metro area, and the booking calendar is the only constraint. A lot of holiday gatherings in homes compete with office parties in the city for the same dates. Boulder parties can tend to skew smaller. Hosted in homes and clubs where the attendees sit close to the band, so solo and duo are the sizes to ask about first. Colorado Springs hosts more of its private calendar around wedding weekends and resort terraces, and the acoustic duo is the size parties in The Springs book most.

Castle Pines, Castle Rock and Evergreen are inside that no-travel-charge area too. So a party at a house in any of the three is quoted the same as Denver.
