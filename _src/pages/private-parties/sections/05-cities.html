Where

Denver, Boulder and the calendar they share.

In Denver, the card’s city column applies as printed, with no travel line inside the metro, and the calendar is the real constraint: a holiday gathering at home competes with every office party in the city for the same December Saturdays. Boulder parties skew smaller, homes and clubs where the crowd sits close to the band, so solo and duo are the sizes to price first, and a Front Range travel line is added to the quote.

If the date is in November or December, book on the corporate season’s clock: those dates are decided August through October, New Year’s Eve books earliest of all, and peak weekends carry their own line on the quote. A party at a mountain house runs on the card’s resort column, with the corridor’s logistics covered on the mountain page. An office event is its own set of pages: the holiday season, client dinners and retreats all live on the corporate side.
