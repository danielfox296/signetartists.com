Three sizes, and what each one does to a party.

Most hosts guess too big. The size that turns an evening is usually smaller than the one people imagine, and it is almost always the one that fits where the party already is.

A solo player

One voice and a guitar in the corner of a dinner. It makes the evening feel hosted rather than catered, and it fits a home without anyone moving furniture for it.

A duo

The working size. It carries arrival and dinner at a volume people talk across, and it has the range to lift the last hour if the party wants lifting.

A trio

This is the one that fills a floor without needing a stage. Guitar, bass and drums, and the furniture ends up against the wall.
