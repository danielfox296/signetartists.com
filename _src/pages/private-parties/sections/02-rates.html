What a private party runs.

A cover or acoustic band for a private party runs {{market:us_private_party_band}} in this market. A smaller build for the same music sits well under that: an acoustic duo over a four-hour evening runs {{market:us_duo}}, and a trio {{market:us_trio}}.

Three things decide where a specific evening lands. How many players the night actually needs, which is usually fewer than a host expects. How many hours the music covers. And whether the date is one the whole market wants, because a Saturday in October and a Wednesday in February are not the same job.

Sound sized to the venue is inside our figure at every size, which for a house party is the difference between a band that fits the space and one that overwhelms it. Travel outside the metro and peak dates are quoted as their own lines, the same way the contract prints them.

How a quote is put together is on the pricing page, and the Colorado market is set out figure by figure in the cost guide. Describe the evening and we will tell you which of the three sizes it actually wants, then price that.
