What YOU'RE BOOKING

Ensemble sizes, and how they impact the vibe.

In our experience, most hosts guess too big.

A solo player

One voice and a guitar or piano in the corner of a dinner makes the evening feel thoughtfully planned, and it fits well into a home without reconfiguring all the furniture.

A duo

A duo is a great intensity for both arrival and dinner - it's a volume people easily talk across, and it has the range to lift the last hour (if that's where things are headed.)

A trio

This is the one that gets people dancing without needing a stage. Usually guitar (or keys), bass and drums. More danceable energy - or it can be a fuller-sounding-but-still laid-back jazz ensemble.
