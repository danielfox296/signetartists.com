Ensemble sizes, and how they impact an event's vibe.

Most hosts guess too big. The size that turns an evening is usually smaller than the one people imagine, and it is almost always the one that fits where the party already is.

A solo player

One voice and usually a guitar or piano in the corner of a dinner. It makes the evening feel hosted rather than catered, and it fits a home without reconfiguring all the furniture.

A duo

A duo is a great intensity for both arrival and dinner - it's a volume people easilly talk across, and it has the range to lift the last hour if that's where things are headed.

A trio

This is the one that fills a floor without needing a stage. Usually guitar (or keys), bass and drums. More dancable energy or a fuller-sounding-but-still laid-back jazz ensemble.
