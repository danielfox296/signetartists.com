Booking it

The whole evening, or the floor after the program.

The band books for the full night or for the set after dinner, and pairs with a jazz duo or the solo guitarist through drinks on the same contract.

Sound support that’s the right size for the venue is included, and a singer in a ballroom is where that matters most: the voice has to reach the back tables without the band being loud at the front ones. When there are remarks in the evening, we plan the sets around them with you so the music stops before the emcee reaches the microphone. If the night runs long, another half hour is easy to add on the night.

Send the date, the venue and the headcount, and we’ll come back with a recommendation on configuration. What live music costs in this market.
