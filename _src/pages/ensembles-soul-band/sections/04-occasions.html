Occasions

Who books a soul and R&B band.

Office holiday parties
The dance floor act for a company party in a hotel ballroom, for a staff whose ages run from twenty-two to sixty-five. Office holiday party ideas puts this band beside the other ways a party can go.
Galas and award nights
A gala with a program in it, and a floor to open once the last award is given. The band plays that floor as the after-party or as the second half of the gala itself.
Wedding receptions
For a couple who wants a band on the floor, with their first dance learned for the night and the ceremony and cocktail hour on the same contract.
Milestone birthdays and anniversaries
A fiftieth or a sixtieth for a crowd that grew up on these records, at a club or a restaurant buyout, or an anniversary with the couple’s song in the set.
