Occasions

Common occasions for a soul and R&B band.

Office holiday parties
The dance floor act for a company party in a hotel ballroom, with a dinner set first and the floor after the program. The holiday party page carries the season’s dates and the ballroom logistics, and office holiday party entertainment ideas puts this band beside the other builds a party can take.
The floor after the last award
A gala has a program in it, and this is the band for the hour when the program is over and the tables empty onto the floor. The galas and award nights page covers the evening before that hour, and the after-party page covers the hour itself.
Wedding receptions
For the couple who wants a band on the floor rather than a playlist, with a first dance the band learns for the night. The weddings page walks through the ceremony and the cocktail hour that come before it, on the same contract.
Milestone birthdays
A fiftieth or a sixtieth for a crowd that grew up on these records, at a club or a restaurant buyout. Private parties covers the sizes and the venues, and the anniversary page the version with the couple’s song in it.
