The set

A sample of the set.

Think Signed, Sealed, Delivered, Respect, Let’s Stay Together and Lean on Me. The set runs from Sam Cooke and Otis Redding through Stevie Wonder, Aretha Franklin, Al Green, Bill Withers and Marvin Gaye to the Motown catalogue, with the newer songs that sit beside them, Amy Winehouse and Leon Bridges, for a crowd that spans three generations.

A set is built around the flow of your evening. Through dinner the singer takes the ballads at a volume the table talks across, and once the floor opens the horn comes out front and the songs get longer, because a soul chorus is built to be sung back. The songs above that are on the site’s own list are searchable on the repertoire page, and the first learned song is included in every booking. The same songwriters without a singer, groove first, is the funk band.
