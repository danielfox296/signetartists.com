The set

Respect, Let’s Stay Together, Lean on Me.

The set runs from Sam Cooke and Otis Redding through Stevie Wonder, Aretha Franklin, Al Green, Bill Withers, Marvin Gaye and the Motown catalog, with Amy Winehouse and Leon Bridges beside them.

The singer takes the ballads while people eat. Once the floor opens the horn comes out front and the songs stretch out, because a soul chorus is made to be sung back. For the same songwriters played without a singer, book the funk band. The rest of the covers list is on the repertoire page.
