Sizes

The quartet, and the full band with a horn.

The quartet
A singer, keys, bass and drums, with the keys covering the horn lines and the guitar parts. It’s the size for a company party of eighty to two hundred or a wedding reception in a restaurant.
Five to six piece
The quartet plus guitar and a horn, and a second voice for a ballroom or a crowd past two hundred. With the horn on the choruses and the guitar beside the keys, it’s the version that sounds most like the records.

A jazz duo or the solo guitarist can play the drinks hour before the band on the same contract, and the DJ can take over afterward through the same sound system.
