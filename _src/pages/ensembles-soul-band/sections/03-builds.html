Configurations

The quartet, and the full band with a horn.

The quartet
A singer, keys, bass and drums. The singer is the band here, and keys cover the horn lines and the guitar parts on the smaller build. It’s the size for a company party of eighty to two hundred or a wedding reception in a restaurant, and it takes 14 by 10 feet, two dedicated 20-amp circuits and a 90-minute load-in.
Five to six piece
The quartet plus guitar and a horn, and a second voice when the crowd is past two hundred or the venue is a ballroom. This is the version that sounds most like the records, with the horn taking the lines on the choruses and the guitar freeing the keys to play what keys played on those sessions.

Either configuration plays dinner at 65 to 72 dBA at the nearest table before the floor opens. An announcement microphone is ready for the remarks and the thank-yous, and the band goes silent on the emcee’s cue when there’s a program in the evening.

Footprints and power by size are on the technical page. When the night should keep going after the band, the DJ takes the same sound system on the same contract.
