Standards for cocktails and dinner

Jazz duo and trio for hire, for weddings and for events.

Guests walk in to Autumn Leaves on upright bass and guitar, pick up a drink, and talk across it all evening. The jazz duo plays standards at a volume the conversation sits on top of, for a single hour or for cocktails and dinner together, and the trio adds drums played with brushes for a bigger crowd.

Weddings book it for the ceremony and the cocktail hour, and companies book it for client dinners and receptions. Standards are the book because guests recognize them without needing to name them.
