Jazz duo and trio

Jazz duo and trio for hire, for weddings and for events.

The duo is the classic cocktail-and-dinner booking: upright bass and guitar playing standards at a volume you can talk across, for a single hour or for cocktails and dinner together. The trio adds drums, played with brushes, for larger floors. Weddings hire it for the ceremony and the cocktail hour, and companies hire it for dinners and receptions.

Arrival, cocktails and dinner are this format's regular work: the hours when the music has to hold the event without anybody raising their voice over it. Standards are the repertoire because guests recognize them without needing to name them, and the whole night feels polished and unhurried.
