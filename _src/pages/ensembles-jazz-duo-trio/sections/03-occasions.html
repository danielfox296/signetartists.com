Where it lands

The front of the night, wherever it happens.

The duo and trio play the stretch of an event where conversation is the point. These are the occasions that book it, and each links to the page that prices the whole night around it.

Client dinners and member events
Twenty at one table or eighty at ten, and the conversation is the event, so the music sits under it for the whole night. The client dinners page covers the corporate side: net-30 on approval, a certificate of insurance with the contract.
Corporate evenings with a program
Dinner at conversation volume around remarks and awards, music up to the moment and silence through it, rehearsed against the run of show. The corporate page sets out the three shapes an enquiry usually takes.
Wedding cocktail hours
The hour between ceremony and dinner is this format's home ground. The cocktail hour page answers that hour's questions: how long it runs, what should play, duo or DJ.
Dinner parties at home or at a club
The size that fits a house or a club without rearranging it. Private parties and dinners carries the shapes and prices, and the Denver page covers the city's venues and logistics.
