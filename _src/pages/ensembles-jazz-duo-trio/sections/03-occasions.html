Occasions

Who books a jazz duo or trio.

Client dinners and member events
Twenty at one table or eighty at ten, where the conversation is the event and the duo plays under it all night.
Company evenings with a program
Dinner around remarks and awards, with the music stopping for the speeches and starting again after them, planned with your run of show. The corporate jazz band page goes through a company evening part by part.
Wedding cocktail hours
The hour between the ceremony and dinner, and the ceremony itself if you want it. For jazz from the processional to the last dance, the jazz band for a wedding sizes the whole day.
Dinner parties at home or at a club
A duo fits a house or a club without moving the furniture, in Denver or anywhere on the Front Range.
Senior living
The standards the residents grew up with, played in the common area on a standing weekday afternoon.
Sunday brunch
The duo under a restaurant’s or a hotel’s Sunday brunch, from the first table to the last.
Memorial receptions
The reception or the celebration of life after a service, with the songs the family chooses.

The acoustic duo is the same size playing singer-songwriter and folk-rock covers, and in December this trio plays the season’s book as the Christmas jazz trio. What a jazz band costs in Denver sets out the market, size by size.
