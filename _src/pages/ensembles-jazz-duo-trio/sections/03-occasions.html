Occasions

Common occasions for a jazz duo or trio.

The duo and trio play the stretch of an event where conversation is the point.

Client dinners and member events
Twenty at one table or eighty at ten, and the conversation is the event, so the music sits under it for the whole night. The client dinners page covers the corporate details.
Corporate evenings with a program
Dinner at conversation volume around remarks and awards, music up to the moment and silence through it, planned with the run of show. The corporate page sets out the common configurations.
Wedding cocktail hours
The hour between ceremony and dinner is where this format fits best. The cocktail hour page covers how long it runs, what should play, and duo or DJ.
Dinner parties at home or at a club
The size that fits a house or a club without rearranging it. Private parties and dinners covers the configurations, and the Denver page covers the city's venues and logistics.
