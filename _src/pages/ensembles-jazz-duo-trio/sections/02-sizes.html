Two sizes

Two sizes, the duo and the trio.

Dinner and cocktail sets run 65 to 72 dBA at the nearest table, a level two people who just met can easily talk across, and two musicians can't get loud by accident.

The duo
Upright bass and guitar, for cocktails and dinner together or a single hour on its own. It sets up in an 8 by 6 ft corner on one dedicated circuit, which is why it fits venues a band can't.
The trio
The duo plus drums, played with brushes. Brushes keep the kit at dinner level while giving a larger floor and a longer guest list the pulse a two-piece can't. The footprint grows to 12 by 8 ft, still on a single circuit.

Both sizes are booked as a format: an instrumentation, a repertoire and a volume, and the musicians we send deliver all three.

Footprints, power and load-in by size are on the technical page. If the night should end on a dance floor, a duo that grows to a quartet is the act for that. If you want a jazz trio with a name on it, Changes Jazz Trio plays the standards with a modern edge under its own three names.
