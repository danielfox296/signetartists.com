Sizes

The duo, or the trio with brushes.

The duo
Upright bass and guitar, for cocktails and dinner together or a single hour on its own. It fits in a corner, so it plays venues a band can’t.
The trio
The duo plus drums, played with brushes. Brushes keep the drums at dinner level and give a bigger crowd the pulse a two-piece can’t.

For a night that ends on a dance floor, Tejas Singh starts as a duo and grows to a quartet. For a jazz trio with names on it, Changes Jazz Trio plays the standards with a modern edge.
