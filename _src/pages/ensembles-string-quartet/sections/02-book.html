The book

Canon in D for the aisle, Viva La Vida for the drinks.

The book runs from the classical processionals and preludes through the hymns to the love songs, pop and film themes couples ask for. Every title here is arranged for strings, one violin to four, and a song that isn’t here can be arranged for your date.
