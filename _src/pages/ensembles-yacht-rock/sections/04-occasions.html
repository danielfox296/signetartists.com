Client appreciation evenings
A thank-you to a client list on a terrace, for the evening that should end on a dance floor.
After-parties and galas
The floor once a program is over, as the after-party or as the second half of a gala or holiday party.
Milestone birthdays
A fortieth or a fiftieth for a crowd that owned these records, at the house, a club or a rented space.
Weddings
A reception where the couple wants everyone singing on the dance floor, with the ceremony and the cocktail hour on the same contract.
