Booking it

A whole evening, or the half of it that dances.

The band books for the full night or for the set after dinner, and pairs with a duo or a solo pianist through drinks on the same contract.

Sound sized to the venue is included, which on a patio matters more than it sounds: it is the difference between a band filling a lawn and a band lost in it. There is no overtime rate; another half hour on the night costs what the earlier hours did.

What live music costs in this market is on the pricing page. Send the date, the venue and the headcount, and we will tell you which build the night needs.
