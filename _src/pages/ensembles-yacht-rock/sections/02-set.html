The set

What a Fool Believes, Reelin’ in the Years, Rich Girl.

The smooth end of 1976 to 1984: the Doobie Brothers, Steely Dan, Hall and Oates, Toto, Christopher Cross, Boz Scaggs, Kenny Loggins, Ambrosia, Player, Michael McDonald on his own, and the Fleetwood Mac ballads that sit beside them.

The ballads play while people eat, and the up-tempo half of the book opens the floor. The rest of the covers list is on the repertoire page.
