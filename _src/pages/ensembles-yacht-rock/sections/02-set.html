The set

What a Fool Believes, Reelin’ in the Years, Rich Girl.

The book is the smooth end of 1976 to 1984: Doobie Brothers, Steely Dan, Hall and Oates, Toto, Christopher Cross, Boz Scaggs, Kenny Loggins, Ambrosia, Player, Michael McDonald on his own, and the Fleetwood Mac ballads that sit beside them. Sung in three-part harmony, with the saxophone taking the solos the records gave it.

A set is built against your run of show and read on the night. A dinner set sits at conversation volume and leans on the ballads; the floor set is the one everybody came for. The band’s wider covers list is searchable on the repertoire page, and the first learned song beyond it is included in every booking.
