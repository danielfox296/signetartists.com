Sizes

The four-piece, or the full band with saxophone.

The four-piece
Keys, guitar, bass and drums, three of them singing. The harmonies are what make it yacht rock, so every size carries them.
Five to six piece
The four-piece plus saxophone, with a lead voice of its own for a ballroom or a crowd past two hundred. It’s the version that sounds most like the records.

An acoustic duo or a solo pianist can play the drinks hour before the band on the same contract, and the DJ can take over afterward through the same sound system.
