The builds

Four voices and instruments, or the full band with the horn.

The four-piece
Keys, guitar, bass and drums, three of them singing. The harmonies are the act, so every build carries them. 14 by 10 feet, two dedicated 20-amp circuits, a 90-minute load-in.
Five to six piece
The four-piece plus saxophone, and a dedicated lead voice when the crowd is past two hundred or the venue is a ballroom. This is the version the records sound like.

Either build plays dinner at 65 to 72 dBA at the nearest table before the night turns, and outdoors it plays on a covered, level and dry performance area with power run to it, with the move-indoors call written before the day.

Footprints and power by size are on the technical page. When the night should keep going after the band, the DJ takes the same sound system on the same contract.
