Configurations

Two configurations, the four-piece and the full band with saxophone.

The four-piece
Keys, guitar, bass and drums, three of them singing. The harmonies are what make it yacht rock, so every configuration carries them. 14 by 10 feet, two dedicated 20-amp circuits, a 90-minute load-in.
Five to six piece
The four-piece plus saxophone, and a dedicated lead voice when the crowd is past two hundred or the venue is a ballroom. This is the version that sounds most like the records.

Either configuration plays dinner at 65 to 72 dBA at the nearest table before the night turns. Outdoors it plays on a covered, level and dry performance area with power run to it, and we agree a move-indoors plan with you ahead of time.

Footprints and power by size are on the technical page. When the night should keep going after the band, the DJ takes the same sound system on the same contract.
