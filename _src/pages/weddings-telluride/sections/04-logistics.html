The far resort

Six hours, a night in town, and 9,545 feet.

Telluride is a far resort from Denver, about six hours each way over the passes, and a band that has driven six hours stays the night. So the quote carries three lines: the fee for the music, one travel line for the drive, and a night’s lodging at cost, meaning the receipt with no markup on it. A winter date is planned with the passes in mind, and the musicians travel a day early when the forecast says so.

The town allows amplified sound under a permit within set hours on Fridays, Saturdays and holidays, and that is the clock the floor is planned to; a ceremony guitarist and a cocktail hour duo sit inside it on their own. The rule is cited and dated on our mountain corridor page, and we confirm the current version with the venue before the day.

Telluride sits at 8,750 feet and Mountain Village at about 9,545. The air is thin and dry, strings drift between an afternoon ceremony and an evening set and get retuned, and anyone singing or playing a horn paces a set differently, so the sound check is longer here than anywhere else we play.
