The venues

On the mountain, in Mountain Village, and in town.

The mountain holds the ceremony people fly in for. San Sophia Overlook is reached by gondola and looks straight down the canyon at the town, Gorrono Ranch is the on-mountain site for a ceremony and a reception in one place, and Allred’s sits at the top of the gondola with the peaks on one side and the town’s lights on the other once it’s dark. A guitarist and a battery-powered system ride up to any of them; a band plays the ones with power and a floor.

Mountain Village holds the terrace hour and the lodge reception. The Madeline’s terraces take a duo while the light is on the peaks, Mountain Lodge Telluride takes a duo through dinner and a trio for the floor, and both are a gondola ride from a ceremony above them and from the town below, so the day moves without a car.

Town holds the wedding that wants Telluride itself around it. The Sheridan Opera House has a stage and a floor and a hundred years of shows in it, and a band on that stage is the reception the couple remembers, and Town Park, at the box canyon end of Colorado Avenue, takes a tent, a band and the town’s amplified-sound permit.
