Telluride weddings

Live music for a Telluride wedding.

A Telluride wedding is the one guests fly in for. The ceremony is up the gondola with the whole box canyon below it, the cocktail hour is on a Mountain Village terrace at nine and a half thousand feet, and the floor fills a lodge, an opera house or a tent in Town Park inside the hours the town allows for amplified sound. So the music is a guitarist who rides up with the guests, a duo for the terrace before the cold comes down, and a band or the DJ for the dancing with the last song planned to the town’s clock, on a quote that carries the drive and the night in town honestly.

This is the Telluride version of our weddings page. Book one part of the day or all of it on one contract, with sound support that’s the right size for the space, a personal contact through the day, one travel line for the drive, and a night’s lodging at cost.
