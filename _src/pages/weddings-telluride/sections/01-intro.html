Telluride weddings

Live music for a Telluride wedding.

A Telluride wedding is the one guests fly in for. The ceremony is up the gondola with the whole box canyon below it, the cocktail hour is on a Mountain Village terrace at nine and a half thousand feet, and the floor fills a lodge, an opera house or a tent in Town Park inside the hours the town allows for amplified sound. So the music is a guitarist who rides up with the guests, a duo for the terrace before the cold comes down, and a band or the DJ for the dancing, with the last song planned to the town’s clock.

It’s one of our weddings bookings: one part of the day or all of it, on one contract with a personal contact through the day, one travel line for the drive and a night’s lodging at cost.
