The setting

About 6,035 feet, and the wind off the range.

The city sits at about 6,035 feet, high enough that guests from sea level feel it and low enough that it changes nothing about how musicians pace a set. What the Springs asks for is a plan for the afternoon. Wind comes off the front of the range and drops as the sun does, and an evening on a terrace can lose fifteen degrees between the first drink and the toast, so outdoor bookings here get a move-indoors call written down before the day.
