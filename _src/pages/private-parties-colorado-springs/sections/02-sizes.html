The size

Why the duo is the Springs answer.

Two players read as live music from anywhere in the venue and still fit an 8 by 6 foot corner on a single dedicated circuit. In a city whose private events happen on terraces, in club dining spaces and in houses with a view, that combination is most of the job.

A solo player

One voice and a guitar, in 6 by 4 feet. It suits a dinner for twelve and an outdoor afternoon where the music is there to make the arrival feel hosted rather than to carry the night.

An acoustic duo

The size this city books most. Voice and guitar for songs guests sing along to, or guitar and upright bass when the set should stay instrumental through dinner. Both hold 65 to 72 dBA at the nearest table.

A trio

The one that fills a floor without a stage, in 12 by 8 feet. Book it when the evening has dancing in the plan rather than as a possibility.
