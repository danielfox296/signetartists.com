The sizes

Ensemble sizes for a Colorado Springs party.

Two musicians come across as live music from anywhere in the venue and still fit an 8 by 6 foot corner on a single dedicated circuit. In a city whose private events happen on terraces, in club dining spaces and in houses with a view, that combination is most of the job.

A solo player

One voice and a guitar, in 6 by 4 feet. It suits a dinner for twelve and an outdoor afternoon where the music is there to make the arrival feel hosted.

An acoustic duo

This is the size this city books most. Voice and guitar for songs guests sing along to, or guitar and upright bass when the set should stay instrumental through dinner. Both hold 65 to 72 dBA at the nearest table.

A trio

This is the one that fills a dance floor without a stage, in 12 by 8 feet, for an evening with dancing in the plan.

What the format plays, and the difference between the acoustic set and a jazz set, is on the acoustic duo and trio page. What live music costs across this market is set out on the pricing page.
