The sizes

Ensemble sizes for a Colorado Springs party.

Two musicians sound like live music from anywhere in the venue and still fit in a corner. In a city whose private events happen on terraces, in club dining spaces and in houses with a view, that combination is most of the job.

A solo player

One voice and a guitar. It suits a dinner for twelve and an outdoor afternoon where the music is there to make the arrival feel hosted.

An acoustic duo

The size this city books most. Voice and guitar for songs guests sing along to, or guitar and upright bass when the set should stay instrumental through dinner.

A trio

The one that fills a dance floor without a stage, for an evening with dancing in the plan.

What the format plays, and the difference between the acoustic set and a jazz set, is on the acoustic duo and trio page.
