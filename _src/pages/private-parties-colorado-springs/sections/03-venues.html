The venues

Resorts, clubs and the wedding weekend around them.

The city's private celebrations concentrate in a short list of places. Garden of the Gods Resort and Club and Cheyenne Mountain Resort hold the family gatherings and the milestone weekends, with terraces built for the hour before dinner. The Broadmoor runs its own calendar at its own scale. Downtown, The Mining Exchange and The Antlers take the celebrations that want to be walked to from a hotel lobby rather than driven to.

More of this calendar is wedding-shaped here than in Denver, and most of a wedding weekend is not the wedding. The welcome party on the Friday, the rehearsal dinner, the brunch on the Sunday: those are private parties in everything but name, and they are the bookings a duo was built for. Where the hour after the ceremony is the one you are planning, the cocktail hour page answers that hour on its own terms.

At home the shape is the same and the venue is easier. A house on the west side with the front of the range in the windows takes a duo in a corner and needs nothing built. Tell us the address and which door the gear comes through, and the load-in is planned before the day rather than on it.
