For Planners

You are not going to have to manage us.

Most of what decides whether a band is easy to work with has nothing to do with the music. This page is the part that does.

A named contact, given to you in advance

Included in every booking, never an add-on. One person, named before the event and reachable throughout it — usually one of the players, most often the bandleader. You get their number with the contract, not at load-in, and you are never hunting for whoever looks in charge.

Paperwork before you ask for it

Certificate of insurance naming the venue, W-9, and the contract, sent together. You should never be chasing a COI the week of an event, and you should never find out at load-in that one doesn’t exist.

A reply inside 24 to 48 hours

Business hours, every time, including the questions that are annoying to answer. If we are going to be the easy vendor, that starts before there is a booking rather than after.

The number quoted is the number contracted

Rates are published. Travel, peak dates and additional hours are their own lines. Nothing appears later that wasn’t on the quote.
