Unbundling is normal

If you want your own ceremony musician, or a marquee DJ you already trust, and us only for dancing — that is a booking we want, and it should not feel like a compromise. We would rather be one good part of a night you assembled than the reason you had to argue with a vendor.

Where it does help to use us across the night, the reason is arithmetic rather than loyalty. Once we are in the building the setup is paid for, so an hour of cocktail duo added to a full band booking is $450, and an hour of DJ after the band is $350. Two vendors cannot reach those numbers.
