Occasions

Who books a solo guitarist.

Cocktail hours
The hour between the ceremony and dinner, for a shorter guest list or a second space the crowd passes through.
Client dinners
A dinner where the conversation is the event and the guitar sits under it all night.
Private dinners at home
A dining table’s evening, in Denver or anywhere on the Front Range, with the house left as it is.
Company receptions
The arrival hour before a program starts, or background music through a reception.
Funerals and memorials
The prelude and the one song at a service, and the graveside after it on a battery-powered system.
Senior living
An afternoon in a memory care neighborhood, played with no amplification at all.
