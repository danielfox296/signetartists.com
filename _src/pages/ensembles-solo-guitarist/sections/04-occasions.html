Where it lands

The occasions one guitarist covers.

A solo is the right size when the music's whole job is to sit under the event. These are the bookings it takes, and each links to the page that prices the night around it.

Cocktail hours
Sixty to ninety minutes between ceremony and dinner, sized for a shorter guest list or a second space the crowd passes through. The cocktail hour page covers the hour itself.
Client dinners
Where the conversation is the event and the music's job is to sit under it all night. The client dinners page covers the corporate paperwork: net-30 on approval, vendor onboarding, a certificate of insurance with the contract.
Private dinners at home
The size that fits a dining table's evening without rearranging the house. Private parties and dinners prices the shapes, and the Denver page covers the city's venues and parking.
Corporate receptions
An arrival set before a program starts, or background through a reception. The corporate page sets out how music sits around remarks and awards.

Where a solo sits against every other size, figure by figure, is in the cost guide.
