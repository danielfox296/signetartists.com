Occasions

Common occasions for a solo guitarist.

A solo is the right size when the music's whole job is to sit under the event.

Cocktail hours
Sixty to ninety minutes between ceremony and dinner, sized for a shorter guest list or a second space the crowd passes through. The cocktail hour page covers the hour itself.
Client dinners
Where the conversation is the event and the music's job is to sit under it all night. The client dinners page covers the corporate details.
Private dinners at home
The size that fits a dining table's evening without rearranging the house. Private parties and dinners covers the configurations, and the Denver page covers the city's venues and parking.
Corporate receptions
An arrival set before a program starts, or background through a reception. The corporate page sets out how music sits around remarks and awards.
Funerals, memorials and senior living afternoons
One guitar plays the prelude and the one song at a service, moves to the graveside on a battery-powered system, and plays a memory care neighborhood’s afternoon without amplification. The funerals and memorials page and the senior living page cover both.
