The set

One voice and a guitar, or the guitar alone.

There are two ways to book it. Sung, it's one voice and a guitar working blues, roots and the singer-songwriter catalog. Instrumental, it's guitar on its own: jazz standards and Spanish-tinged sets that hold a background without asking for attention. The nylon-string version, a Spanish guitarist, has a page of its own.

The choice usually follows the occasion. Dinner conversation sits easiest over instrumental guitar, and a cocktail hour or a patio takes a voice well. Either way the set runs at 65 to 72 dBA at the nearest table, the same dinner level every size on this roster plays, and one musician can't drift above it by accident.

It's booked as a format: an instrumentation, a repertoire and a price, and the contract names all three. The covers list the roster works from is published and searchable on the repertoire page, and the first learned song you ask for is included in every booking.
