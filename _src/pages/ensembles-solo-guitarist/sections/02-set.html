The set

One voice and a guitar, or the guitar alone.

Two ways to run it. Sung, it is one voice and a guitar working blues, roots and the singer-songwriter catalog. Instrumental, it is guitar on its own: jazz standards and Spanish-tinged sets that hold a background without asking for attention.

The choice usually follows the occasion. Dinner conversation sits easiest over instrumental guitar, and a cocktail hour or a patio takes a voice well. Either way the set runs at 65 to 72 dBA at the nearest table, the same dinner level every size on this roster plays, and one player cannot drift above it by accident.

It is sold as a format: an instrumentation, a repertoire and a price, guaranteed on any date it is available. The covers list the roster works from is published and searchable on the repertoire page, and the first learned song you ask for is included in every booking.
