The set

One voice and a guitar, or the guitar alone.

Sung, it’s one voice and a guitar working blues, roots and the singer-songwriter catalog, and a cocktail hour or a patio takes a voice well. Instrumental, it’s jazz standards and Spanish-tinged sets, which sit easiest under dinner conversation. The nylon-string version is the Spanish guitarist, and the sung version has its own page as the singer and guitarist.

One musician with a small sound system fits where a band can’t: a private dining space at a restaurant, a gallery evening set between the works, a house where nobody wants the furniture moved. When the guest list outgrows one instrument, the acoustic duo and the jazz duo are the next sizes up. The covers list is on the repertoire page.
