The footprint

Footprint, power and load-in.

A solo setup takes 6 by 4 feet, one dedicated 20-amp circuit, a 45-minute load-in and a 30-minute strike. That's all it needs, and it's why this booking works in venues that couldn't fit a trio.

A private dining space at a restaurant, a gallery evening set between the works, a house with furniture nobody wants moved, a client dinner on the top floor of an office: these are venues where the first question is what fits, and one musician with a small PA fits. Power is one circuit, and the requirement that it be dedicated, shared with nothing from catering, is the single detail worth checking with the venue early.

Footprints, inputs and circuits for every size are published on the technical page, written so you can hand your venue the link.
