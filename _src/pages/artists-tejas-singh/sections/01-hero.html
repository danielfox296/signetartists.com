Covers, duo to quartet.

Tejas Singh builds a song you know from the ground up, one looped layer of guitar at a time, until the whole record is playing under his voice. He starts the night as a duo under dinner, the rhythm section joins, and by the end the same voice is fronting a quartet on the dance floor, all on one contract.
