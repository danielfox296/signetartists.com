Where it lands

Four nights, one voice, sized to each.

Covers are the working repertoire of a private event. The four nights below each ask for a different size of the same act, and each one carries a full page of its own.

Office holiday parties
The quartet is the dance floor booking of the season. It plays dinner at 65 to 72 dBA at the nearest table, then opens the floor at 88 to 95 once the program ends. The November and December dates are published on the season page, and Denver carries the downtown calendar in detail.
Client evenings and corporate dinners
At duo size the same songs sit under conversation instead of over it, the register a client dinner or a member evening asks for. An hour on its own is a real booking rather than a minimum to work around, and the corporate page covers the paperwork side, purchase orders and net-30 included.
Milestones and private parties
A milestone birthday or an anniversary usually wants both halves of the night: dinner guests can talk through, then a floor. One booking covers both without a second act, and the trio is the size for a party that wants a floor without a full band.
Wedding cocktail hours
The duo is the pick when the hour should have a voice in it rather than an instrumental set. It covers arrival and cocktails at conversation volume, and if the reception wants a floor later, the same act grows instead of handing off.

How the night is paced around speeches and a program is run of show work, written before the day. Where the ladder's figures sit in the wider Colorado market is set out in the cost guide.
