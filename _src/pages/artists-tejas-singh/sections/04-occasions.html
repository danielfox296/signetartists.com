Occasions

Common occasions for Tejas Singh.

Each of these occasions asks for a different size of the same act, and each one has a full page of its own.

Office holiday parties
The quartet is the dance floor size for a holiday party. It plays dinner at 65 to 72 dBA at the nearest table, then opens the floor at 88 to 95 once the program ends. The season’s dates are on the holiday party page, and Denver carries the downtown season in detail.
Client evenings and corporate dinners
At duo size the same songs sit under the conversation, which is what a client dinner or a member evening asks for. An hour on its own is a real booking, and the corporate page covers the corporate details.
Milestones and private parties
A milestone birthday or an anniversary usually wants both halves of the night: dinner guests can talk through, then a floor. One booking covers both without a second act, and the trio is the size for a party that wants a floor without a full band.
Wedding cocktail hours
The duo is a great pick when you want a voice in the hour. It covers arrival and cocktails at conversation volume, and if the reception wants a dance floor later, the same act grows into it.

How the music sits around speeches and a program is planned with the run of show. What live music costs across Colorado, size by size, is set out in the cost guide.
