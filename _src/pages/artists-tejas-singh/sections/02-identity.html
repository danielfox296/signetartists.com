One act, three sizes. This is the only booking on the roster that changes shape during the night instead of being replaced.

The reason to care is the contract. Most events buy the front of the night and the back of the night from two different acts, which means two load-ins, two sound checks, two sets of names to keep straight and a seam in the middle that guests can hear. This booking ladders instead: a duo through arrival and dinner, then the rhythm section arrives and the same voice fronts the dance floor. The party sees one band getting bigger.

At quartet it stops being a cocktail act. The full rhythm section behind one voice, keys or a second guitar on top, playing a covers set built for a floor, and it is the size to book when filling the floor is the actual objective rather than a hope.

It is a premium cover band. It is not a show band. Nobody changes costume, there is no choreography and there is no host segment. The band plays, reads the floor, and plays the next thing.

This is a named act, published under the name of the person who fronts it, and any substitution in the lineup is named to you in advance.
