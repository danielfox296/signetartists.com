One act, three sizes. It’s the one booking on the roster that grows during the night instead of handing off to another act.

A night that wants dinner music and a dance floor often books two different acts, which means two load-ins, two sound checks and a seam in the middle that guests can hear. This booking grows instead: a duo through arrival and dinner, then the rhythm section arrives and the same voice fronts the dance floor. The party sees one band getting bigger.

At quartet size it’s a dance band: the full rhythm section behind one voice, keys or a second guitar on top, playing a covers set built for a floor. It’s the size to book when filling the floor is the goal.

It’s a premium cover band, and it isn’t a show band with costume changes, choreography or a host segment. The band plays, reads the floor and plays the next song.

This is a named act, booked under the name of the person who fronts it, and if anyone in the lineup changes, we tell you in advance.
