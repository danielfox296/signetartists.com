René Moffatt writes the songs he sings. He’s a folk and Americana songwriter based just outside Denver, with six records behind him, King of the Mountain and Here and Now Is Home among them, and an NPR Tiny Desk Contest entry every year since 2019. He calls it heady folk for the open-hearted, and the company he keeps on a playlist is Josh Ritter, Jeff Tweedy and The Avett Brothers.

Solo, it’s a voice and an acoustic guitar. It plays under welcome drinks and on a patio at a volume people talk across, and it’s the right size for a dinner party or a retreat evening where the songs are the point of the night. He’s played the listening venues around Denver, Swallow Hill and the Black Rose Acoustic Society among them, and stages at the Denver Botanic Gardens and the Clyfford Still Museum.

With the band, René Moffatt & The Moonshadows, the same songs get lead guitar, bass and drums under them. The duo is his voice and guitar with a lead guitar beside it, the trio adds bass, and the four piece adds drums, which is the band you can watch on a Denver club stage and at the Underground Music Showcase in the footage below. They played the Boulder Roots Music Fest as well, and that size is the one for a park stage or a summer party.

His own songs lead a set. He plays covers beside them when a night wants them, and the ones on his channel run from Willie Nelson to The Beatles and The Cars, so tell us the balance you want and he builds the set to it.

This is a named act, booked under the name of the person who fronts it, and if anyone in the lineup changes, we tell you in advance. He joined the roster under his own name in September 2026.
