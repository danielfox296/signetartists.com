He’s a folk and Americana songwriter based just outside Denver, with six records behind him, King of the Mountain and Here and Now Is Home among them, and an NPR Tiny Desk Contest entry every year since 2019. He calls it heady folk for the open-hearted. He joined the roster under his own name in September 2026.

Solo, he’s played the listening venues around Denver, Swallow Hill and the Black Rose Acoustic Society among them, and stages at the Denver Botanic Gardens and the Clyfford Still Museum. With the four piece, the same songs get lead guitar, bass and drums under them, which is the band you can watch on a Denver club stage and at the Underground Music Showcase in the footage below, and the band that played the Boulder Roots Music Fest.
