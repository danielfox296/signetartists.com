A song nobody at the table has heard before, sung over an acoustic guitar, and by the second chorus the conversation has stopped to listen. René Moffatt writes his own folk and Americana songs, the kind that sit on a playlist beside Josh Ritter and The Avett Brothers. He plays solo for welcome drinks, a patio or a dinner where people listen, brings his band, René Moffatt & The Moonshadows, when the night wants one, and plays covers beside his own songs when you ask.
