Original singer-songwriter

René Moffatt

A named act. His own songs and covers, solo to four piece.

René Moffatt writes his own songs and sings them with an acoustic guitar. It’s folk and Americana that sits on a playlist beside Josh Ritter and The Avett Brothers. He plays solo for welcome drinks, a patio or a dinner where people listen, and brings his band, René Moffatt & The Moonshadows, when the night wants one. He’ll play covers alongside his own songs if you ask for them.
