Occasions

Common occasions for René Moffatt.

A songwriter suits the parts of an event where people are close enough to hear the words. Each of these wants a different size of the same act, and each one has a full page of its own.

Wedding ceremonies and the hour after
Solo voice and acoustic guitar plays the ceremony, then the cocktail hour at a volume the receiving line talks across. Couples who want the music to sound like one person singing to the people in front of him book this size, and the hour can be his own songs, covers, or both.
Retreat evenings in the mountains
The evening after a day of sessions, on a deck or around a fire, is where a songwriter belongs. Solo or the trio plays it at a size that lets people keep talking and gives them something worth listening to when they stop.
Dinner parties and house concerts
A dinner for twenty or a house concert for fifty is the booking where his own songs get the whole crowd’s attention. Solo is the size indoors, and the trio fits a backyard once the party moves outside.
Farmers markets, libraries and summer series
He’s a featured artist of the Denver Public Library’s Volume Denver program, so a library afternoon or a market morning is a natural solo booking, and the band plays the park stage a city’s summer series books. Send us the call for performers and we answer it with the size that fits the stage.

How the music sits around speeches and a program is planned with the run of show. What live music costs across Colorado, size by size, is set out in the cost guide.
