Corporate retreats

An offsite is judged by its evenings.

Nobody flies a team to Vail to remember the breakout sessions. They remember the last night: the bar in the lodge, the snow outside, a band playing and a group of colleagues behaving like people who like each other. That is the night worth building, and it is the one this page is about.

Three evenings usually need covering: the welcome dinner, the program night and the last-night party, and they want different music. One contract covers all three. It is the mountain branch of our corporate work, and because the corridor is genuinely harder than a Denver ballroom, the altitude, power and curfew detail resorts actually enforce is set out below.
