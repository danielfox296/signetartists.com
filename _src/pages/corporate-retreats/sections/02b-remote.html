Meeting in person

Offsites for remote and hybrid companies.

The people organising company offsites now mostly work remote or hybrid themselves, {{market:offsites_remote_hybrid_share}} of them in the most recent industry report, and {{market:offsites_essential_remote}} of fully remote companies call the offsite essential to the business. Most gather once or twice a year for a few weekdays, and Colorado is where a lot of them do it, because it has a hub airport a distributed team can reach without connecting and a mountain evening within two hours of it.

A remote team’s offsite has a different first night from any other corporate booking. People who know each other’s faces from a video grid are in one place for the first time, and the first ten minutes decide whether the week feels like a reunion or a conference. A duo at conversation volume through the arrival dinner gives colleagues who’ve never met a reason to stand in the same corner. The last night is the one where the whole company dances, and the working days in between belong to the venue and the outfitters.
