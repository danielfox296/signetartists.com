The three nights a retreat books

The welcome dinner
The group has been travelling all day and the first night's job is conversation. A duo plays it at 65 to 72 dBA at the nearest table, the level people talk across, so colleagues who flew in from three offices can actually meet.
The program night
Awards, remarks, the number for the year. Music runs up to the moment and stops while the speaker is still steps from the microphone, with a dedicated announcement mic live on its own channel from sound check on.
The last night
The one the group retells. A quartet fills a floor at 88 to 95 dBA, inside whatever cap and curfew the resort sets, and where the retreat wants a named act in front, Tejas Singh leads the roster.

Each night is its own line on one contract. And once we are in the building for a date, the call-out is already paid, so a second set the same evening bills at the hourly rate only. Retreat dates book on shorter windows than the holiday season, and any date gets a straight yes or no by email. When the evening is a dinner in town rather than a retreat, client dinners has a page of its own.
