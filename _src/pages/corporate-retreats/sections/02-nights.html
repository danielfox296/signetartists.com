The three nights a retreat books

The welcome dinner
The group has been travelling all day and the first night's job is conversation. A duo plays it at 65 to 72 dBA at the nearest table, the level people talk across, so colleagues who flew in from three offices can actually meet.
The program night
Awards, remarks, the number for the year. Music runs up to the moment and stops while the speaker is still steps from the microphone, and the speaker gets an announcement microphone of their own.
The last night
The loud one. A quartet fills a dance floor at 88 to 95 dBA, inside whatever cap and curfew the resort sets, and where the retreat wants a named act in front, Tejas Singh leads the roster.

Each night is its own line on one contract, and a second set on the same evening usually costs less than a second booking would. Retreat dates book on shorter windows than the holiday season. When the evening is a dinner in town rather than a retreat, client dinners has a page of its own.

Daytime activities usually plan themselves, and the evenings are what a group remembers. Eight of them, with the size each one needs, are set out in corporate retreat evening ideas in Vail.
