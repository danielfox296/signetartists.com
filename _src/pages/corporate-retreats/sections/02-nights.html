The three nights a retreat books

The welcome dinner
The group has been traveling all day and the first night’s job is conversation. A duo plays it at a level people talk across, so colleagues who flew in from three offices can actually meet.
The program night
The night with the awards, the remarks and the number for the year. Music runs up to the moment and stops while the speaker is still steps from the microphone.
The last night
The loud one. A quartet fills a dance floor inside whatever limit and curfew the resort sets, and where the retreat wants a named act in front, Tejas Singh leads the roster.

Each night is its own line on one contract, and retreat dates get booked on shorter notice than holiday-season dates. Eight evenings, with the size each one needs, are in corporate retreat evening ideas in Vail; a dinner in town has its own page as client dinners.

Mornings and firesides have their own acts. Firstwater is a seventy-five-minute sound bath with the whole group lying on the floor, placed to open a morning or close an evening, and René Moffatt plays his own folk and Americana songs on a deck or by a fire after dinner.
