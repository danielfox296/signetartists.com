The resort towns

Four resort towns, each with its own page.

Each retreat market in the corridor runs on its own logistics, so each carries its own page: the venues that market books, the travel line that applies, and the questions specific to the town.

Vail
The corridor's incentive-event center, with venues like the Sonnenalp and the Arrabelle inside the villages and Donovan Pavilion at the edge of them. I-70 travel and Eagle County load-in patterns are on the Vail page.
Aspen
The far-resort booking, where dinners at the Little Nell and the Hotel Jerome come with the far travel line and a night's lodging at cost. The detail is on the Aspen page.
Beaver Creek
Venues up the mountain, Beano's Cabin among them, sit above village hotels like the Park Hyatt, and load-ins run through a gated village. The Beaver Creek page covers the access windows that shape an evening there.
Breckenridge
The highest of the four towns at roughly 9,600 feet, with a historic district doing the venue work that hotels do elsewhere. Altitude and the town's own caps are the Breckenridge page's subject.
