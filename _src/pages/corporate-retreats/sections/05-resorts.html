The corridor

Four resort markets, four pages.

Each retreat market in the corridor runs on its own logistics, so each carries its own page: the venues that market books, the travel line that applies, and the questions specific to the town.

Vail
The corridor's incentive-event center, with the Sonnenalp and Arrabelle class of venue inside the villages and Donovan Pavilion at the edge of them. I-70 travel and Eagle County load-in patterns are on the Vail page.
Aspen
The far-resort booking, where the Little Nell and Hotel Jerome class of calendar meets the far travel tier: lodging at cost, and a two-day commitment for the band whatever the run of show says. The arithmetic is on the Aspen page.
Beaver Creek
On-mountain venues, Beano's Cabin above the Park Hyatt class of village property, and load-ins that run through a gated village. The Beaver Creek page covers the access windows that shape an evening there.
Breckenridge
The highest town on our travel card at roughly 9,600 feet, with a historic district doing the venue work that hotels do elsewhere. Altitude and the town's own caps are the Breckenridge page's subject.
