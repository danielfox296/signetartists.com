The resort towns

Four resort towns, each with its own page.

Each resort town in the corridor runs on its own logistics, so each has its own page with the travel line that applies and the questions specific to the town.

Vail
The corridor’s incentive-event center, an I-70 drive from Denver with its own Eagle County setup patterns.
Aspen
The far-resort booking, with the far travel line and a night’s lodging at cost.
Beaver Creek
Venues up the mountain above the village hotels, with setup through a gated village and access windows that shape the evening.
Breckenridge
The highest of the four towns at roughly 9,600 feet, with a historic district doing the venue work that hotels do elsewhere.
