What it costs

The resort column is the honest number.

Resort dates price from their own column rather than a Denver figure with a surcharge discovered later. The gap between the columns is travel, the extra day the calendar loses, and lodging where the call runs too late to drive down.

Travel is published too. Vail, Beaver Creek, Breckenridge and Winter Park add $600. Aspen and the far resorts add $600 plus lodging at cost, at cost meaning the receipt, with no markup on it. Both sit on the quote as their own lines, so the performance fee your leadership sees is the same fee a Denver client sees.

Every size and both columns are on the rate card, and where these figures sit against the wider Colorado market is set out in the cost guide.
