Resort dates

What a resort date adds to a quote.

A resort date costs more than a metro one everywhere in this market, and it’s worth knowing why before you read a quote.

The first is the drive. Vail and Beaver Creek are a mountain highway from Denver, and Aspen is most of a working day each way. Travel is its own line on the quote, so what your leadership approves reads as a Denver evening with a drive attached.

The second is the night at the far end. A band that finishes late in Aspen isn’t driving back over the passes that night, so a far-resort date carries a night’s lodging, billed at cost. The I-70 towns are close enough that most dates don’t.

Then the calendar. A holiday weekend is priced above a standard date across this market, and the strong resort dates are decided early. Both are on the quote before you sign.
