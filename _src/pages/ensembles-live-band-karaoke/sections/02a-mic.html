Getting to the mic

A name on the host’s list, and the band counts you in.

There’s no app and nothing to download, just a printed songbook and a host with a sign-up list.

Pick a song
The songbook is printed for the tables and up on the screen. Flip to the song you have sung in the car a hundred times, or the one the table dares you into.
Give the host your name
The host keeps the sign-up list and works the floor with it, so nobody has to walk up to the stage to volunteer. Come up as a pair or a group if going alone is the problem. The first singers of the night usually do.
Take the mic
The host calls your name, the band counts you in, and the words scroll on the screen facing you. The band follows your tempo, covers the harmony and the high notes, and ends the song when it should.
