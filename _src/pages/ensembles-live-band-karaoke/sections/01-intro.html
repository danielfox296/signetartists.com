Live band karaoke

The band plays the hits. Your people take the mic when they’re ready.

Colleagues who don’t want to sing don’t have to. The band plays the hits on its own, Journey to Outkast, sung out front, and hands the microphone over the moment somebody gets brave. The words are on a screen so nobody needs to know them, a host works the floor, and a real band is behind whoever is up there.

This is the company party act for the crowd that’s done the casino night and the photo booth and wants something people talk about on Monday. It’s great for a holiday party, a sales kickoff evening, a team offsite or the after-party after a conference dinner, and you can book it on its own or as the late set of a longer night, on one contract with the sound already in the building.
