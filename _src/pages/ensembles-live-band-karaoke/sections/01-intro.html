Live band karaoke

The band plays the hits. Your people take the mic when they’re ready.

Somebody from accounting takes the mic for Livin’ on a Prayer, the band hits the key change behind them, and the whole party sings the last chorus. A real band plays the 80s and 90s hits on its own, sung out front, and hands the microphone to anyone who wants a song. The words are on a screen, a host works the floor, and the band follows whoever is singing: their tempo, the harmony and the high notes.

It books for an office holiday party, a sales kickoff or a team offsite, a conference after-party and the fortieth birthday of anyone who grew up on this songbook.
