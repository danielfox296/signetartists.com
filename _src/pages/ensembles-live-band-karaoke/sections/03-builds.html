Sizes

A four-piece with a host, or a bigger band with a horn.

The four-piece
Voice and guitar, keys, bass and drums, with one of the musicians hosting from the stage. It’s the usual size for a company party of eighty to two hundred.
Five to six piece
The four-piece plus a host of its own on the floor and a horn or a second voice. It’s the size for a ballroom, a conference after-party or a crowd past two hundred, where getting the first singers up is a full-time job and the choruses want the extra weight.

When the party should keep going after the band, the DJ takes over through the same sound system on the same contract.
