Configurations

A four-piece with a host, or a bigger band with a horn.

The four-piece
Voice and guitar, keys, bass and drums, with one of the musicians hosting from the stage. The usual size for a company party of eighty to two hundred: 14 by 10 feet, two dedicated 20-amp circuits, a 90-minute load-in, and two lyric screens, one facing the singer and one facing the crowd.
Five to six piece
The four-piece plus a dedicated host on the floor and a horn or a second voice. For a ballroom, a conference after-party or a crowd past two hundred, where the host’s job of getting the first singers up is a full-time one and the band wants the extra weight on the choruses.

Either configuration can play dinner at conversation volume, 65 to 72 dBA at the nearest table, before the night turns up. An announcement microphone is ready for the remarks, the raffle and the thank-yous.

Footprints and power by size are on the technical page. When the same night wants a DJ after the band, the DJ runs through the same sound system on the same contract.
