The songbook

The songs an office of every age already knows.

The book runs from the 80s and 90s onward, because those are the songs a twenty-six-year-old and a fifty-eight-year-old can both sing without looking at the screen: Journey, Bon Jovi, Whitney Houston, Prince, Madonna, Queen, Oasis, No Doubt, Alanis Morissette, TLC, the Backstreet Boys, Outkast and the Killers.

Between singers the band plays the same book as a party band, sung out front, and the floor keeps dancing. A song for one particular person, the CEO’s karaoke song or the retiring colleague’s, is a request we take the week before if it’s already in the book, and a learned song with four weeks’ notice if it isn’t. The band’s straight party set is on the repertoire page.
