The songbook

The songs an office of every age already knows.

The book runs from the 80s and 90s onward, because those are the songs a twenty-six-year-old and a fifty-eight-year-old can both sing without looking at the screen: Journey, Bon Jovi, Whitney Houston, Prince, Madonna, Queen, Oasis, No Doubt, Alanis Morissette, TLC, the Backstreet Boys, Outkast, the Killers.

When nobody is on the mic the band plays the same book as a party band, sung out front, and the floor doesn’t know the difference. The songbook is printed for the tables and on the screen. A song for a specific person, the CEO’s one karaoke song or the retiring colleague’s, is a request we take the week before if it’s already in the book, and a learned song with four weeks’ notice if it isn’t.

The band’s own covers list, the songs it plays when the night is a straight party set, is searchable on the repertoire page.
