Booking it

Book it on its own, or as the late set of a longer night.

Book the whole evening, or just the part of the night that follows dinner and the remarks. Either way it’s one contract with the sound already handled.

What comes with the booking

  • The band and the host
  • Sound support that’s the right size for the venue, with the band’s own monitors so the singer can hear themselves
  • Two lyric screens, one facing the singer and one facing the crowd
  • The printed songbook for the tables
  • An announcement microphone for the remarks and the raffle
  • Load-in, sound check and load-out, with the certificate of insurance and W-9

What the venue provides

  • A 14 by 10 foot performance area for the four-piece; the bigger band’s footprint is on the technical page
  • Two dedicated 20-amp circuits
  • 90 minutes of load-in before the first set
  • A venue contact for load-in and sound check

If the list is still long when the booked time ends, you can add another half hour on the night, at the same rate as the rest of the evening.

Tell us the venue, the headcount and whether there are speeches, and we’ll recommend the configuration and come back with a price. What live music costs in this market is on the pricing page.
