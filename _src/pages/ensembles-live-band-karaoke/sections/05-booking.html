Booking it

On its own, or as the loud chapter of a longer night.

The act books as a whole evening or as the part of the night that follows dinner and the remarks, and the difference is one line on the contract.

What comes with the booking

  • The band and the host
  • Sound sized to the venue, with the band’s own monitors, which for a karaoke act is what decides whether the singer can hear themselves
  • Two lyric screens, one facing the singer and one facing the crowd
  • The printed songbook for the tables
  • An announcement microphone on its own channel for the remarks and the raffle
  • Load-in, sound check and load-out, with the certificate of insurance and the W-9 on the contract

What the venue provides

  • A 14 by 10 foot performance area for the four-piece; the bigger band’s footprint is on the technical page
  • Two dedicated 20-amp circuits
  • 90 minutes of load-in before the first set
  • A venue contact for load-in and sound check

There is no overtime rate. If the list is still long when the contracted time ends, another half hour costs what the earlier ones did, and you can decide it on the night.

What live music costs in this market is on the pricing page. Tell us the venue, the headcount and whether there are speeches, and we will tell you which build the night needs.
