Who throws it

The company, the family, or the department.

The company
A dinner in a restaurant’s private dining space or a hotel function space, booked by whoever books the holiday party, with a procurement process behind it. Vendor setup, a purchase order and a certificate of insurance are all available and none of them are fast, so the corporate page covers how to start early and how a band works around a program of speeches.
The family
A dinner at home or at the club, with the grandchildren at one end of the table, which is the milestone shape: a toast, an announcement microphone, and a set built from the years the retiree actually cares about. The Denver private parties page covers the club dinners this city books and what a house has to provide.
The department
The Friday send-off with the whole floor there, which wants something to do rather than something to listen to. Live band karaoke is the answer, and the after-party page covers the hour after a dinner when the sound system is already up.
