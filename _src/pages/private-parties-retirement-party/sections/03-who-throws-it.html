Who throws it

The company, the family, or the department.

The company
A dinner in a private dining space or a hotel, booked by whoever books the holiday party and handled the way any company event is, with the speeches built into the plan.
The family
A dinner at home or at a Denver club, with the grandchildren at one end of the table, a toast, and a set built from the years the retiree cares about.
The department
The Friday send-off with the whole floor there, which wants something to do more than something to listen to. Live band karaoke is the answer, played as the after-party if a dinner comes first.
