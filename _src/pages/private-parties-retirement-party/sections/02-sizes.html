Sizes

Music that gets out of the way of the speeches.

The speeches are the point of a retirement party, so the music is built around them: silent for the remarks, back the moment the glass goes down, and sized to the place.

The jazz duo, or one guitar
Upright bass and guitar, or one jazz guitarist, playing the standards quietly under the dinner. The standards reach a table of colleagues aged twenty-five to seventy at once, and the jazz duo stops for every speech.
A singer with a guitar
One voice and a guitar playing the songs the office knows and the one the retiree has always asked for, learned for the night. Tejas Singh plays it.
Live band karaoke for the department
The band plays the 80s and 90s hits on its own and hands the microphone to the colleagues who’ve been threatening to sing for a decade. The retiring colleague’s one karaoke song is a request we take the week before. Live band karaoke needs a big private space or a hotel event space.
The party band
A trio or a quartet for the send-off that ends on a floor. Tony Medina plays it up to a four piece, the same band plays the dinner first, and the DJ can take the late end through the same sound system.
