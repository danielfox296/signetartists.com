Retirement party

Live music for a retirement party.

The third speech ends, the glasses go up, and the guitarist starts the song the retiree has asked for at every office party since the nineties. Music for a retirement party fills everything around the speeches and stops for them: a jazz duo under the dinner, a singer with a guitar for the one song, and for the department that wants to make noise, the band that hands them the microphone.

It’s one of our private parties bookings when the family throws it and one of our corporate bookings when the company does, and the music is the same either way.
