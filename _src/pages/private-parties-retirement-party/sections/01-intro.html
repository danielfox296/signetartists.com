Retirement party

Live music for a retirement party.

A retirement party has forty years of colleagues in one place and at least three speeches, and the music has to get out of the way of the speeches and fill everything around them. A jazz duo playing the standards at a volume the table talks across, a singer with a guitar who plays the one song the retiree has been requesting at every office party since the nineties, and, for the department that wants to make noise, the band that hands them the microphone, on one contract with one personal contact and an announcement microphone ready for whoever stands up.

A retirement party is part of our private parties bookings when the family throws it and part of our corporate bookings when the company does, and the music is the same either way. It comes with sound support that’s the right size for the space, a certificate of insurance and a W-9 with the quote, and vendor setup and a purchase order where a company needs them.
