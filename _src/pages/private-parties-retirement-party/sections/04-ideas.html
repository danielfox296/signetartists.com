Ideas

Retirement party ideas that aren’t a slideshow.

The slideshow is happening anyway. These are the ideas that give the rest of the night something to do, each with the page that covers it.

The retiree’s song, sung
The one they’ve requested at every office party for thirty years, learned for the night and played with everyone watching. The first learned song is included in every booking, and the proposals and surprise songs page covers how a single song gets placed.
A set from the year they started
The standards and the songs from the decade they joined, threaded through the dinner. The repertoire page is searchable by title.
Live band karaoke for the colleagues
The band plays the hits and hands the microphone to the accounting team. The live band karaoke page covers the size it needs.
A jazz duo silent for the speeches
Upright bass and guitar at a volume the table talks across, silent for the remarks, back the moment the glass goes down. The jazz duo and trio page covers it.
A DJ built around their decade
A set built from the year they started work, in 6 by 4 feet on one circuit, for the send-off that turns into a floor. The DJ page covers it.
A band for the floor
A trio or a quartet playing the songs the whole office knows. Tony Medina plays it up to a four piece.
