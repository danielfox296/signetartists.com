Corporate retreats

Beaver Creek corporate retreats, in the village or up the mountain.

Snow falling on the plaza outside, the village quiet, and inside a band playing for a group of colleagues who have stopped checking their email. A Beaver Creek retreat books a welcome night, dinner at conversation volume and a closing night with a dance floor, solo through quartet on one contract, in the village or up the mountain.

Beaver Creek’s event calendar is split in two: hotels on a terraced, pedestrian village where escalators do the climbing, and venues up the mountain that gear reaches over snow in winter. Which half your evening books into decides the setup, and we settle it with the venue before the day. Every resort town starts from the corporate retreats page, and the off-site dinner up the mountain this valley is known for is among the corporate retreat evening ideas.
