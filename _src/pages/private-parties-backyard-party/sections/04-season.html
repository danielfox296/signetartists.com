The season

May to September, on the wedding calendar’s Saturdays.

Backyard parties run from the first warm weekend in May to the last one in September, and the Saturdays in June and September are the ones every wedding in the state wants too, so a summer Saturday is the date to send early. A Sunday afternoon, a Friday evening or any weeknight in July is the easier ask, and the party is usually better for it, because a backyard party is an afternoon that turns into an evening rather than a night that starts late.

The afternoon and the evening want different music, and the same booking covers both: a singer with a guitar or a duo while the food is on, at a volume the tables talk across, and the band or the DJ once the light goes. Sets run 45 to 60 minutes with music through the breaks, so the yard never goes quiet, and a party that runs from the afternoon into the night is one contract rather than two.
