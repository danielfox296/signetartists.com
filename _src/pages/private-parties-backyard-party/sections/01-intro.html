Backyard party

Live music for your backyard party.

The backyard party is the one where the string lights go up over the patio, the neighbors are invited so nobody minds the noise, and the band plays on the deck while the sun goes down behind the fence. It’s the easiest party in the calendar to throw and the easiest one to get the music wrong at, because a lawn eats sound and a July evening in Denver runs from ninety degrees in the afternoon to a sweater after dark. A singer with a guitar on the patio, a trio on the lawn for the part of the night that dances, or a DJ once it moves inside, and the whole evening on one contract with one personal contact in charge of the music.

A backyard party is part of our private parties bookings. It comes with sound support that’s the right size for a yard, a certificate of insurance and a W-9 with the quote, and the musicians arrive with the smallest setup the evening allows. What the yard has to provide, from the power to the cover, is a paragraph down this page.
