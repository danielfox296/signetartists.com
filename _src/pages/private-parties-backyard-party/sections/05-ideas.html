Ideas

Backyard party ideas for adults.

A yard has two places the music can go, the patio and the lawn, and the size follows from which one the party faces.

A singer with a guitar on the patio
One voice and a guitar playing the songs people ask for while the grill is going. Tejas Singh builds them live with a looper and grows to a quartet if the lawn turns into a floor, and any singer and guitarist on the roster plays the same size.
A flamenco trio for a summer evening
Flamenco guitar, bass and cajón playing the traditional forms as the light goes, with a dancer when the evening wants a set piece. Dirty Flamenco plays it, and the garden party clip above is what it sounds like outdoors.
A jazz duo for a dinner on the deck
Upright bass and guitar playing the standards under a long table outdoors. The jazz duo plays it.
A party band on the lawn
A trio or a quartet playing the hits to a lawn with a floor on it, after playing the dinner quietly first. Tony Medina plays it up to a four piece.
Live band karaoke in the garage
The band plays the 80s and 90s hits and hands the microphone to whoever wants a song, with the cars out and the door up. Live band karaoke fits a two-car garage.
A DJ once it moves inside
The smallest setup a party can book, and the one that carries the late end of a summer night indoors. Band or DJ has its own post.

The cost guide sets out what each size costs in Colorado, house parties included.
