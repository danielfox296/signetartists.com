Ideas

Backyard party ideas for adults.

Hosts search for ideas before they search for a band, so here are the ones that work in a yard, each with the page that covers it.

A singer with a guitar on the patio
One voice and a guitar, building the songs people ask for from the ground up while the grill is going. Tejas Singh does it with a looper, and the singer-guitarist page covers the size.
A flamenco trio for a summer evening
Flamenco guitar, bass and cajon, or one Spanish guitarist, playing the traditional forms at conversation volume as the light goes. Dirty Flamenco plays it, and the garden party clip above is what it sounds like outdoors.
A jazz duo for a dinner on the deck
Upright bass and guitar playing the standards under a long table outdoors, at 65 to 72 dBA at the nearest seat. The jazz duo and trio page covers it.
A party band on the lawn
A trio or a quartet playing the hits to a lawn with a floor on it, before the light goes. Tony Medina plays it up to a four piece.
Live band karaoke in the garage
The band plays the 80s and 90s hits and hands the microphone to whoever wants a song, with the cars out and the door up. The live band karaoke page covers how a guest gets to the microphone.
A DJ once it moves inside
The smallest setup a party can book, in 6 by 4 feet on one circuit, and the one that carries the late end of a summer night indoors. The band or DJ question has its own post.
