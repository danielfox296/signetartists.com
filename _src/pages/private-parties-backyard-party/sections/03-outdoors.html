Outdoors

What the yard needs to provide.

Power first. A wall socket through the nearest window on a dedicated circuit is the answer for every size, and it beats any generator. A generator works if it’s a quiet one, properly grounded and sized with headroom above what the band draws; the domestic one at the edge of the lawn isn’t that, and its noise sits underneath every quiet moment of the evening. Tell us where the socket is and we’ll run the cable.

Cover second. The band and the sound desk need cover in any forecast, a patio roof, a pergola with a top on it or a tent, because a Colorado afternoon cooks a guitar and a Colorado evening cools fast enough that by the end of a damp night the musicians are retuning between songs. If there’s a move-indoors plan, it needs a decision time and a person named to make the call, and the main floor or the garage should be ready to take the smaller setup.

Then the neighbors. The loud part of an outdoor party has a short life anywhere in the city, and the later the hour the shorter it gets, so the plan we’ll propose puts the band on the patio while the sun is up and moves the loud part indoors or to the DJ when it goes down. We won’t quote an ordinance, because the rules vary by street and they change; if your street or HOA has a number in writing, give it to us at contract and we build the night under it.
