Jazz band for a wedding

A jazz band for a Colorado wedding, from the ceremony to the last dance.

A guitar plays the processional, the same standards drift across the terrace an hour later, and by the time the cake is cut there’s a horn on the melody and a full dance floor. A jazz band for a wedding is one booking that grows through the day, from one or two musicians at the ceremony to four on the floor.

It’s the jazz version of our weddings page: one part of the day or all of it, on one contract with a personal contact through the day. The duo and the trio are on the jazz duo and trio page, and Changes Jazz Trio is the named trio on the roster.
