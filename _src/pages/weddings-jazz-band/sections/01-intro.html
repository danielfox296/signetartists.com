Jazz band for a wedding

A jazz band for a Colorado wedding, from the ceremony to the last dance.

The same book of standards does three different jobs on a wedding day. It carries a walk down the aisle, it sits under a hundred conversations on the terrace, and it fills a floor once the cake is cut. What changes is how many musicians are playing it. A jazz band for a wedding is one booking that grows through the day.

This is the jazz version of our weddings page. Book one part of the day or all of it, on one contract with sound included at every size and a personal contact through the day. The duo and the trio are spec’d on the jazz duo and trio page, and Changes Jazz Trio is the named trio on the roster.
