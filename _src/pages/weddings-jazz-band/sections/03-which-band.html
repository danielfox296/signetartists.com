The jazz duo and trio
Booked as a format: upright bass and guitar, plus drums when the night wants them. We guarantee the instrumentation, the repertoire and the volume, and the musicians we send deliver all three. It’s the booking for a couple who want the sound and don’t need a name attached to it.
Changes Jazz Trio
Guitar, fretless bass and drums under three published names, playing the standards with a modern edge, and a quartet with saxophone when the floor wants a horn out front. It’s the booking for a couple who want to hear exactly who is playing before they decide.
Jazz until dinner, then something else
Plenty of weddings want standards through the ceremony, the hour and dinner, and a floor that dances to pop and rock. That pairs the jazz musicians with a covers band like Tejas Singh or with the DJ, on one contract and one sound system.
