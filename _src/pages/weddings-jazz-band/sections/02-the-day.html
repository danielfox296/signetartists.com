The day

Four moments, four sizes of the same band.

One or two musicians at the ceremony, two on the terrace, three at dinner and four on the floor, and the guests hear it as one band getting bigger.

The ceremony
A solo jazz guitarist or the duo, playing a prelude of ballads while the seats fill, the processional you chose, and something with a lift in it for the walk back up the aisle.
The cocktail hour
Upright bass and guitar, at a level guests who’ve just met can talk across. Standards are the repertoire because nobody has to be told what they are.
Dinner
The trio, with the drums played with brushes so the kit sits under the tables. The music stops for each toast while your speaker is still steps from the microphone. Changes Jazz Trio plays this size with a modern edge on the standards.
The first dance and the floor
The quartet, the trio plus a horn or a voice, playing the swing and the soul end of the standards book for the hour after the first dance, and the DJ carries the late end through the same sound system, on the same contract.
