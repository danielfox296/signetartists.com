Who plays it

The duo, the singer, the solo, and the piano.

Upright bass and guitar

The jazz duo is the booking we recommend first for a common area: two instruments playing the standards, a volume two musicians can’t push past by accident, and a sound you can tell is live from across the space. It’s the size for a dining hall of sixty and for a lounge of fifteen.

One singer, one guitar

The singer and guitarist is the version for a community that wants the words sung: the Beatles, Motown, the folk songs and the country songs, with the standards mixed in.

Solo guitar, or solo piano

A solo guitarist plays the standards and Spanish-tinged sets instrumentally for a smaller group or a memory care neighborhood. Where the community has a piano in the common area, a pianist plays the standards and the covers on it, with a voice when the program wants singing.

The published song list is searchable on the repertoire page, and the first learned song a community asks for is included in every booking.
