The afternoon

What a visit looks like.

Music in the common area, built from the decades the residents know, with requests taken as they come.

The set
The standards and the Great American Songbook: Autumn Leaves, All of Me, Someday My Prince Will Come, The Girl from Ipanema. The duo plays it without a singer, so the melodies carry the songs and anyone who wants to sing along can. A singer with a guitar is the version for a community that wants the words: the Beatles, Motown, the folk songs and the country songs from the years the residents were young. In December the same musicians play the season’s songs, which the Christmas jazz trio page covers.
A sing-along
Where the program wants the residents singing, Your Bird Can Sing is the booking: two musicians who play the Beatles and get the whole common area singing the choruses.
The volume
About the level of a conversation, so a resident at the back of the space hears the melody and a resident at the front can still talk to the person beside them. The musicians set it to the space, and in a small lounge the guitar plays with no amplification at all.
Memory care
The same musicians play a shorter, quieter set to a smaller group, closer to the residents, leaning on the songs most likely to be recognized: the standards, a hymn or two, and the songs of the fifties and sixties. The musician follows the residents’ lead on requests and on when a set should end.
