Senior living and memory care

Live music for senior living communities, on a weekday afternoon.

The afternoon program at an assisted living community, a memory care neighborhood or a senior center wants music the residents already know: the standards and the Great American Songbook, and the songs of the sixties and seventies, played live in the common area by musicians who take requests. We book that as a solo musician or a duo, for one date or as a standing weekday afternoon, and the duo we recommend first is upright bass and guitar.

This is the senior living page of our daytime bookings, written for the activity director or life enrichment coordinator who fills a calendar every month and has a budget line to fill it from. The booking comes with sound support that’s the right size for a common area, a certificate of insurance naming the community, a W-9 and one itemised invoice with the quote, and one personal contact for every visit.
