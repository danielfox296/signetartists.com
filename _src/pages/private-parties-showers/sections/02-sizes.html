The configurations

A guitarist, a singer, or a duo.

A shower is a table, so the music has to fit in the corner of a dining space and never rise above the conversation. These are the three sizes it books.

One guitar
A jazz guitarist or a Spanish guitarist playing under the meal at 65 to 72 dBA at the nearest table, in 6 by 4 feet on one circuit. It’s the size we recommend first for a shower at home or in a private dining space, and the solo guitarist page covers it. Dirty Flamenco plays the Spanish version as one guitarist.
A singer with a guitar
One voice and a guitar taking the guest of honor’s requests, building the songs from the ground up. Tejas Singh does it with a looper, and the one song the afternoon is built around, the couple’s song at a bridal shower or the lullaby a mother-to-be asked for, is learned for the day if it isn’t already in the book. The first learned song is included in every booking.
The jazz duo
Upright bass and guitar, for a shower of forty in a garden or a restaurant’s private dining space, where one guitar would be lost. It sets up in 8 by 6 feet on one circuit, plays at the same volume, and the jazz duo and trio page covers it.

What live music costs in this market is on the pricing page, and the cost guide sets out the Colorado figures for each kind of booking.
