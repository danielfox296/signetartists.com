Who books it

The baby shower, the bridal shower, and the one with everybody.

The baby shower
An afternoon at home or in a garden, with the guest of honor’s mother and her friends at one end of the table and her own friends at the other. One guitar under the food, and a song for the mother-to-be if there’s one she wants.
The bridal shower
A private dining space, a garden or a house, usually a few months before the wedding. A guitarist or a duo plays the standards under lunch, a singer plays the couple’s song if the host wants it, and the same duo can play the cocktail hour on the day, on the same contract.
The couples shower
The evening version on a patio, with partners and friends of both invited, which makes it a small party more than a lunch. A singer with a guitar or a duo plays it, the way it plays a backyard party.
