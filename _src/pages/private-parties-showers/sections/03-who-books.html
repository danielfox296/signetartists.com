Who books it

The baby shower, the bridal shower, and the one with everybody.

The baby shower
An afternoon at home or in a garden, with the guest of honor’s mother and her friends at one end of the table and her own friends at the other, and a host who wants the afternoon to have something in it other than games. One guitar under the food, at a volume the table talks across, and a song for the mother-to-be if there’s one she wants.
The bridal shower
A private dining space, a garden or a house, usually a few months before the wedding, and the first time the two families sit at one table. A guitarist or a duo plays the standards under lunch, a singer plays the couple’s song if the host wants it, and the same duo can play the cocktail hour on the day, on the same contract. A shower served as a brunch is on the brunch page.
The couples shower
The evening version, on a patio, with partners and friends of both invited, which turns it into a small party rather than a lunch. A singer with a guitar or a duo plays it, and the backyard party page covers what a yard has to provide once the light goes.
