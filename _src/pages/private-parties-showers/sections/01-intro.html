Baby and bridal showers

Live music for a baby shower or a bridal shower.

A shower is an afternoon at a long table, at home, in a garden or in a restaurant’s private dining space, with three generations of one family and the guest of honor’s friends talking over each other. The entertainment that fits it is a guitarist in the corner playing the standards at a volume the table talks across, or a singer with a guitar taking the guest of honor’s requests, so the afternoon has something in it other than the games. It’s one of the smallest bookings we take.

A shower is part of our private parties bookings and it happens in daylight, so it sits beside our daytime bookings too. It comes with sound support that’s the right size for a dining space or a garden, a certificate of insurance and a W-9 with the quote, and one personal contact, and a guitarist sets up in 6 by 4 feet on one circuit with a 45-minute load-in.
