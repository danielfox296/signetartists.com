Ideas

Shower entertainment ideas, without a game in sight.

The ideas that fit a table in daylight, each with the page that covers it.

A guitarist under the meal
The standards on one guitar, at a volume the table talks across, so the afternoon has music in it and nobody has to do anything. The solo guitarist page covers it.
A song for the guest of honor
The couple’s song, the lullaby, the song her mother danced to: one song learned for the day and played at the table, with the set carrying on around it. The proposals and surprise songs page covers how a single song gets placed in an afternoon.
A singer taking requests
One voice and a guitar building the songs the table asks for. Tejas Singh plays it, and the singer-guitarist page covers the size.
A Spanish guitarist in the garden
Something with real technique in it under a long table outdoors, which is the sound in the second clip above. Dirty Flamenco plays it as one guitarist.
A jazz duo for a shower served as a brunch
Upright bass and guitar playing the standards while the coffee keeps coming, the same duo restaurants book for a standing Sunday. The brunch page covers it.
