Ideas

Shower entertainment ideas.

A shower is a table, so the music has to fit in the corner of a dining space and stay under the conversation.

A guitarist under the meal
A jazz guitarist or a Spanish guitarist playing under lunch, the size most showers book first, at home or in a private dining space. The solo guitarist plays the standards, and Dirty Flamenco plays the Spanish version as one guitarist.
A singer taking requests
One voice and a guitar playing the songs the table asks for. Tejas Singh builds them live with a looper, and any singer and guitarist on the roster plays the same size.
A song for the guest of honor
The couple’s song, the lullaby, the song her mother danced to: one song learned for the day and played at the table, placed the way a surprise song is, with the set carrying on around it.
A jazz duo for a bigger shower, or a brunch
Upright bass and guitar for a shower of forty in a garden, where one guitar would be lost, and the same duo restaurants book for a Sunday brunch. The jazz duo plays it.
