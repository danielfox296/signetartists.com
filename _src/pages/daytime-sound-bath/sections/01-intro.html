Daytime sound bath

A daytime sound bath for your studio, your office or your program.

Firstwater’s sound bath is on this roster for corporate teams, offsites and retreats, and it books during the day just as well: a Sunday morning class at a yoga studio, a lunchtime session in a conference space, an afternoon at a senior center, a staff day at a school, a session at a hospice or a recovery center, or the morning that opens a retreat. Seventy-five minutes, one facilitator, and people lying down in low light with nothing to do and nothing to say.
