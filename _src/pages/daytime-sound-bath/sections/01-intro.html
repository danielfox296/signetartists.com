Daytime sound bath

A daytime sound bath for your studio, your office or your program.

A studio full of people lies down on mats in the middle of a Sunday morning, the lights go low, and for seventy-five minutes nobody has anywhere to be. Firstwater’s sound bath books during the day as well as for teams and retreats: one facilitator who brings the sound system, the mats and the blankets to your space, or holds the session in a partner space in Denver.

It’s one of our daytime bookings, and the act itself is on Firstwater’s roster page.
