The session

Seventy-five minutes, and what a venue needs.

The lights go down, everyone lies down, and the facilitator opens the session with a short ceremony. For seventy-five minutes there are live instruments played over music composed for the session, with bass deep enough to feel in your chest, and a stretch of real silence in the middle. It closes the way it opened. There’s nothing to follow and nothing to get right, and someone who sleeps through the second half has used the hour well.

The venue supplies enough floor for everyone to lie down with a little space between the mats, a standard outlet, and about ninety minutes before doors for the facilitator to set up and tune the sound system to the space. Firstwater brings the rest, the sound system, the mats, the blankets and the waivers, and phones stay in bags for the length of it.
