The bookings

Five kinds of daytime booking, and what each one wants.

The evening rules mostly still hold: the music sits at a level people talk across, the footprint is a corner, and one contract covers the date. What changes during the day is who is listening and why.

Senior living and memory care
An activity director books a musician for the residents of an assisted living, memory care or independent living community, usually on a weekday afternoon and usually as a standing date. The set is the standards and the Great American Songbook on upright bass and guitar, or one singer with a guitar working the decades the residents grew up with, played in the common area at the level of a conversation.
Funerals, memorial services and celebrations of life
A guitarist for the prelude and the one song at a funeral or a memorial service, a pianist where the chapel has a piano, and a jazz duo through the reception or the celebration of life afterward. A funeral is usually arranged inside a week, the music is exposed because nobody is talking over it, and the songs are the ones the family chooses. The funerals page sets out where the music goes in a service and how it’s planned with the officiant or the funeral director.
The daytime sound bath
Firstwater brings a seventy-five-minute sound bath to a yoga studio’s Sunday morning schedule, a conference space at lunchtime, a senior center, a hospice or a recovery center, a school’s staff day, or the morning that opens a retreat. People lie down in low light, there’s nothing to do and nothing to say, and the facilitator brings the sound system, the mats and the blankets.
Sunday brunch
Upright bass and guitar, a solo guitarist or a pianist under a Sunday brunch at a restaurant, a hotel or a club, booked as a standing Sunday or for one date, and the same music for a private brunch: the morning after a wedding, a bridal brunch, a shower, a birthday.
Farmers markets, libraries and rec centers
A singer with a guitar at a Saturday market on a battery-powered system, a duo in a library’s meeting space for an adult program or on the lawn for a summer concert, an Americana band for a rec center’s community evening. These are public programs with a coordinator, a season and a purchase order.

The acts are the ones on the rest of the roster. The jazz duo, the solo guitarist, the singer and guitarist and the Spanish guitarist play most of these; Firstwater is the sound bath.
