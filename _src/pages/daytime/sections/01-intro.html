Daytime bookings

Live music and sound baths during the day.

The rest of this site is built around evenings. These pages are for the bookings that happen while the sun is up: upright bass and guitar in the common area of an assisted living community on a weekday afternoon, a guitarist playing the prelude at a memorial service, a jazz duo under a Sunday brunch, a sound bath at a yoga studio or in an office at lunchtime, and a singer with a guitar at a Saturday farmers market. The music is smaller, the person booking it usually has a program to fill and a budget line to fill it from, and the booking often repeats.

Each one has its own page here: senior living and memory care, where an activity director books a solo or a duo on a standing weekday afternoon; funerals, memorial services and celebrations of life; the daytime sound bath with Firstwater, for yoga studios, workplaces, senior centers, hospice and recovery centers and schools; Sunday brunch at a restaurant, a hotel or a family’s table at home; and farmers markets, libraries and rec centers, the programs a city runs during the day. All of it comes with sound support that’s the right size for the space, a certificate of insurance and a W-9 with the quote, and one personal contact.
