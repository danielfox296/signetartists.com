The practical part

A 6 by 4 ft booth on one circuit.

The DJ takes a 6 by 4 ft footprint, one dedicated 20-amp circuit, a 45-minute load-in and a 30-minute strike, with a PA sized to the venue included in the figure. A dedicated announcement microphone is live on its own channel from the end of sound check, so the program can borrow the system whenever it needs a voice.

On the music you get as much say as you want: build the list yourself, hand over a do-not-play list and leave the rest, or describe the crowd and the DJ reads it song by song. Requests from the floor get taken where they fit; a request that would empty the floor gets a straight answer and an alternative.

Open format means the set is built wider than any one list. The roster's live covers catalog, the songs the bands on this site play, is on the repertoire page, and footprints and power for every size are on the technical page.
