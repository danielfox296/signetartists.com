The live instrument option

A live instrument over the set.

Bass or saxophone, played live on the floor over the DJ set. It's the difference between a playlist and a crowd watching something happen, and it holds a dance floor late.

It's quoted as an option when you ask for it, and it works the same on a corporate floor and a wedding floor: the end of the party stays something guests watch as well as dance to.

If the night should open with live musicians instead, a duo that grows to a quartet can play in front of the same late-night handoff, and the whole roster is on the roster page.
