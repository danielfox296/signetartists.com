The live instrument option

A live instrument over the set.

Bass or saxophone, played live on the floor over the DJ set, so the end of the party is something guests watch as well as dance to. It holds a dance floor late, on a company floor or a wedding floor, and it’s quoted as an option when you ask for it.

For live musicians earlier in the night, Tejas Singh starts as a duo, grows to a quartet and hands the floor to the DJ, and every act that can open for it is on the roster page.
