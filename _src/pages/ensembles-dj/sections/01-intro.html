The DJ, the corporate season's most common single booking.

Open format, on its own or carrying the late end after a band steps off, through the same sound system, run by the same team, on the same contract. A Denver DJ carrying a night with production lighting runs {{market:denver_dj}} in this market; ours is quoted for the night you send us.

Most December parties split into two halves: dinner at conversation volume, then a floor that wants to move. The DJ is how the second half gets covered without a second vendor or a second load-in, and a duo through drinks and dinner with the DJ carrying the back half covers a holiday party end to end, priced on the holiday party calendar. That season is decided August through October, and New Year's Eve books first.
