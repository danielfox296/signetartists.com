After the band, the same sound system.

Once we are in the building the evening is already committed, so adding the DJ to a band booking costs far less than hiring one separately would. The band's last set ends, the DJ picks up through the same sound system, and the music does not stop between them.

On its own
Booth, decks and a PA sized to the venue, with a 45-minute load-in. Open format, read live, for a corporate late night or a wedding floor.
After the band
The same contract, the same load-in and the same invoice. A duo through cocktails and dinner with the DJ carrying the back half is the standard Denver holiday shape, set out on the holiday party page.
Late
Added time costs what the earlier hours did, in 30-minute increments, agreed on the night with whoever holds the budget. There is no overtime rate, so the only question at 11pm is whether the crowd wants one more.
