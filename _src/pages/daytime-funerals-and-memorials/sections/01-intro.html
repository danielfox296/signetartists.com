Funerals and memorials

A musician for a funeral, a memorial service or a celebration of life.

As people find their seats, a guitarist is playing the hymn the family chose, and later in the service, the one song that was theirs. A funeral or a memorial has three or four places where music goes, from the prelude to the reception afterward. One guitarist or a pianist covers the service, upright bass and guitar cover the reception, and a service a few days away is a normal request.

It’s one of our daytime bookings, arranged with whoever is leading the service, the family, the funeral director, the officiant or the church, with one personal contact from the first email to the last song.
