Who plays it

Guitar, piano, strings, or a voice.

Acoustic or Spanish guitar

The solo guitarist plays hymns, standards and the songs the family names on steel strings, and the Spanish guitarist plays the same service on nylon strings, which is the sound in the clip above. Either one plays a small chapel without amplification and moves to the graveside.

Piano

Where the chapel, the church or the funeral home has a piano, a pianist plays the prelude, the song and the recessional on it, and sings when the family wants the words.

A singer

The singer and guitarist is the booking when the words matter: a hymn sung, a country song, the one from the year they married. One voice and a guitar, and the same musician plays the prelude instrumentally.

When the family wants strings, a violinist, or violin and cello, plays Ave Maria and the hymns in the chapel and at the graveside. For the reception, the jazz duo or the trio plays the hour after. When a celebration of life at a home or a club runs into the evening, the private parties page covers a longer booking.
