The evening

Doors, dinner, the program, and after.

Each slot wants a different amount of music, and the one in the middle wants none. A jazz band is the act that can do all four without a handoff.

As the doors open
The duo, upright bass and guitar, playing the standards while guests find their names and their people. The music reads as live from across the venue and stays under the first hour of conversation, which is the hour that decides what kind of evening it’s going to be.
Through dinner
The trio, drums played with brushes, at 65 to 72 dBA at the nearest table, which is a level the whole table talks across. Sets run 45 to 60 minutes with music through every break, so the evening never goes silent by accident. Changes Jazz Trio plays this size under its own three names, with a modern edge on the book.
The remarks and the awards
Silence, on cue. The announcement microphone is live on its own channel from sound check, the band plays up to the moment and stops while your speaker is still steps from it, and a walk-up for each award is planned on the run of show. This is the slot most bands get wrong, and it’s the one the program depends on.
After the program
The quartet, the trio plus a horn or a singer out front, playing the swing and the soul end of the book for the hour the floor opens. When the night should run past that, the DJ or a covers band carries the late end through the same sound system, on the same contract. The after-party page covers that hour.
