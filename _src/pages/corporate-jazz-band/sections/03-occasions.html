Occasions

The corporate nights that book a jazz band.

Client dinners and member evenings
The duo plays the whole evening, for twenty at one table or eighty at ten, and the conversation is the event.
Galas and award nights
The trio plays through dinner, goes silent for the program, and comes back as the quartet after it. A gala is where the horn is worth having.
Holiday parties
The trio plays drinks and dinner, then the quartet with a singer takes the first hour of the dance floor, or hands off to the DJ. In December the Christmas jazz trio plays the season’s songs.
Retreat evenings in the mountains
The duo plays a lodge dinner, with the mountain going dark through the glass.
