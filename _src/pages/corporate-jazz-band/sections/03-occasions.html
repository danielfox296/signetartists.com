Occasions

The corporate nights that book a jazz band.

Client dinners and member evenings
The duo for the whole evening. Twenty at one table or eighty at ten, and the conversation is the event. The client dinners page is the volume argument in full.
Galas and award nights
The trio through dinner, silence for the program, the quartet after it. The galas page sets out the cues, and a gala is where the horn earns its place.
Holiday parties
The trio through drinks and dinner, then the quartet with a singer for the first hour of the floor, or a handoff to the DJ. In December a Christmas jazz trio plays the season’s book at the same volume.
Retreat evenings in the mountains
The duo at a lodge dinner, with the mountain going dark through the glass. The retreats page covers the resort towns and their logistics, altitude included.
