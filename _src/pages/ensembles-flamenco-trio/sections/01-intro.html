Spanish guitar

Spanish guitar for private events, played by Dirty Flamenco.

Spanish guitar is what people ask for and flamenco is what they're picturing: nylon strings, a hand on the body of the guitar keeping time, and a sound that turns a dinner party into an evening guests describe afterwards. Dirty Flamenco is a working group with a standing repertoire of real palos, fronted by Gary Meyers and sized for a private event.

Book it for the night that has a moment in it. Think a dinner party for twenty where the guest of honour shouldn't have to host, a milestone birthday, a cultural centerpiece at a member event, or a corporate dinner that has to be remembered. It plays the whole evening under the conversation and then steps forward for one set piece where the program leaves space for it.

This is a named act, an existing group booked under its own name. The solo booking, one Spanish guitarist for a ceremony, a cocktail hour or a dinner, is on its own page.
