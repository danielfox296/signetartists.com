Spanish guitar

Spanish guitar for private events, played by Dirty Flamenco.

Spanish guitar is what people ask for and flamenco is what they are picturing: nylon strings, a hand on the body of the guitar keeping time, and a sound that turns a dinner party into an evening guests describe afterwards. In Colorado that sound is sold two ways, as a concert on a stage or as background with a Spanish flavor. Dirty Flamenco is a third thing. A working group with a standing repertoire of real palos, fronted by Gary Meyers, sized and priced for a private event rather than a concert hall.

Book it for the night that has a moment in it: a dinner party for twenty where the guest of honour should not have to host, a milestone birthday, a cultural centerpiece at a member event, a corporate dinner that has to be remembered. It plays the whole evening under the conversation and then takes the space for one set piece when the program leaves it space.

This is a named act rather than a format, an existing group booked under its own name, and any change to the lineup is named to you in advance.
