Occasions

Who books flamenco.

Member and association events
An evening with remarks in it that wants one thing guests describe the next day, with the set piece placed where the program has space for it.
The corporate dinner with one photographed moment
Dinner at conversation volume, and the set piece after the remarks, placed against the timeline the way a toast is.
The dinner party at home
Twenty around a long table, a courtyard or a backyard, with flamenco under the whole meal. It’s the smallest booking the trio takes and the one it fits most naturally.
The milestone birthday
A fiftieth, a sixtieth, an anniversary or a retirement, in Denver or up in the mountains, with a centerpiece the guest of honor doesn’t have to host and no program to run.
The wedding ceremony and cocktail hour
Processional, prelude and recessional on nylon strings, then an hour of palos at conversation volume through the cocktail hour.
