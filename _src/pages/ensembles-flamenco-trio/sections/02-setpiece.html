The set piece

The set piece, and where it goes in the evening.

This is a booking guests watch, and that changes how it sits in an evening.

The set piece is planned. It lands where the program leaves space for it, after remarks or between dinner and the late end. We place it with your timeline, the same way a toast or an award is placed, and the run of show page shows how that works.

The repertoire is palos, the traditional forms. Bulerias, solea, alegrias, tangos: the forms themselves. That's the difference between a flamenco booking and a Spanish guitar booking.

The trio still plays the whole evening around its moment. Flamenco guitar, bass and cajon play cocktails and dinner with a full musical range that doesn't depend on a dance floor, so guests hear one act all night and the set piece arrives as its peak.
