The set piece

One set piece, placed like a toast.

The set piece is planned. The trio plays the whole evening, and the set piece is its peak. We place it with your timeline the same way a toast or an award is placed, after the remarks or before the dancing, and the run of show page shows how that works.

The repertoire is palos, the traditional forms. The trio plays bulerías, soleá, alegrías and tangos as the forms themselves, which is what separates a flamenco booking from a Spanish guitar booking.
