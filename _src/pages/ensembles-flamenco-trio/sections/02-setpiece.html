The set piece

Built to be watched, placed where the program leaves space.

Most live music at a private event is designed to be felt and not watched. This is the opposite booking, and that changes how it sits in an evening.

The moment is scheduled. A set piece lands where the program leaves space for it: after remarks, between dinner and the late end, at the turn of a milestone evening. We place it against your timeline before the night, the same way a toast or an award is placed, and the run of show page shows how that document works.

The repertoire is palos, the traditional forms. Bulerias, solea, alegrias, tangos: the forms themselves, not a set of pop songs with nylon strings on them. That is the difference between a flamenco booking and a Spanish guitar booking, and it is audible inside eight bars.

The trio still does the whole evening's work around its moment. Flamenco guitar, bass and cajon play cocktails and dinner with a full musical range that does not depend on a dance floor, so guests hear one act all night and the set piece arrives as its peak.
