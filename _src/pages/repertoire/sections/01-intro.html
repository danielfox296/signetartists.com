Repertoire

a sample of crowd pleasers

A long song list proves range. It doesn’t tell you what to do with it. Six moments make up a night, from what plays while guests arrive to what sits under dinner to what fills a floor, and the whole book is below, where you can search it.

Nothing here is a fixed setlist. We draw from this material and read the crowd song by song. If your client wants something that isn’t on it, the first learned song is included in every booking and each one after that is its own line on the quote.
