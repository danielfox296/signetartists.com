Repertoire

Six sets, and everything they’re built from.

A long song list proves range. It doesn’t tell you what to do with it. So this page starts with the six shapes a night takes, from what plays while guests arrive to what sits under dinner to what fills a floor, then hands you the whole book underneath, searchable.

Nothing here is a fixed setlist. We draw from this material and read the crowd song by song. If your client wants something that isn’t on it, the first learned song is included in every booking and each one after that is its own line on the quote.
