The program

What the hour looks like.

The booklets go out first. Then the duo plays one set of 45 to 60 minutes, or two shorter ones with a break.

The space
The duo sets up at floor level in about 8 by 6 feet, plugs into one standard outlet, and arrives about an hour before the start. It works in a common area, a dining hall after lunch, a library, a backyard or a courtyard.
The volume
65 to 72 dBA at the nearest seat, which is the level of a conversation. Residents at the back of the common area hear the melody, and the front table can keep talking. In a small lounge the duo plays with no amplification at all, and outdoors it plays louder to reach the back of a courtyard.
A standing date
The same afternoon each month, or every other week, on one contract with one personal contact for the whole run. The set changes from visit to visit, and the duo brings back the songs your residents ask for again.
