The program

What the hour looks like.

One set of 45 to 60 minutes, or two shorter sets with a break in between, with the booklets handed out at the start.

Requests
Requests are welcome, and the duo takes them as they come.
The space
The duo sets up on the floor in about 8 by 6 feet, needs one standard outlet, and arrives about an hour before the program starts. A common area, a dining hall after lunch, a library, a backyard or a courtyard all work.
The volume
65 to 72 dBA at the nearest seat, which is the level of a conversation, so someone at the back can hear the melody and the people up front can still talk. In a small lounge the duo plays without amplification, and outdoors it plays louder to reach the back of a courtyard.
A standing date
The same afternoon each month, or every other week, on one contract with one personal contact for the whole run. The set changes from visit to visit, and the duo keeps the songs people ask for again.

Power, footprint and weather cover for every size are on the technical page.
