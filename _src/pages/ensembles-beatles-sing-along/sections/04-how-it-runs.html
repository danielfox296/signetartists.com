How it runs

What the hour looks like.

The booklets go out first. Then the duo plays one set of 45 to 60 minutes, or two shorter ones with a break.

The space
The duo sets up at floor level in about 8 by 6 feet, plugs into one standard outlet, and arrives about an hour before the start. A backyard, a tent, a company patio, a library, a school gym, the common area of a senior living community: the setup is the same in all of them.
The volume
65 to 72 dBA at the nearest seat, which is the level of a conversation. People at the back of a full backyard hear the melody, and the front table can keep talking. In a small space the duo plays with no amplification at all, and outdoors it plays louder to reach the far end of a lawn or a courtyard.
A standing date
The same afternoon each month, or every other week, on one contract with one personal contact for the whole run. The set changes from visit to visit, and the duo brings back the songs the regulars ask for again.
