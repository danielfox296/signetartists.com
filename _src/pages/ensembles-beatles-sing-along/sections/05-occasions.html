Occasions

Who books a Beatles sing-along.

Senior living and memory care
An activity director books it for one afternoon or puts it on the calendar as a standing date. The residents know these songs, and a resident who can’t remember the verse reads it from the booklet. In memory care the same duo plays a shorter set to a smaller group, at the low end of the volume.
Libraries and community programs
A program hour for a public audience of every age, indoors or in a courtyard. With the booklets, people who have never heard a song can still sing it.
Family parties
A fiftieth anniversary or an eightieth birthday at the house, a retirement send-off, or a backyard party with four generations on the lawn. Whoever the party is for grew up on these records, and the booklets get their grandchildren singing them too.
