Occasions

Where a Beatles sing-along lands.

Senior living and memory care
The songs residents already know, with the words in their hands so they can sing them. An activity director can book it for one afternoon or put it on the calendar as a standing date, and in a memory care neighborhood the same duo plays a shorter set to a smaller group at the low end of the volume.
Libraries and community programs
A program that has to hold a space full of strangers of every age for an hour. Handing out booklets gives everyone something to do with that hour, and people who came expecting to sit and watch end up singing.
Anniversary parties
A fortieth or fiftieth anniversary where the couple, their children and their grandchildren are all in the garden. These are the records the couple owned, and the booklets are what get the grandchildren singing them too.
Retirement parties
A send-off for someone who has had these songs playing somewhere in the background their whole working life. It suits the part of the evening once the speeches are finished and everyone has stopped talking at the guest of honour.
Family parties in a garden
There are four generations on the lawn and no stage anywhere. A duo plays on the grass off one outlet, and the volume sits where a neighbor won’t mind and a table of teenagers will still join in.
