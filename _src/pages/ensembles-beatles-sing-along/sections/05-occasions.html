Occasions

Who books a Beatles sing-along.

Family parties
An anniversary, a milestone birthday, a retirement send-off or a backyard party, at the house or at a rented space. Whoever the party is for grew up on these records, and their grandchildren end up singing them too.
Company events
A summer picnic, the holiday party or the last night of a retreat. It’s the one thing the whole company does together, and it plays at a volume the tables can still talk across.
Weddings
A welcome party, a rehearsal dinner or the after-party. The two families meet for the first time that weekend, and they all know these songs, so it’s the first thing they do together.
Community events
A library’s summer program, a school’s family night, a church picnic, a block party or a farmers market. It’s open to whoever walks up, so the audience is every age at once, and this is one of the few programs that works for the toddlers and the retirees in the same hour.
Senior living and memory care
The residents know every one of these songs from the first time around. In memory care the same duo plays a shorter, quieter set to a smaller group.
