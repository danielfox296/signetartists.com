Occasions

Who books a Beatles sing-along.

Senior living and memory care
A resident who can’t remember the verse reads it from the booklet. In memory care the same duo plays a shorter set to a smaller group, at the low end of the volume.
Libraries and community programs
A program hour open to the public, so the audience is every age at once. With the booklets, someone who has never heard a song can still sing it.
Family parties
An anniversary, a milestone birthday, a retirement send-off or a backyard party, at the house or at a rented space. Whoever the party is for grew up on these records, and the grandchildren learn them from the booklet on the day.
