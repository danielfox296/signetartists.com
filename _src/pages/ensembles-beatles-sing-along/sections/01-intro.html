Beatles songs, and everybody sings

Your Bird Can Sing

A named act. A multi-instrumental duo, with sing booklets for the guests.

Two musicians play the Beatles catalogue and hand out booklets with the words in them, for a crowd that runs from small children to great-grandparents. Nobody has to be a singer to join in, and by the second chorus of Hey Jude the whole venue is singing.

Book it for the afternoon that has to work for everybody in the space at once. Think the common area of a senior living community on a standing weekday afternoon, a library or community center program, a family party where four generations are in the garden, a school assembly, or a summer courtyard event where you want people to stay rather than drift off once the food is gone.
