Beatles songs, and everybody sings

Your Bird Can Sing

A multi-instrumental duo, with lyric booklets for the guests.

Two musicians play the Beatles and hand out lyric booklets, and everyone sings, from the grandchildren to the great-grandparents. Most people know the choruses already, and the booklets have the rest of the words. By the second chorus of Hey Jude the whole place is singing.

It’s the booking for a crowd of mixed ages, and for an afternoon where you want the whole audience taking part. Activity directors book it for senior living and memory care, libraries put it on as a program hour, and families book it for a fiftieth anniversary or an eightieth birthday, when four generations are in one backyard.
