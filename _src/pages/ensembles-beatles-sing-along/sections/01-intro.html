Beatles songs, and everybody sings

Your Bird Can Sing

A multi-instrumental duo, with lyric booklets for everyone.

You know the part of Hey Jude where everyone starts singing. A Beatles sing-along is a playful hour of that. Two musicians play and everyone has the words in their hands, so the grandparents and the grandchildren end up on the same chorus.

If you run the activities calendar at a senior living community, your residents know every one of these songs, and it books for one afternoon or as a standing date. It’s also the act for your parents’ fiftieth anniversary or your dad’s eightieth birthday, when four generations are in one backyard and you want them all doing one thing together.
