Booking it

How to book it.

Send us the date, the venue and roughly how many people, and we’ll come back with a price for that afternoon or for a run of them. A senior living community, a library or a school gets the certificate of insurance, the W-9 and an invoice that matches a purchase order in the same email as the contract, so the vendor setup is done before the first visit. What live music costs in this market is on the pricing page.
