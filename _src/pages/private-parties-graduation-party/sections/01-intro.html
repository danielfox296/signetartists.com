Graduation party

Live music for your graduation party.

A graduation party is two parties in one yard. The grandparents, the aunts and the neighbors are at the table, and the graduate’s friends are on the lawn wanting nothing to do with the table, and the music has to reach both without either one leaving. A singer with a guitar who plays the songs the whole family knows and then the ones the graduate asked for, a duo for the family lunch, and a DJ or a party band for the part of the afternoon that belongs to the friends, on one contract with one personal contact in charge of the music.

A graduation party is part of our private parties bookings. It comes with sound support that’s the right size for a house or a yard, a certificate of insurance and a W-9 with the quote, and the musicians arrive with the smallest setup the afternoon allows. It almost always happens outdoors, so the backyard party page covers what the yard has to provide, from the power to the cover.
