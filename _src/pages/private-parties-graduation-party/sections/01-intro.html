Graduation party

Live music for your graduation party.

The grandparents are at the table, the graduate’s friends are on the lawn, and one singer with a guitar plays Brown Eyed Girl for the first and Mr. Brightside for the second. A graduation party is two parties in one yard, and the music reaches both: a singer who plays the family’s songs and then the graduate’s, a duo for the family lunch, and a DJ or a band for the part of the afternoon that belongs to the friends.

It’s one of our private parties bookings, and it almost always happens outdoors, the way a backyard party does.
