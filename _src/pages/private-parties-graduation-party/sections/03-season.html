The season

Late May and June, on everybody’s calendar at once.

Graduation parties land in a three-week window at the end of May and the start of June, on the same weekends as every other graduation in the district and the first wedding Saturdays of the summer, so the date is the thing to send early. A college graduation runs a couple of weeks ahead of the high schools, and the Sunday afternoon after a Saturday ceremony is the easier ask on both calendars.

The party is an afternoon that turns into an evening, which is the backyard shape: a singer with a guitar or a duo while the food is on, at a volume the tables talk across, and the band or the DJ once the family thins out and the friends have the yard. Sets run 45 to 60 minutes with music through the breaks. The loud part of it belongs early or indoors, which the backyard party page sets out, along with the power and the cover a yard has to provide.
