Ideas

Graduation party entertainment ideas.

The ideas that work with two generations in one yard, each with the page that covers it.

A singer taking the graduate’s requests
One voice and a guitar, building the songs the graduate asks for from the ground up after the family’s songs are done. Tejas Singh plays it with a looper.
Live band karaoke for both generations
The band plays the hits from the 80s onward and hands the microphone over, so the graduate’s friends and the graduate’s parents end up singing in the same hour. It needs a big main floor or a garage with the cars out, and the live band karaoke page covers how a guest gets to the microphone.
A DJ set built from the graduate’s playlist
Hand us the playlist and the DJ builds the afternoon around it, in 6 by 4 feet on one circuit, and carries the late end of the party indoors.
A jazz duo for the family lunch
Upright bass and guitar under a long table for the graduation that’s a lunch for the grandparents first. The jazz duo and trio page covers it.
One song for the graduate
The song a parent wants played for their kid in front of everyone, learned for the day if it isn’t in the book. The first learned song is included in every booking, and the proposals and surprise songs page covers how a single song gets placed in a party.
