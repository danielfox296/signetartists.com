Ideas

Graduation party entertainment ideas.

The graduate’s friends and the graduate’s grandparents want different music, and the size that suits the party is the one that can play both.

A singer taking the graduate’s requests
One voice and a guitar playing the songs the whole family knows while the food is on, then the ones the graduate asked for once the table thins out. Tejas Singh builds requests live with a looper, and the same booking grows to a quartet if the lawn turns into a floor.
A jazz duo for the family lunch
Upright bass and guitar under a long table, for the graduation that’s a lunch for the grandparents first. The jazz duo plays it.
A DJ set built from the graduate’s playlist
Hand us the playlist and the DJ builds the afternoon around it, then carries the late end of the party indoors.
A party band, or live band karaoke
A trio or a quartet for a party big enough to have a floor, which Tony Medina plays up to a four piece. Live band karaoke gets the friends and the parents singing in the same hour, because its book runs from the 80s onward.
One song for the graduate
The song a parent wants played for their kid in front of everyone, learned for the day if it isn’t already in the book.
