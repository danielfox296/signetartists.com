The party

The dinner, the patio, and the night after the question.

The dinner with the two families
A long table at home, at a Denver club or in a private dining space, the parents meeting the parents, a toast from each side, and the duo playing the standards under all of it.
The cocktail party on a patio
The summer version, with friends of both invited and the party the point more than the meal. A singer with a guitar or a duo plays it while the light lasts, the way a backyard party does.
The party right after the proposal
The friends set up the house while the question is being asked somewhere else, so the couple walks in to everyone they know and their song already playing. One booking can cover the proposal and the party, and the wedding weekend’s own welcome party is on the weddings page.
