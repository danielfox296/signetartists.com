The party

The dinner, the patio, and the night after the question.

The dinner with the two families
A long table at home or in a restaurant’s private dining space, the parents meeting the parents, a toast from each side, and the duo playing the standards underneath all of it at a volume the table talks across, with the announcement microphone live for whoever stands up. The Denver private parties page covers the club dinners this city books and what a house has to provide.
The cocktail party on a patio
The summer version, with friends of both invited and the party the point rather than the meal. A singer with a guitar or a duo plays it while the light lasts, and the backyard party page covers the power, the cover and what happens to the loud part once the sun goes down.
The party right after the proposal
The one the friends set up in the house while the question was being asked somewhere else, so the couple walks in to everyone they know and their song already playing. The proposals and surprise songs page covers the musician who was already there, and the same booking can cover the question and the party. The wedding weekend’s own welcome party, the night before the day, is on the weddings page.
