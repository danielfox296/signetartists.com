Engagement party

Live music for your engagement party.

An engagement party is the first night the two families are in one place, and it’s smaller and looser than the wedding will be: a dinner at home, a patio in summer, a restaurant’s private dining space, a toast from whoever gets there first, and the couple’s song played once with everyone watching. The music that fits it is a jazz duo or a Spanish guitarist at a volume the table talks across, a singer with a guitar who learns the song for the night, and a trio if the party is the kind that ends on a floor, on one contract with one personal contact in charge of the music.

An engagement party is part of our private parties bookings, and it’s the first booking of the wedding: the duo the families hear at the engagement party can play the cocktail hour on the day, on the same contract. Both sides of the family get one contract and one personal contact to deal with, the paperwork (a certificate of insurance and a W-9) comes with the quote, and there’s an announcement microphone for whichever parent stands up first.
