Engagement party

Live music for your engagement party.

Two sets of parents are meeting over dinner, somebody raises a glass to the couple, and the song they’ll dance to at the wedding gets its first hearing. An engagement party is smaller and looser than the wedding will be, and the music is a jazz duo or a Spanish guitarist under the dinner, a singer who learns the couple’s song, and a trio if the party ends on a floor.

It’s one of our private parties bookings, and often the first booking of the wedding: the duo the families hear that night can play the cocktail hour on the day, on the same contract.
