Ideas

Engagement party entertainment ideas.

The ideas that fit the first night the families meet, each with the page that covers it.

The couple’s song, once
The song they’ll dance to at the wedding, learned for the night and played once in front of both families, so the wedding has a first rehearsal nobody calls one. The first learned song is included in every booking, and the proposals and surprise songs page covers how one song gets placed.
A preview of the wedding’s musicians
The duo at the engagement party is the duo at the cocktail hour, so the families have already heard them by the day. The cocktail hour page covers that hour of the wedding.
A Spanish guitarist under dinner
Something with real technique in it under a long table, and the same guitar couples book for the ceremony. Dirty Flamenco plays it as one guitarist.
A singer taking both families’ requests
One voice and a guitar building the songs each side asks for from the ground up. Tejas Singh plays it with a looper.
A trio for the friends’ version
A trio or a quartet playing the songs the wedding party will dance to, for the engagement party the friends throw. Tony Medina plays it up to a four piece.
Live band karaoke for the wedding party
The band plays the hits and hands the microphone to the best man, months early. The live band karaoke page covers the size it needs.
