Engagement party entertainment ideas.

An engagement party is usually a dinner or a cocktail party for forty, so most start with the duo, and the rest follows from whether the night ends on a floor.

A jazz duo the two families can talk over
Upright bass and guitar playing the standards quietly enough that parents who’ve just met can hear each other, at home, on a patio or in a private dining space. The jazz duo is the same size that plays a wedding cocktail hour.
The couple’s song, once
The song they’ll dance to at the wedding, sung once in front of both families by Tejas Singh, who learns it for the night and takes requests from both sides of the table for the rest of the evening.
A Spanish guitarist under dinner
A guitar with real technique in it under a long table, which is the first clip above, and the same sound couples book for the ceremony. Dirty Flamenco plays it as one guitarist or as a trio.
A trio for the friends’ version
When the friends throw it, it’s a party with a floor. Tony Medina plays it up to a four piece, and the DJ is there for the friends who want the night to run past the band.
Live band karaoke for the wedding party
The band plays the hits and hands the microphone to the best man, months early, with live band karaoke.

The cost guide sets out what each size costs in Colorado, wedding cocktail hours included.
