At home

What the house has to provide, measured.

A solo takes 6 by 4 feet and one dedicated 20-amp circuit, loads in over 45 minutes and strikes in 30. A duo wants 8 by 6 feet, the same single circuit and an hour. That is the entire ask: a corner of the main floor, an outlet not shared with the kitchen, and a door we can carry through.

Load-in at a Denver house is a parking question before it is a music question. Tell us the block and which door is ours, alley, driveway or front walk. We arrive with the smallest rig the night allows, and in street-parking neighborhoods we sort the curb with you the week before rather than circling on the night.

Dinner plays at 65 to 72 dBA at the nearest table, a volume you can talk across without raising your voice. Sets run 45 to 60 minutes with short breaks between them, and audio continues through every break, so the party never goes silent.

Footprints, power and load-in times for every size are on the technical page.
