After dark

Denver evenings, stated plainly.

Amplified sound outdoors at a residential evening event has a short life anywhere in this city, and the later the hour, the shorter it gets. We are not going to quote you an ordinance; the rules vary by zone and they change. The planning fact is enough on its own: the loud part of a home party belongs early or indoors.

So the shape we will propose is the one that works: the patio at dinner volume while the light lasts, the floor inside after dark, the smallest honest size late. If your street, venue or HOA has a number in writing, give it to us at contract and we build the night under it, the same way we treat the published caps at the mountain venues.

Boulder runs earlier and tighter on all of this, and gets its own page.
