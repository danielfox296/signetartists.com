What it costs at home

Solo and duo, sized for the house.

These are the two sizes a Denver house actually books, with sound sized to the space and a named contact for the night inside both. Larger builds exist, and the venue paragraphs below say where they belong.

A solo carries cocktails and dinner for a table that stays a table. A duo carries the whole evening and has the range to lift the last hour of it. Both book for a single hour as readily as for a night, which matters when the music is covering one part of an evening rather than all of it.

What live music costs in this market, and what moves a number, is on the pricing page; the cost guide sets out the Colorado figures for each kind of night, house parties included.
