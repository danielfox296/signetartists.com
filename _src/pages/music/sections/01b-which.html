Where to start

Which configuration fits which kind of event.

Most people book by size first and by sound second, so this is the roster sorted the way they ask for it. Every act named here has a page of its own, and if you send us the date, the venue and the guest count we’ll come back with a recommendation.

A cocktail hour, a dinner, or background music
A solo guitarist, a singer and guitarist, a Spanish guitarist, a jazz duo or an acoustic duo, at 65 to 72 dBA at the nearest table, so your guests keep talking. A solo sets up in 6 by 4 feet on one circuit, and in December the Christmas jazz trio plays the season at the same volume.
A party with a dance floor
A trio or a quartet. Tejas Singh and Tony Medina both grow from a small build to a four piece on one contract, and the Americana band, the yacht rock band, the soul and R&B band and the funk band are each a floor with a different sound on it. Live band karaoke is the one where your people take the mic. A quartet takes 14 by 10 feet and two dedicated circuits.
A DJ, on its own or after the band
The DJ is open format, and it books alone or after any band on the roster through the same sound system, on the same contract.
A set piece your guests watch
Dirty Flamenco, Changes Jazz Trio with a saxophone added, René Moffatt playing his own songs, Your Bird Can Sing for a crowd that sings along, and Firstwater for a sound bath at an offsite or a wellness day.
A wedding
An acoustic or Spanish guitarist for the ceremony, a duo for the cocktail hour and the DJ for the floor, on one contract. The weddings page walks through all three, and the wedding jazz band covers the whole day in one style.
During the day
A jazz duo or a guitarist for senior living, a memorial or a Sunday brunch, the Beatles sing-along for a community afternoon, and a daytime sound bath. The daytime page has each of them.

Every act plays Denver, the Front Range and the mountain towns, and travel past the metro is quoted as its own line. What live music costs in this market is on the pricing page.
