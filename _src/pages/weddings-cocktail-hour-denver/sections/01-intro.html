The home market

Wedding cocktail hour music in Denver.

Denver is the home market, so a metro date carries no travel line: the figure on the rate card is the figure on the contract. A jazz duo plays the hour at any of the lobbies, galleries and garden terraces where this city holds its cocktail hours.

What should play, how long the hour runs and whether a duo or a DJ should hold it are answered on the cocktail hour page. This page is the Denver part: where the hour happens, and what load-in, parking and power look like once it does.
