What a mountain date costs

A resort date costs more than a metro date everywhere in this market, and the gap is made of three things: travel, the extra day the calendar loses, and lodging where the call runs late enough that driving I-70 afterwards is not a plan. All three are itemised on a quote of ours rather than folded into a fee.

Travel tiers

Front Range
Boulder, Fort Collins, and Colorado Springs including the Broadmoor. A day trip, so these carry a short travel line and no lodging. The Broadmoor is a resort property on Front Range travel, which makes it one of the better value venues in the state for live music.
I-70 corridor
Vail, Beaver Creek, Breckenridge and Winter Park. The corridor adds travel, and same-day return is realistic for an early call and unrealistic for a late one, so a late curfew usually brings a night's lodging with it.
Far resorts
Aspen, Telluride, Steamboat Springs and Crested Butte. Travel, plus a night's lodging billed at what it cost. These are two-day commitments for the band whatever the run of show says, and that is what makes them a different booking rather than a longer drive.

Lodging is billed at cost and it is on the quote as its own line, not folded into the fee. Nothing about a mountain booking should turn up on the invoice for the first time.
