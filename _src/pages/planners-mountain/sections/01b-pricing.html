What a mountain date costs

A resort date costs more than a metro date everywhere in this market. The gap is travel, the extra day the calendar loses, and lodging when the night runs late enough that driving I-70 home afterward isn't sensible. All three are itemized on our quote.

Travel tiers

Front Range
Boulder, Fort Collins, and Colorado Springs including the Broadmoor. A day trip, so these carry a short travel line and no lodging. The Broadmoor is a resort property on the Front Range travel tier, which makes it one of the better value venues in the state for live music.
I-70 corridor
Vail, Beaver Creek, Breckenridge and Winter Park. The corridor adds travel. Same-day return works for an early finish, and a late curfew usually brings a night's lodging with it.
Far resorts
Aspen, Telluride, Steamboat Springs and Crested Butte. These add travel, plus a night's lodging billed at what it cost. These are two-day commitments for the band whatever the run of show says.

Lodging is billed at cost, on the quote as its own line.
