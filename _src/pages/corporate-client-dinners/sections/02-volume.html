Music the table talks across.

A client dinner is a table of people who were invited to talk to each other, so the music is set to the conversation: quiet enough that nobody leans in, present enough that the evening sounds hosted.

A duo is built for that. Upright bass and guitar playing standards leave the middle of the sound open, so the conversation sits on top of the music, and two musicians can hold a dinner for hours at the same level. The music lifts a little between courses and stops when somebody stands up to speak, planned with your run of show.

A toast gets an announcement microphone, and the music stops while your speaker is still steps away from it. If the dinner wants a singer out front, Tejas Singh plays the same hours as a duo.
