Volume at a client dinner

Most of what goes wrong with live music at a dinner is a volume decision. A band playing at party level makes guests shout across a table they were invited to talk at, and the host spends the night apologising for it. So the dinner level we work to is 65 to 72 dBA at the nearest table, measured where your guests actually sit.

A duo is built for that number. Upright bass and guitar playing standards leave the middle of the sound open, so conversation sits on top of the music, and two musicians can hold a dinner for hours without drifting louder. The dinner feels polished and unhurried. Sets run 45 to 60 minutes with 15 to 20 between them, and music continues through every break, so the evening never goes silent.

Toasts and remarks are covered at every size: a toast gets an announcement microphone, and the music stops while your speaker is still steps away from it. If the dinner wants a singer out front instead of standards underneath, Tejas Singh leads our roster and plays the same hours as a duo.
