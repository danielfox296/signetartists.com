Built for a firm's paperwork

A client dinner is usually a firm's money, which means procurement, and procurement is where event bookings go slow. The path is written down here so your finance team can check it before anyone commits to anything.

Net-30 on approval
Approved corporate accounts hold the date on signature and are invoiced after the event, due 30 days later, with a purchase order number on the invoice if your finance team uses one.
One itemised invoice
Performance, sound and any travel on separate lines, in a form accounts payable accepts the first time. The W-9 comes with the contract rather than after a chase.
Certificate of insurance
{{gl_per_occurrence}} per occurrence and {{gl_aggregate}} aggregate, naming your venue as additional insured, in the same email as the contract. Clubs ask for this before they confirm a date, so it arrives before they ask.
