Client dinners

Dinner music you can talk across.

A client dinner works when the table forgets the music is there, and then remembers it on the way out. Upright bass and guitar in the corner, standards played beautifully and played low, and an evening that feels considered rather than catered.

This is the page for client dinners, member and association evenings, private clubs, firms and showrooms: the bookings where nobody dances and the evening still has to sound like somebody thought about it. The jazz duo leads here, and it plays an hour under a toast as readily as a whole evening under a table. It is one branch of our corporate work, with one contract and one named contact for the night.
