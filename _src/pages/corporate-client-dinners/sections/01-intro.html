Client dinners

Dinner music you can talk across.

A client dinner works when the table forgets the music is there, and then remembers it on the way out. It’s upright bass and guitar in the corner, standards played beautifully and played low, and an evening that feels considered.

Client dinners, member and association evenings, private clubs, firms and showrooms book it: the evenings where nobody dances and the evening still has to sound like somebody thought about it. Most start with the jazz duo, for an hour around a toast or a whole dinner, and it’s one branch of our corporate work.
