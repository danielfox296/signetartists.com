Client dinners

Dinner music you can talk across.

A client dinner works when the table forgets the music is there, and then remembers it on the way out. Upright bass and guitar in the corner, standards played beautifully and played low, and an evening that feels considered.

This is the page for client dinners, member and association evenings, private clubs, firms and showrooms: the bookings where nobody dances and the evening still has to sound like somebody thought about it. The jazz duo leads here, and it can play an hour around a toast or a whole evening of dinner. It’s one branch of our corporate work, with one contract and one personal contact for the night.
