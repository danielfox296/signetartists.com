Client dinners

Dinner music you can talk across.

At a client dinner the conversation is the event, so the music is built to sit under it: 65 to 72 dBA at the nearest table, a level people talk across without leaning in. The jazz duo is the lead booking for it, and it plays an hour under a toast as readily as a whole evening under a table.

This is the page for client dinners, member and association evenings, private clubs, firms and showrooms: the corporate bookings where nobody dances and the evening still has to sound deliberate. It is one branch of our corporate work, sized solo to quartet, with one contract and one named contact for the night.
