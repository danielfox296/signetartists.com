What you are buying

An evening that sounds like it was thought about.

Two players, no stage, no cables across the carpet. They arrive early, set up before your guests do, and play for as long as the evening wants them.

What that buys is a table that talks easily. The music holds under the conversation, lifts a little between courses, and stops dead when somebody stands up to speak. Nobody has to ask for any of it on the night, because it is set against your run of show the week before.

Guests notice it on the way out rather than during. That is the point of a dinner booking, and it is why the smallest act on the roster is often the right one.
