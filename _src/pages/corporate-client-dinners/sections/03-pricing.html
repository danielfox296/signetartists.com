What a dinner booking runs.

A jazz trio or acoustic duo for a dinner runs {{market:us_dinner_band}} in this market. The spread is mostly how long the music plays and how much production comes with it, and a dinner needs less production than a dance floor does.

What moves it for a client dinner: how many hours the music covers, whether the evening runs past a standard date into a holiday weekend, and how far outside the metro the venue sits. There is no overtime rate with us, so an extra half hour at the end costs what the earlier ones did.

Tell us the date and roughly how many are sitting down, and the quote comes back sized to the table rather than to a package. The wider Colorado market is set out in the cost guide, and how a quote is put together is on the pricing page.
