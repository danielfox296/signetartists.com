Why the smallest act works

What a dinner booking looks like.

Two musicians, with no stage and no cables across the carpet, playing for as long as the evening wants them.

What that buys is a table that talks easily. The music holds under the conversation, lifts a little between courses, and stops dead when somebody stands up to speak. Nobody has to ask for any of it on the night, because it’s planned with your run of show.

That’s the point of a dinner booking, and it’s why the smallest act on the roster is often the right one.
