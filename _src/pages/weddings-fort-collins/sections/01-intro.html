Fort Collins weddings

Live music for a Fort Collins wedding.

A Fort Collins wedding has a long horizon in it. The ceremony is on a ranch with the foothills behind it, beside the Poudre with the river running under the vows, or in a courtyard in Old Town; the cocktail hour is a patio with a breeze coming off the plains; and the floor fills a ballroom, a barn or a taproom’s event space. So the music is a guitarist with a small system that reaches the last row outdoors, a duo for the hour at a volume the conversation sits on top of, and a band or the DJ for the dancing, each one planned for the wind and the drive.

This is the Fort Collins version of our weddings page. Book one part of the day or all of it on one contract, with sound support that’s the right size for the space, a personal contact through the day, and one flat Front Range travel line for the hour up I-25.
