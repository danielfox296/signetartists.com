Fort Collins weddings

Live music for a Fort Collins wedding.

A Fort Collins wedding has a long horizon in it: the ceremony on a ranch with the foothills behind it, beside the Poudre with the river running under the vows, or in a courtyard in Old Town, the cocktail hour on a patio with a breeze off the plains, and the floor in a ballroom, a barn or a taproom’s event space. The music is a guitarist with a small system that reaches the last row outdoors, a duo for the hour, and a band or the DJ for the dancing.

It’s one of our weddings bookings: one part of the day or all of it, on one contract with a personal contact through the day and one flat Front Range travel line for the hour up I-25.
