Load-in and strike

Times below are from the vehicle arriving to the band being ready for sound check, assuming a ground-floor space with parking within a reasonable push. Add 30 minutes for an elevator, and add 30 more if the elevator is shared with catering during their load-in.

{{loadin_table}}
Doors and corridors
36 inches clear. The widest single item is the bass amp or a keyboard case; nothing needs a double door, but a 30-inch doorway with a turn in it will cost you 20 minutes.
Elevator
A car of about 4 by 6 feet takes everything in several trips. Tell us the window we have it in and we will plan inside it.
Sound check
30 minutes once the band is set, and it finishes 60 minutes before the first guest arrives. That gap is not padding. It is where the problems found at sound check get fixed.
Where the cases live
Out of sight of guests, and not in the kitchen path. Any back-of-house corner works. If there is nowhere, tell us at contract and the cases go back to the vehicles, which adds 20 minutes to the strike.
Strike
Begins after the last note, not before. We do not break down during the final set, and nothing gets carried through a space with guests still in it unless the venue requires it.
