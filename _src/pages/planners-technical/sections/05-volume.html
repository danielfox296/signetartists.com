Volume

Volume is a decision somebody makes in advance and writes into the run of show. Left undecided, it gets made on the night by whoever is standing nearest the desk.

Ceremony, cocktails and dinner, measured at the nearest table
65 to 72 dBA
Remarks, awards and anything on a microphone that is not us
Silent
Dancing, measured on the floor
88 to 95 dBA
Peaks during the last hour
100 dBA

If the venue enforces a cap, give us the number when you book. A band that knows at contract the ceiling is 95 dBA chooses arrangements and instrumentation that land inside it. A band told on the night just gets quieter.

Curfews are honoured as written. Where there's one, the last twenty minutes are planned so the night ends on the right song.
