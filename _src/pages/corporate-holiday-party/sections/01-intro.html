Holiday season 2026

Denver corporate holiday parties, November and December.

The company holiday party is the one night a year your people are all in the same place, off the clock, with a drink in their hand. Get it right and it is unforgettable, and you are the one who booked it. Get it wrong and everybody is in a coat by nine.

What gets it right is a band that reads the crowd on the night: conversation volume through dinner, silent through the remarks, and loud on cue the moment the program ends. One contract covers the music and the sound, and the paperwork arrives with it. The season is decided between August and October, and the December Fridays and Saturdays go first, so the date is the part worth settling early.
