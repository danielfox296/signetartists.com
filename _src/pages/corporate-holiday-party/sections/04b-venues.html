Downtown hotels

Denver’s December venues.

A Denver company party in December usually means a downtown hotel, and every company that waits until November is asking those calendars for the same two or three Saturdays, which is why the season is decided months before it starts.

The Denver page carries the downtown season in detail, and sixteen of the venues this market books are covered one by one in the Denver venue guide. Colorado Springs, an hour south, runs a calendar of its own, and a Springs date carries a Front Range travel line. Both sit under the corporate page, what the Colorado market charges for a December band is in the cost guide, and when December’s evening is a dinner more than a party, client dinners has a page of its own.
