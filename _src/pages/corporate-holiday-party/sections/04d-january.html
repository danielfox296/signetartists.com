Holding it in January

The January holiday party.

A growing share of Denver companies hold the party in January, and the venues have followed: several now write their holiday packages to run into the third week of January and past it. In January, the party has the calendar to itself.

Everything on this page works in January without a change of repertoire, because standards, covers and a dance set were never seasonal. A few things work even better in January, when the crowd isn’t spread across a season of invitations: the live band karaoke night, the yacht rock band, and the set piece from Dirty Flamenco.

The mountain version is the same evening with snow outside it. A January date at a Vail or Breckenridge venue is an après-ski party by default, and the retreats page carries the travel and lodging lines that go with it.

Ideas built for that month, themes included, are in January office party ideas.
