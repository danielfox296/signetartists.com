The season

Four things to know about a December party.

There are speeches, and they're the point. Somebody thanks the team, somebody announces the number, somebody hands out an award. The speeches get an announcement microphone at every size we bring, and the music stops while the speaker is still steps from it.

The venue is usually a restaurant or a hotel function space, not somewhere built for a band. That means a load-in question, a power question, and a limit on volume. We settle all three with the venue before the day.

Half the crowd doesn't want to dance and the other half is waiting. The night has to work at conversation volume through dinner and then get loud. That's what the duo-plus-DJ pairing is for: standards under the conversation, then the DJ turns the same sound system to the dance floor, and neither half is an afterthought.

Procurement is slower than you think. Vendor setup, a PO and a certificate of insurance are all available, and none of it is fast, so it helps to start early.

Footprints, power, run of show and booking details are on the planners pages.
