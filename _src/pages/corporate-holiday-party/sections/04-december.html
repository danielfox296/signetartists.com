The season

Three things to know about a December party.

There are speeches, and they’re the point. Somebody thanks the team, somebody announces the number, somebody hands out an award. The speaker gets an announcement microphone at every size, and the music stops while they’re still steps from it.

Half the crowd wants to talk and the other half is waiting to dance. That’s what the duo-plus-DJ pairing is for: standards under the conversation through dinner, then the DJ turns the same sound system to the dance floor, and both halves get their night.

The venue is usually a restaurant or a hotel ballroom, built for dinners more than bands, and we settle setup, power and the volume limit with it before the day.
