Occasions

Common occasions for Last Consulate.

The evenings where the music should feel like a late jazz set or a hotel lobby, and the talking is the point. Each has a page of its own.

Client dinners and member events
Twenty at one table or eighty at ten, with the music under the conversation for the whole night. It reads as a record playing in a very good hotel, and then somebody notices the bass line is being played.
Galas and award nights
The doors and the dinner, before the program. The galas page already lists it beside the jazz duo and the pianist for that slot, and it goes silent on the emcee’s cue like any act on the roster.
The welcome evening of a retreat
The first night, when the group has flown in and the point is to sit down and talk. One musician and one rig travel well over the passes, and corporate retreats covers the mountain venues and the travel line.
Dinner parties and cocktail parties at home
A table of twelve, or a cocktail party where nobody is going to dance. It fits in a corner on one outlet, and private parties covers what a home needs.

How the music sits around remarks and a program is planned with the run of show. What live music costs across Colorado, size by size, is set out in the cost guide.
