What a Vail date adds.

Two things, both on the quote as their own lines.

Travel, first. Vail is up I-70, a mountain highway with weather on it, and the drive is planned around the dock window. It’s a longer day than a Denver evening and it’s priced as one.

Then the calendar. A holiday weekend in Vail is the busiest thing the corridor does, and this market prices those dates above a standard one. A late curfew or a closing night that runs long can add a night’s lodging, which is on the quote before you sign.

What doesn’t change is the fee for the music itself: a duo through a welcome dinner in Vail is the same booking it is in Denver, playing the same set at the same volume. The wider Colorado market is set out in the cost guide, and how a quote is put together is on the pricing page.
