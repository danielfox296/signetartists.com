What a Vail date adds.

Two things, both named on the quote as their own lines rather than folded into a fee.

Travel, first. Vail is the I-70 corridor, which is a mountain highway with weather on it and a drive that has to be planned around the dock window rather than the set time. It is a longer day than a Denver evening and it is priced as one.

Then the calendar. A holiday weekend in Vail is the busiest thing the corridor does, and this market prices those dates above a standard one. A late curfew or a closing night that runs long can add a night's lodging, which we say at contract rather than after the event.

What does not change is the fee for the music itself: a duo through a welcome dinner in Vail is the same booking it is in Denver, playing the same set at the same volume. What the corridor adds, it adds visibly. The Colorado market these figures sit in is set out in the cost guide, and how a quote is put together is on the pricing page.
