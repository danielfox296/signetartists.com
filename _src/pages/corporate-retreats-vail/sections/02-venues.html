The venues

Where the Vail calendar happens.

Six venues carry most of the corporate calendar here, and each one books a different kind of evening.

The village hotels hold the classic retreat arc: a general session by day, an awards dinner at night. The Sonnenalp and the Four Seasons Resort Vail anchor that calendar in Vail Village, The Arrabelle at Vail Square and The Hythe carry it in Lionshead, and the Grand Hyatt Vail runs the same program at the west end of town, a step removed from the village core. Their ballrooms take a quartet without strain, and their private dining floors are built for a duo at dinner volume.

Donovan Pavilion is the town's own event venue on Gore Creek, and the one to hold when the group wants a single floor with its own vendors and its own run of show rather than a hotel package around it.

Where one act should carry the whole arc, Tejas Singh's duo grows to a quartet across the night on the same contract, so guests hear one band getting bigger. Where the program wants dinner music and nothing louder, a jazz duo holds the evening at a volume you can talk across.
