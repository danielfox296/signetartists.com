The builds

The trio for a reception, the quartet for a floor.

The trio
Voice and acoustic guitar, fiddle or mandolin, upright bass. Conversation volume for a welcome reception or a dinner, 65 to 72 dBA at the nearest table, in 12 by 8 feet on one dedicated 20-amp circuit with a 60-minute load-in. The build for the reception a group walks into on its first night.
The quartet
The trio plus drums. A dance floor act for a ranch dinner, a picnic that runs into the evening or the last night of a retreat, in 14 by 10 feet on two circuits with a 90-minute load-in. It still plays the dinner set first at talking level and turns when the plates are cleared.

Outdoors, and most of this act’s work is outdoors, it plays on a covered, level and dry performance area with power run to it, with the move-indoors call written before the day. Mountain venues often carry a decibel cap or a curfew, and the set is built under the number you give us at contract.

Altitude, load-in windows, lodging past the passes and what Colorado’s noise law does to a town sound permit are on the mountain corridor page. Footprints and power by size are on the technical page.
