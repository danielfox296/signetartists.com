Configurations

Two configurations, a trio and a quartet.

The trio
Voice and acoustic guitar, fiddle or mandolin, upright bass. It plays a welcome reception or a dinner at conversation volume, 65 to 72 dBA at the nearest table, and sets up in 12 by 8 feet on one dedicated 20-amp circuit with a 60-minute load-in. It’s the configuration for the reception a group walks into on its first night.
The quartet
The trio plus drums. It’s a dance floor act for a ranch dinner, a picnic that runs into the evening or the last night of a retreat, and it sets up in 14 by 10 feet on two circuits with a 90-minute load-in. It still plays the dinner set first at talking level and picks up the energy once the tables are cleared.

Outdoors, and most of this act’s work is outdoors, it plays on a covered, level and dry performance area with power run to it, and we agree a move-indoors plan with you ahead of time. Mountain venues often carry a decibel cap or a curfew, and we build the set under the number you give us.

Altitude, load-in, lodging past the passes and Colorado’s noise law are on the mountain corridor page. Footprints and power by size are on the technical page.
