Americana band

The Colorado evening, for a crowd that flew in for it.

Acoustic guitar, fiddle, upright bass and drums, playing the songs people expect to hear in the mountains and the songs written in them: the Grateful Dead and Tom Petty beside Nathaniel Rateliff and Gregory Alan Isakov. A welcome reception, a ranch dinner, a summer picnic, the last night of a retreat.

This is the act for the planner who has a group landing at DIA and one evening to make it feel like Colorado. It plays a conference welcome reception downtown, the dinner at a Vail or Beaver Creek resort, the company picnic in July, and a patio wedding, and it reads as the place rather than as a genre. The retreats page carries the mountain logistics that come with it.
