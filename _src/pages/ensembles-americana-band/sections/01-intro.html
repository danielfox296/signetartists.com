The Colorado songbook

An Americana band for welcome receptions, retreats and mountain evenings.

A fiddle takes the melody of Friend of the Devil, the upright bass walks underneath, and a group that flew in that morning is singing along on a ranch deck. This Americana band plays acoustic guitar, fiddle, upright bass and drums, and its songbook is the one people expect in the mountains and the one written in them: the Grateful Dead and Tom Petty beside Nathaniel Rateliff and Gregory Alan Isakov.

It books for a conference welcome reception downtown, a ranch or resort dinner in Vail or Beaver Creek, a company picnic in July and a patio wedding.
