Occasions

Who books an Americana band.

Conference welcome receptions
The first evening of a conference or an incentive trip, downtown or at a convention-center hotel, for a group that wants to hear where it has landed.
Mountain retreats and incentive groups
The ranch dinner, the resort deck or the last night in Vail, Beaver Creek, Aspen or Breckenridge. A date past the passes adds the drive and a night’s lodging to the quote, and the mountain planning page covers altitude and local sound rules.
Summer company picnics
A lawn, a park pavilion or a brewery patio in July, where the trio plays the afternoon and the quartet can carry it into the evening.
Patio and ranch weddings
A mountain cocktail hour or a ranch reception, for a couple who wants the music to sound like where they got married.
Birthdays and parties outdoors
A milestone birthday in a backyard or at a rented ranch, with a crowd that knows every word of Wildflowers.
