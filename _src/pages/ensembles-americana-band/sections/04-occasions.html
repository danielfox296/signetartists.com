Occasions

Common occasions for an Americana band.

Conference welcome receptions
The first evening of a conference or an incentive trip, downtown or at the convention-center hotels, when the group has just landed and the planner wants the city to introduce itself. The corporate page covers the corporate details.
Mountain retreats and incentive groups
The ranch dinner, the resort deck, the last night in Vail, Beaver Creek, Aspen or Breckenridge. The retreats page covers the mountain evening, the drive and the lodging.
Summer company picnics
A lawn, a park pavilion, a brewery patio in July. Company picnic entertainment ideas in Denver puts this band beside the other formats a summer party can take.
Patio weddings and private parties
A mountain wedding cocktail hour, a ranch reception, a milestone birthday outdoors. Weddings and private parties cover the configurations and the venues.
