Booking it

One reception, or the whole evening.

The trio books for a single reception hour or for arrival through dessert, and the quartet takes the same evening onto a dance floor, on one contract and one load-in.

Sound sized to the venue is included at every size, which on a ranch or a resort deck is the whole job: acoustic instruments outdoors disappear without it. A resort date past the passes carries the drive and a night’s lodging as their own lines, itemised, never folded into a total.

There is no overtime rate. Another half hour on the night costs what the earlier hours did.

What live music costs in this market is on the pricing page. Send the date, the venue and the headcount, and we will tell you whether the evening is a trio or a quartet.
