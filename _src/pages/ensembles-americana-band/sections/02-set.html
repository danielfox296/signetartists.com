The songbook

Friend of the Devil, Wildflowers, S.O.B., Big Black Car.

Two songbooks in one. The first is what everybody pictures for a mountain evening: the Grateful Dead, Tom Petty, Neil Young, Bob Dylan, The Band, the Allman Brothers, John Prine and the Avett Brothers. The second was written here, by Nathaniel Rateliff and the Night Sweats out of Denver, Gregory Alan Isakov out of Boulder, the Lumineers and The Head and the Heart in their Colorado years. The fiddle and mandolin carry the bluegrass standards between them.

A welcome reception gets the acoustic end of the book, quiet enough to talk over, and a ranch dance floor gets Petty and the Dead with the drums in. The rest of the covers list is on the repertoire page.
