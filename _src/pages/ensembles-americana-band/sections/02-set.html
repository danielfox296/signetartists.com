The songbook

A sample of the songbook.

Think Friend of the Devil, Wildflowers, S.O.B. and Big Black Car. It’s two songbooks in one. One is the songs everybody associates with a mountain evening: the Grateful Dead, Tom Petty, Neil Young, Bob Dylan, The Band, the Allman Brothers, John Prine, the Avett Brothers. The other is the songs written here: Nathaniel Rateliff and the Night Sweats out of Denver, Gregory Alan Isakov out of Boulder, the Lumineers, The Head and the Heart’s Colorado years. The fiddle and mandolin carry the bluegrass standards between them.

A set is built around the flow of your evening. A welcome reception leans acoustic and conversational; a ranch dance floor leans Petty and the Dead with the drums in. The band’s wider covers list is searchable on the repertoire page, and the first learned song is included in every booking.
