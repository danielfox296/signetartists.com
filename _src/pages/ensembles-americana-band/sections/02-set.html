The songbook

Friend of the Devil, Wildflowers, S.O.B., Big Black Car.

Two books in one. The songs everybody associates with a mountain evening: the Grateful Dead, Tom Petty, Neil Young, Bob Dylan, The Band, the Allman Brothers, John Prine, the Avett Brothers. And the songs written here: Nathaniel Rateliff and the Night Sweats out of Denver, Gregory Alan Isakov out of Boulder, the Lumineers, The Head and the Heart’s Colorado years. The fiddle and mandolin carry the bluegrass standards between them.

A set is built against the evening. A welcome reception leans acoustic and conversational; a ranch dance floor leans Petty and the Dead with the drums in. The band’s wider covers list is searchable on the repertoire page, and the first learned song beyond it is included in every booking.
