The programs

Markets, libraries, rec centers, and the summer series.

Farmers markets
A singer with a guitar, a solo guitarist or an acoustic duo, playing a market morning in sets with breaks, on a battery-powered system or none at all where the market has no power. The musician plays the folk, country and singer-songwriter end of the song list, the songs people know the words to, at a volume the stall next door can still sell over. Markets run a season, and a whole season of Saturdays can be booked with one enquiry.
Libraries
An adult program in the meeting space, a concert series in the evening or on the lawn in summer, an older adults’ afternoon with the standards, a Sunday afternoon jazz duo in the atrium, or a sound bath for a wellness program with the lights down. A library is a building built for quiet, so the sizes are solo and duo at 65 to 72 dBA at the nearest seat, and a guitarist plays a meeting space without amplification at all. For a songwriter’s afternoon, René Moffatt is a featured artist of the Denver Public Library’s own Volume Denver program and plays a library solo. For an all-ages afternoon, Your Bird Can Sing is a duo that hands out booklets with the words in them and plays the Beatles catalogue, which is the songbook a hall of strangers between six and ninety already shares.
Rec centers and community centers
A community evening, a holiday open house, a summer concert in the park behind the building, an older adults’ lunch program with a guitarist, a family day with a singer. The Americana band, acoustic guitar, fiddle, upright bass and drums, plays the outdoor evening at trio or quartet size, and a solo or a duo plays everything indoors. Cities and districts book through a purchase order and want a certificate of insurance on file, and the certificate and the W-9 come with the contract.
Summer concert series
A city’s or a district’s summer series is booked months ahead, often through a call for performers, and the acts on this roster that play a park stage with a real sound system are the Americana band, Tejas Singh, Tony Medina, René Moffatt with his band, and the yacht rock band. Send the call and we’ll answer it with the act that fits the stage.
