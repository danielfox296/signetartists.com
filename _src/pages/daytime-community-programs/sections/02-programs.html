The programs

Markets, libraries, rec centers, and the summer series.

Farmers markets
A singer with a guitar, a solo guitarist or an acoustic duo, playing a market morning in sets, on a battery-powered system or none at all where the market has no power. The musician plays the folk, country and singer-songwriter songs people know the words to, at a volume the stall next door can still sell over, and a whole season of Saturdays can be booked with one enquiry.
Libraries
An adult program in the meeting space, a concert series on the lawn in summer, an older adults’ afternoon with the standards, a Sunday jazz duo in the atrium, or a sound bath for a wellness program with the lights down. A library is built for quiet, so the sizes are solo and duo, and a guitarist plays a meeting space without amplification at all. René Moffatt is a featured artist of the Denver Public Library’s own Volume Denver program, and Your Bird Can Sing plays the Beatles for an all-ages afternoon, the songbook a hall of strangers between six and ninety already shares.
Rec centers and community centers
A community evening, a holiday open house, a summer concert in the park behind the building, an older adults’ lunch with a guitarist, a family day with a singer. The Americana band plays the outdoor evening at trio or quartet size, and a solo or a duo plays everything indoors.
Summer concert series
A city’s or a district’s summer series is booked months ahead, often through a call for performers. The acts that play a park stage with a real sound system are the Americana band, Tejas Singh, Tony Medina, René Moffatt with his band, and the yacht rock band. Send the call and we’ll answer it with the act that fits the stage.
