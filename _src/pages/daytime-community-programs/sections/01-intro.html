Community programs

Musicians for farmers markets, library programs and rec centers.

Saturday morning at the farmers market: a singer with a guitar between the peach stand and the bakery, playing Take It Easy loud enough to hear from the next stall. Markets, libraries, rec centers and summer series book the same roster as everyone else, from one singer on a battery-powered system to the Americana band on a park stage, on one contract with sound included and one personal contact.

It’s the community side of our daytime bookings, with the singer and guitarist, the acoustic duo, the Americana band, the Spanish guitarist, the jazz duo and Firstwater’s sound bath.
