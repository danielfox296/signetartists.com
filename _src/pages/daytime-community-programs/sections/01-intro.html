Community programs

Musicians for farmers markets, library programs and rec centers.

A Saturday farmers market wants a singer with a guitar on a battery-powered system between the stalls. A library wants a duo for an adult program, a summer concert on the lawn, or a sound bath in the meeting space. A rec center wants an Americana band for a community evening or a guitarist for the older adults’ lunch. These are public programs, booked by a coordinator with a season to fill, a purchase order to raise and a certificate of insurance to file, and we book them the way we book everything else: one contract, sound included, one personal contact.

This is the community side of our daytime bookings. The acts are the ones on the rest of the roster: the singer and guitarist, the acoustic duo, the Americana band, the Spanish guitarist, the jazz duo and Firstwater’s sound bath.
