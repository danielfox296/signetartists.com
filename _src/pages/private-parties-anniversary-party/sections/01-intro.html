Anniversary party

Live music for your anniversary party.

An anniversary party is a dinner with one song in it that matters more than the rest of the night. The kids threw it, the friends from the wedding flew in, somebody stands up with a glass, and at some point the song the two of them danced to at their wedding gets played again with everyone watching. The music around that moment is a jazz duo or a singer with a guitar at a volume the table talks across, the song itself learned for the night, and a band on the floor afterward if the party is the kind that dances, on one contract with one personal contact in charge of the music.

An anniversary party is part of our private parties bookings. It comes with sound support that’s the right size for a house, a club or a private dining space, a certificate of insurance and a W-9 with the quote, and an announcement microphone for the toasts. The milestone birthday section on the private parties page covers the birthday version of the same night.
