The party

The surprise, the dinner, and the vow renewal.

The surprise party the children throw
The couple thinks it’s dinner with the kids, the restaurant’s private dining space is full of everyone they’ve known for forty years, and the musician was already there playing at conversation volume, so the first thing they hear as the door opens is their song. The proposals and surprise songs page covers how a cue and a learned song work, and the same mechanics run a surprise anniversary.
The dinner at home or at the club
A long table, the toasts, the slideshow somebody made, and the duo playing the standards underneath all of it at a volume the table talks across, with the announcement microphone live for whoever stands up. The Denver private parties page covers the club dinners this city books and what a house has to provide.
The vow renewal
Some 25ths and 50ths renew the vows in the garden before the dinner, and that half-hour books ceremony music: a guitarist or a duo for the walk in, the song the two of them chose, and the walk out. The wedding ceremony page covers how the music is placed, and the dinner afterward goes on the same contract.
