The party

The surprise, the dinner, and the vow renewal.

The surprise party the children throw
The couple thinks it’s dinner with the kids, the private dining space is full of everyone they’ve known for forty years, and the musician is already playing, so the first thing they hear as the door opens is their song. It runs on the same cue as a surprise proposal song.
The dinner at home or at the club
A long table, the toasts, the slideshow somebody made, and the duo playing the standards under all of it, at home or at a Denver club.
The vow renewal
Some 25ths and 50ths renew the vows in the garden before the dinner: a guitarist or a duo for the walk in, the song the two of them chose, and the walk out, placed the way wedding ceremony music is, with the dinner afterward on the same contract.
