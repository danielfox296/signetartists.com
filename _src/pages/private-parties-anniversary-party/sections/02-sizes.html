Sizes

A duo for the dinner, a band if it dances.

Most anniversaries are a seated dinner with a toast in it, so most start with the duo, and the rest follows from whether the night ends on a floor.

The jazz duo
Upright bass and guitar playing the standards at a volume the table talks across, for a dinner at home, at a club or in a private dining space. The jazz duo builds the set from the years the two of them have been married.
A singer with a guitar
One voice and a guitar for the anniversary where the couple’s song should be sung. Tejas Singh learns it for the night, and the same booking grows to a quartet if the party turns into a floor.
A Spanish guitarist for a long dinner
A guitar with real technique in it under a long table, for the 50th that’s a dinner for the whole family. Dirty Flamenco plays it as one guitarist or as a trio.
The band, for the 25th that dances
A trio or a quartet for the anniversary whose friends are still the people who danced at the wedding. Tony Medina plays it up to a four piece, the same band plays the dinner first, and the DJ can carry the late end through the same sound system.
