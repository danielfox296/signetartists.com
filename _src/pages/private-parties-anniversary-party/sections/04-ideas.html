Ideas

Anniversary party entertainment ideas.

The ideas that fit a dinner with one important song in it, each with the page that covers it.

A sing-along the grandchildren can join
A fortieth or fiftieth anniversary usually has four generations in it, and the Beatles catalogue is the one songbook all four already know. Your Bird Can Sing is a duo that plays it and hands out booklets with the words in them, so the grandchildren sing along instead of standing at the edge with their phones.
Their first-dance song, played again
The song from the wedding, learned for the night and played while the two of them dance in front of the people who watched the first time. The first learned song is included in every booking, and the proposals and surprise songs page covers how one song gets placed.
A set built from their years
The standards and the songs from the decade they met, threaded through the dinner so the evening sounds like their life rather than a playlist. The repertoire page is searchable by title.
A jazz duo under the toasts
Upright bass and guitar at a volume the table talks across, silent for the toasts and back in the moment the glass goes down. The jazz duo and trio page covers it.
A floor for the 25th
A trio or a quartet playing the songs the wedding party danced to, for the anniversary whose guests are still up for it. Tony Medina plays it up to a four piece.
A Spanish guitarist for the 50th
A guitar with real technique in it under a long family dinner. Dirty Flamenco plays it as one guitarist.
Live band karaoke for the wedding party
The band plays the hits and hands the microphone to the best man, thirty years later. The live band karaoke page covers the size it needs.
