Ideas

Anniversary party entertainment ideas.

A sing-along the grandchildren can join
A fortieth or fiftieth usually has four generations in it, and the Beatles are the one songbook all four know. Your Bird Can Sing is a duo that plays it and gets the whole family singing.
Their first-dance song, played again
The song from the wedding, played while the two of them dance in front of the people who watched the first time.
A set built from their years
The standards and the songs from the decade they met, threaded through the dinner so the evening sounds like their life. The repertoire page is searchable by title.
Live band karaoke for the wedding party
The band plays the hits and hands the microphone to the best man, thirty years later, with live band karaoke.
