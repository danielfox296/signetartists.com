The set

The songs people sing along to under their breath.

The singer-songwriter book, pop and soul from the last sixty years, and the acoustic side of classic rock: Tom Petty, Bill Withers, the Beatles ballads. The voice sings the song and the guitar carries the rest of the record.

The ballads play while people eat, and the songs with a pulse come out once people are standing. The whole covers list is on the repertoire page, and one learned song of your choosing comes with every booking, which for a wedding is usually the first dance or the processional.
