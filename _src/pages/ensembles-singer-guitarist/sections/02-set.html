The set

The songs people sing along to under their breath.

The set is the singer-songwriter book, pop and soul from the last sixty years, and the acoustic side of classic rock. Think Tom Petty, Bill Withers, the Beatles ballads, the songs a wedding guest and a company picnic both know. It’s sung out front, with the guitar carrying the rest of the record on its own.

One musician can only get so loud, and that’s the point. A cocktail set runs at 65 to 72 dBA at the nearest table, the level two guests who’ve just met can talk across, and a voice and a guitar can’t drift above it by accident. Sets run 45 to 60 minutes with music through the breaks, and a set is built around the flow of your evening: ballads through dinner, the songs with a pulse in them once people are standing.

The whole covers list is searchable on the repertoire page, and the first learned song you ask for is included in every booking, which for a wedding is usually the first dance or the processional.
