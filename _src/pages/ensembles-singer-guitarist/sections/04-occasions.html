Occasions

Common occasions for a singer and guitarist.

Wedding cocktail hours and welcome drinks
The hour after the ceremony, or the night before it. One voice sings the songs the two families both know, and the same musician can play the ceremony first, which the ceremony page covers.
Dinner parties, patios and milestone birthdays
A table of twelve at home, a backyard in July, a sixtieth that wants the songs of one particular decade sung well. The private parties page covers the configurations by size.
Company picnics and client appreciation evenings
The one-musician booking for a lawn or a rooftop, and the size that lets a client evening feel hosted without feeling produced. The corporate page covers the corporate details.
Retreat evenings in the mountains
A deck or a fire after a day of sessions, with the acoustic end of the book. The retreats page covers the resort towns and their logistics.
Farmers markets and daytime programs
One voice and a guitar on a battery-powered system at a Saturday market, or the songs of the sixties and seventies sung in a senior community’s common area on a weekday afternoon, which the senior living page covers.

Sourced market figures for what one musician costs for a cocktail hour are in the cost guide.
