Occasions

Who books a singer and guitarist.

Wedding cocktail hours and welcome drinks
The hour after the ceremony, or the night before it, with one voice singing the songs both families know. The same musician can play the ceremony first.
Dinner parties, patios and milestone birthdays
A table of twelve at home, a backyard in July, a sixtieth that wants the songs of one particular decade sung well.
Company picnics and client appreciation evenings
A lawn or a rooftop for a company, or a client evening that should feel hosted.
Retreat evenings in the mountains
A deck or a fire after a day of sessions, with the acoustic end of the book.
Farmers markets and daytime programs
One voice and a guitar on a battery-powered system at a Saturday market or a library’s summer series.
Senior living
The songs of the sixties and seventies, sung in a community’s common area on a weekday afternoon.
