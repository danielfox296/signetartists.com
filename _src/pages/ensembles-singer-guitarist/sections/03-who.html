The singer and guitarist
One of the working singers who play this size, matched to your date and your crowd. Book it when the sound and the date matter more than the name.
Tony Medina
A smoky voice and a guitar played with a funk guitarist’s right hand. He plays blues and classic rock, solo for welcome drinks and dinner and up to a four piece when the night wants a floor, with his own footage on his page.
René Moffatt
A songwriter with his own catalog, folk and Americana on voice and acoustic guitar, who plays covers beside his own songs when the hour wants them. Solo for a ceremony, a cocktail hour or a dinner where people listen, and up to a four piece with his band, with his own footage on his page.
Tejas Singh
A singer-songwriter who builds familiar songs from the ground up with live looping, fronting a duo that grows to a quartet on one contract. It’s the booking when the hour should be one voice and the night should end on a dance floor.
