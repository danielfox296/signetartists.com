The set

Singer-songwriter and folk-rock covers people know.

The set is singer-songwriter and folk-rock covers, songs guests know the words to. That’s the difference between this page and the jazz duo, which plays standards. The footprint, volume and price are the same, so the choice comes down to what the crowd should hear.

A set is built around the flow of your evening. The full covers list is published and searchable on the repertoire page, and the first learned song is included in every booking.
