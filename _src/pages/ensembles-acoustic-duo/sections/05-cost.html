Booking it

One hour, or the whole front of the night.

A single hour is a real booking here, not a minimum to work around. So is arrival through dessert, with the same two players and one load-in.

Sound sized to the venue is included at every size, which for a duo matters more than it sounds: it is the difference between two people filling a garden and two people being drowned out by it.

There is no overtime rate. If the evening runs long and you want another half hour, it costs what the earlier hours did and you can decide it at the time.

What live music costs in this market is on the pricing page. Tell us how long the music has to cover and where, and we will tell you whether this is a duo night or a trio one.
