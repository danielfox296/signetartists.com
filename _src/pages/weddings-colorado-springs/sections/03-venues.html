The venues

Where the Springs gets married, and what each one asks of the music.

The resort weddings, at Garden of the Gods Resort and Club and Cheyenne Mountain Resort, put the ceremony outdoors with the view behind it and the reception indoors, which is the easiest version of the day for live players: an outdoor set from a covered spot, then an indoor floor with power on the wall, and the second act loading in while the first is still playing. The Broadmoor books at its own scale and on its own calendar, and a wedding there is planned with its events office line by line.

Downtown, The Mining Exchange and The Antlers take the weddings that want a hotel around them: ceremony and reception under one roof, guests walking rather than driving, and a floor that runs late under a downtown curfew. A duo fits a hotel's smaller function spaces; the band and the DJ want the ballroom.

Manitou Springs, Monument and the ranches out toward Larkspur are the same drive and the same travel line. What changes there is cover: a barn or a garden site needs a level, dry, covered performance area with power run to it, agreed in writing before the day.
