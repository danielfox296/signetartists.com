The light and the wind

Sun, wind and the red rocks.

An outdoor ceremony in Colorado Springs is planned around two things the venue can’t control. The first is the light: the sun drops behind the Front Range early, the color on the rocks goes with it, and a ceremony timed for that light has a fixed start the music is built around. The second is the wind, which comes off the range in the afternoon and settles as the sun does. A guitarist’s system is small and low to the ground for that reason, and the ceremony spot is chosen with the venue for cover as much as for the view.
