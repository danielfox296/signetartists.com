The pairing

A duo through dinner, a DJ to close.

A Denver holiday party has two halves: dinner, which has to work at conversation volume, and a dance floor that has to fill. The pairing that covers both is a duo through cocktails and dinner with a DJ carrying the back half, through one sound system on one contract.

Still at the ideas stage? Seven holiday party ideas, from the DJ night to live band karaoke, are written up on the blog.

When one act should carry the whole night instead, Tejas Singh starts as a duo and finishes as a quartet on a single contract, with the same voice out front all night. Either way it’s one sound system and one invoice, and the handoff is planned so the music runs from the first drink to the last song.
