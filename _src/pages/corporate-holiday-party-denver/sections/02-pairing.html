When one act should carry the whole night instead, Tejas Singh ladders inside a single contract: starts as a duo, finishes as a quartet, the same voice out front. Either way the night is one invoice, with sound included at every size.

Every size and both columns are on the rate card. Holiday and peak weekends carry a published 25 percent line and New Year's Eve 50 percent, and the cost guide walks the arithmetic around every figure. Still at the ideas stage? Seven party ideas, priced, from the DJ night to live-band karaoke.
