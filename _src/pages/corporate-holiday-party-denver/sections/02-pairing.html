Built for the season

A duo through dinner, a DJ to close.

A Denver holiday party has two halves: two hours that must work at conversation volume, and a floor that has to turn. The pairing that covers both is a duo through cocktails and dinner with a DJ carrying the back half, through one sound system on one contract.

When one act should carry the whole night instead, Tejas Singh ladders inside a single contract: starts as a duo, finishes as a quartet, the same voice out front. Either way the night is one load-in and one invoice, with sound included at every size.

Whichever way you go, the handoff is the part guests feel. No gap, no awkward five minutes while somebody finds a cable, no moment where the energy drops and people start looking at their coats. The music runs from the first drink to the last song.
