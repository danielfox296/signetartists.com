Denver holiday parties

A Denver corporate holiday party has 21 dates to land on.

The program ends, the duo that played dinner hands the floor to the DJ without a gap, and a ballroom off 17th Street fills up. Thursdays, Fridays and Saturdays across November and December 2026, minus Thanksgiving and Christmas, come to 21 party dates, ten of them in December, and the whole market wants the same peak weekends, marked below.

The season is decided August through October, and New Year’s Eve books earliest of all. Downtown it’s tighter still: the companies filling the December calendars at the Brown Palace, the Four Seasons and the Oxford are choosing from the same short list of Saturdays.
